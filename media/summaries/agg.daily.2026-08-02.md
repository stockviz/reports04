### ✅ **Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) Re-Entry**
- After a **3–3.5 lakh crore INR outflow** due to:
  - High domestic valuations
  - Better global returns
  - Lagging AI development
  - Geopolitical tensions
  - Oil-price-driven forex stress

- **Future inflows depend on**: Geopolitical stability (especially Iran), oil prices, and domestic policy clarity.

### ✅ **Portfolio Diversification Strategy (Vandana’s Insight)**
- **True diversification** across:
  - **Equity** (multi-cap blend: large-cap stability + mid/small-cap growth)
  - **Debt** (steady returns, acts as stabilizer during equity downturns)
  - **Gold** (historically negative correlation with equities/debt, though recent macro events have altered this)
- **Key benefits**:
  - Reduces portfolio volatility
  - Delivers returns comparable to pure equity over the long term
  - Encourages discipline, mitigates drawdowns, ensures smoother performance
- **Rule-based, balanced approach** recommended for FY27.

## 🏭 **2. Corporate & Sectoral Updates**

### 🔹 **Tata Steel UK – Green Steel Transformation**
- **Project**: £1.25bn electric arc furnace (EAF) at Port Talbot (3.2 Mt/year capacity).
- **Timeline**:
  - Target completion: **2028**
  - Electricity supply via National Grid: **2029**
- **Government support**: £500m
- **Environmental impact**:
  - **90% reduction** in site-level CO₂ emissions (~5 Mt/year)
  - Replaces non-operational blast furnace
- **Progress**:
  - Major demolition complete
  - Equipment fabrication ongoing
  - Transmission lines to be completed by **end of 2027**
  - Trial runs possible before full power connection

### 🔹 **Raymond Lifestyle (Q1 FY27)**

- **Key drivers**:

### 🔹 **NTT Data Payment Services India**

- **Background**:
  - Entered India in 2019 via **Atom Technologies acquisition**
  - Currently focused on **back-end infrastructure** for NPCI and others
- **Challenges**:
  - Low UPI merchant discount rates limit revenue
- **Growth Plan**:
  - Organic investment in infrastructure
  - Seek partnerships/acquisitions for lending licenses
- **Platform**: **Adaptis** (used in SEA) for:
  - Merchant payments
  - Cash-flow-based credit
  - Cross-border UPI-linked transactions
- **Advantages**:
  - Japanese banking relationships (e.g., SMBC)
  - Long-term, non-VC-backed strategy
- **Bullish on India** due to high transaction volume, digitization, and strategic AI tech importance
