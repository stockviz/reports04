Agastya Green Energy Ltd, under the Anubhav Agarwal Group, initiated a ₹7,800 crore expansion project to establish 12 GW of integrated ingot and wafer manufacturing capacity at Orvakal Industrial Area, Kurnool, enhancing domestic solar PV value chain integration and supporting India’s energy transition goals. The project is expected to generate over 3,500 jobs.

In July, UPI transaction volume reached 23.66 billion, valued at ₹29.88 lakh crore. PhonePe processed 10.86 billion transactions (45.89% volume, 48.33% value), and Google Pay handled 7.65 billion (32.33% volume, 33.51% value), collectively accounting for 78.22% of transaction count and 81.83% of total value. Paytm recorded 1.90 billion transactions (8.05% volume, 6.85% value), while Navi, Flipkart’s super.money, BHIM, FamApp, and WhatsApp followed. CRED maintained a significant value share (1.98%) despite lower volume.

Reliance Industries and Rolls-Royce entered a strategic partnership to develop a sovereign combat engine for India’s advanced fighter jet programme, with plans to establish a dedicated aerospace gas-turbine manufacturing complex. The collaboration supports the government’s objective of self-reliance in defence manufacturing, facilitating the transition from the single-engine Tejas to twin-engine fighters and reducing dependence on Russian-supplied systems. Specific investment and joint venture structures were not disclosed.

Belrise Industries, following the completion of a ₹1,700 crore QIP, shifted focus from debt reduction to growth, deploying capital toward acquisitions—specifically Hyva India’s tipper-body unit—and capacity expansion. In Q1 FY27, PAT increased 8.9% to ₹121.7 crore, manufacturing revenue rose 20%, and total revenue grew 12.6%. New orders in chassis and electric vehicle localization, along with entry into solar-tracker manufacturing, expanded the company’s market reach. With 71% of manufacturing revenue derived from powertrain-neutral components, mid-teens revenue growth is projected for FY27, alongside stable EBITDA margins and disciplined capital expenditure.

Mahindra & Mahindra, under the leadership of Anish Shah, is refocusing on the niche pickup-truck segment after divesting loss-making overseas operations. The company is targeting growth in South Africa, Australia, and New Zealand, while testing the lifestyle-oriented Scorpio Lifestyler in India. The domestic pickup market, currently at 4,000–5,000 units annually, is targeted for expansion to approximately 10,000 units per year. The Scorpio Lifestyler, priced under ₹19.79 lakh ex-showroom, will be launched in April 2027 in three editions (Valley, Reef, Trail), with a focus on RHD markets. Aggressive pricing, under ₹19.75 lakh, is planned for 2027 to compete with higher-priced rivals. Local assembly may be considered if unit economics and regulatory conditions are favorable. Growth will be driven by internal product development rather than acquisitions.

Amazon reinstated a dispute resolution clause in its user agreement requiring arbitration and prohibiting class-action lawsuits, while preserving access to small-claims courts. The change, effective immediately, follows the 2021 removal amid privacy concerns and comes amid multiple ongoing class-action lawsuits related to product safety and Prime subscription cancellations. The clause, though not automatically binding in court, is intended to provide legal protection and expedite dispute resolution. The announcement coincided with Q2 2026 earnings, which reported $200.6 billion in sales (20% YoY growth), a 43% increase in operating income to $27.5 billion, and strong performance in AWS, AI, advertising, and delivery speed.

The Scorpio Lifestyler, developed at Mahindra’s Indian design and research centers, will be launched in India by April 2027, with three variants targeting mid-size lifestyle pickup demand in RHD markets. The vehicle is built on a new body-on-frame platform and emphasizes capability, technology, safety, and comfort. Simultaneously, Mahindra introduced the BE 6-SPORTEQ electric SUV series, including launch and Formula E Freedom editions, with deliveries commencing on 26 August. Expansion strategies include strengthening dealer networks, leveraging existing strong markets, and pursuing selective growth in high-potential overseas regions.

BMW Industries reported a 25.76% year-on-year increase in consolidated net profit to ₹19.04 crore for the Apr-Jun quarter, up from ₹15.14 crore, driven by a 15% revenue rise to ₹176.68 crore. EBITDA margin improved by 147 basis points to 25.1%, with EBITDA growing 22.2%. The greenfield downstream steel complex at Bokaro, part of an ₹800 crore expansion under the PLI scheme, is progressing and expected to begin revenue generation in Q2 FY27. Total income for FY2025-26 rose to ₹680.02 crore from ₹638.6 crore. A partnership with Indian Oil Corp was established for piped natural gas supply to the Bokaro plant. The company continues to focus on downstream steel value-addition products, including coils, sheets, pipes, and TMT rebars.

The Tata Sons annual general meeting scheduled for August 18 faces potential adjournment due to a governance impasse between the Sir Ratan Tata Trust (SRTT) and the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust (SDTT). The SRTT’s inability to participate stems from a regulatory restriction preventing it from holding shares in Tata Sons, following a Supreme Court directive. This has led to a deadlock in shareholder representation. The situation underscores the complexity of family trust governance in large Indian conglomerates.
