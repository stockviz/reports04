### 🚀 **Technology & AI: Momentum, Challenges, and Strategic Shifts**

#### **AI Infrastructure & Data Centers**
- **MTAR Tech** is highlighted as a pivotal player in AI/data-center infrastructure, with recent capacity expansion and a now-reasonable valuation post-correction. Deepan Mehta sees strong long-term potential.
- **Pronto**, a Bengaluru-based home services startup, has expanded its **"Verified"** service using head-mounted cameras to collect anonymized video data for training robotics AI. 
  - **700+ verified bookings/day** (up from 15), with workers earning 2–3x more.
  - Data processed on GPUs without human review; in-house storage.
  - Projected to generate **~90% of revenue** from physical-AI data segment within 5 years at **~50% gross margin**.
  - Positioned against rivals like Urban Company that avoid in-home recordings.

#### **Semiconductors & Supply Chain**
- **Qualcomm** forecasts Q4 earnings of **$2.05–$2.25/share**, below expectations, citing:
  - Rising supply-chain costs.
  - Tighter Apple supply constraints → **price hike starting Sept 1**.
  - Shift toward **data-center chips**; smartphone segment under pressure.
  - Chinese handset revenue has bottomed, but **pricing and inventory issues hurt margins**.
- **TSMC remains stable**, but memory stocks have corrected.

#### **AI ROI & Open-Source Risks**
- Rahul Chadha warns that **AI earnings may weaken** over the next few quarters due to:
  - Price and manpower cuts in IT.
  - Commoditization of open-source AI models.
- Market focus shifting from hype to **real AI ROI**.

### 💼 **Private Equity & M&A Activity**

#### **Pharma & Healthcare**
- **ChrysCapital** acquired **70.68% stake in Novartis India Ltd** for **₹1,446 crore**, marking its **first majority-controlled pharma investment**.
  - Separates NIL from Novartis AG; parent continues operations via **Novartis Healthcare Pvt. Ltd**.
  - New leadership: **Dr. Vikas Gupta (CEO & MD)**, 3 independent directors appointed.
  - Goal: Build a **leading branded-generics platform** in India using legacy brands (Voveran, Calcium Sandoz, Tegrital) in pain management, gynecology, neurosciences, etc.
  - Advisors: Kotak, EY, Alvarez & Marsal, Dhruva, PwC.

#### **Global Healthcare Expansion**
- **Cupid** increased stake in **GII Healthcare Investment** ($3.5B asset manager) across **Saudi Arabia, UAE, Europe, US, India**.
  - Deepens partnership with GII (owning Abeer Medical, Al-Meswak Dental).
  - Reflects confidence in **GCC’s growing healthcare market** and aligns with Cupid’s strategy in **preventive health and wellness**.
