India’s Commercial Insurance Rates Plummet Amid Rising Competition and Capacity

In the first nine months of 2026, commercial insurance rates in India—covering property, financial/professional liability, and cyber insurance—declined due to increased insurer capacity and heightened competition, per Marsh’s Global Insurance Market Index. India saw some of the sharpest rate reductions in the India, Middle East, and Africa (IMEA) region, with property insurance rates dropping for the fourth consecutive quarter (regional average: 10%, with some clients securing 45% cuts in Q1). The removal of tariffs and expanded insurer capacity intensified rivalry, empowering businesses to negotiate better premiums, coverage limits, and policy terms.

Financial and professional liability rates also fell, with directors and officers (D&O) liability rates down by up to 20% and professional indemnity rates declining. Meanwhile, cyber insurance rates recorded their 12th straight quarter of declines, with India experiencing the steepest reductions in IMEA (Q3 regional average: 10%).

Despite softer pricing, Marsh warned that cyber risks are evolving with digital dependence and emerging tech. The report urged businesses to leverage current market conditions to review coverage, strengthen protections, and prepare for potential future rate hikes amid geopolitical risks, supply-chain disruptions, and natural catastrophes
