Gifting Mutual Fund Units: Rules, Tax Implications, and Practical Considerations

TV18BRDCST ANANDRATHI

The segment explains how investors can transfer mutual fund units as gifts, especially for Diwali. Recipients must have a valid KYC and a zero‑balance folio with the same AMC; a DMAT is not required. Gifts to relatives (spouse, parents, siblings, children) incur no tax at the time of transfer, and the recipient’s cost base for capital gains remains the original purchase price of the donor. Gifts to non‑relatives (friends) are taxable if the value exceeds ₹50,000, with the entire amount considered a gift under income‑tax rules. Lock‑in schemes such as ELSS and certain child‑specific plans may restrict transfers. Gifting to minors is currently complex due to guardian‑account requirements. Choosing a fund for the recipient should align with their time horizon, risk appetite, and financial goals, often requiring rebalancing for different life stages. Key pitfalls include ensuring KYC compliance for both parties, confirming folio details, and educating new investors on portfolio management. The process is a “non‑financial transaction” for tax purposes when involving relatives, but tax treatment changes for friends beyond the ₹50,000 threshold
