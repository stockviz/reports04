Trent partners with Caffe Nero to open coffee shops in Westside and Zudio stores after talks with Starbucks fall apart

TRENT

Tata retailer Trent has signed a licensing deal with UK coffee chain Caffe Nero to launch cafés inside its Westside and Zudio outlets, following a breakdown in negotiations with Tata‑Starbucks over store format and location control. The move reflects a broader trend of retailers adding cafés to drive footfall and dwell time, offering a specialist coffee brand without building its own café business. Details on the number of cafés, rollout timeline, and commercial terms remain undisclosed

Digital Gold Purchases Surge 83% in September

MMTC AUGMONT

Digital gold buying in India jumped 83% YoY to Rs 2,590 crore in September, with volumes hitting 1.7 tonnes—18% above the long‑term monthly average. Year‑to‑date purchases rose 76% to 16.3 tonnes, driven by low entry barriers, app‑based convenience, and a surge in small‑ticket, UPI‑led orders, especially among younger, first‑time buyers. While the global digital gold market is valued at $45‑55 billion (under 1% of physical gold), the World Gold Council projects a 27% CAGR to $160‑170 billion by 2031, potentially reaching $700 billion if trust and regulatory issues are resolved. Regulators warn that digital gold isn’t covered by securities or commodity rules, prompting industry moves toward a self‑regulatory framework for custody, transparency, and consumer protection. Companies like MMTC‑PAMP and Augmont are launching festive campaigns and systematic investment plans to capitalize on the trend, noting that 52% of digital gold holders had never owned gold before
