Gold Buying Gains Momentum Amid Price Dip, but Shoppers Prioritise Affordability

SKYGOLD GANDHAR THANGAMAYL

Gold prices in India have slipped about 4% to Rs 1.47 lakh per 10 g, spurring selective purchases ahead of the festive and wedding season. Industry leaders note continued resilience across price segments, with consumers favoring lightweight, lower‑carat, design‑focused pieces and old‑gold exchanges to manage costs. While overall demand remains strong, especially from B2B wholesaler orders and retailer expansions, buyers are more price‑conscious, comparing charges, offers, and brand trust before committing. The trend underscores a shift toward value‑for‑money designs rather than sheer volume of gold purchases

TCS Withholds Senior Staff Bonuses Amid Weak Q3 Performance

TCS

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) has withheld quarterly variable pay (QVA) for senior employees (grades C3A and above, typically 7–10 years of experience or higher) due to missed internal performance targets in the September quarter, according to an internal memo. This is the first time in at least two years that all variable pay was withheld for these workers, though junior employees still received 100% of their eligible bonuses.

The decision follows TCS’s weakest sequential revenue growth in three years (0.5% constant-currency growth), despite a near-20% surge in AI-related revenue. The company cited broader pressures on traditional IT services demand, while investors reacted positively to AI gains (stock rose 6.2%).

This move coincides with U.S. visa restrictions targeting major Indian IT firms (including TCS, Infosys, HCLTech) under a green-card program crackdown, adding to sector uncertainty. TCS did not respond to Reuters’ queries

Uniform 5% GST on Cab and Delivery Platforms

ZOMATO SWIGGY OLAELEC

The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIT) clarified that a flat 5% Goods and Services Tax applies to cab aggregators (Ola, Uber) and delivery platforms (Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon, Flipkart) irrespective of their business models, covering both marketplace and direct delivery services. GST rates will be revised annually, taking effect from April 1, with the GST Council deciding ahead of each fiscal year. Future reforms will focus on simplifying compliance, improving refunds and input tax credit processes, and making the Invoice Management System mandatory. The effective GST rate has fallen to 10.84% after rationalisation, boosting revenue growth
