Venugopal Dhoot Declared Bankrupt by NCLT Over ₹12,668 Cr SBI Loan Default

SBIN ICICIBANK AARON

The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai, declared Venugopal Dhoot, 75, founder of Videocon Industries, bankrupt over a ₹12,668-crore loan default to State Bank of India (SBI). Dhoot, who personally guaranteed loans to Videocon and its subsidiaries, was declared bankrupt after SBI’s petition was accepted. The tribunal appointed a Bankruptcy Trustee to manage proceedings, including asset distribution among creditors.

Dhoot, arrested in 2022 over an ICICI Bank fraud case, had proposed repaying just ₹600 crore (0.42% of dues) over 36 months via a Siliguri project, but the NCLT rejected it as insufficient. The default stems from loans taken by Videocon Industries Ltd (VIL) and Videocon Telecommunications Ltd (VTL), with SBI invoking guarantees in 2018. The bankruptcy process will continue until Dhoot’s discharge under Section 138 of the Insolvency Code.

The NCLT ordered Dhoot to submit a financial statement within 7 days, while the trustee must file a preliminary report in 90 days and quarterly updates. The case involves loans from SBI and its merged associate banks, with defaults totaling ₹9,322 cr (term loan), ₹1,437 cr (VTL facility), and ₹1,908 cr (working capital) as of **September 2026
