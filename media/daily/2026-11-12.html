Lawmakers Urge Gordon Brothers to Extend Poundland’s Creditor Protection

BCG BRIGHTBR

The firm, which bought Poundland for a nominal £1, has not pledged to prioritise job protection, while the UK government also seeks to safeguard employment and business value

Rising Tariffs Spike Costs for US Lithium Mine

EICHERMOT DELTACORP

Lithium Americas raised the projected cost of its Thacker Pass Nevada lithium project by $900 million, citing Trump‑era tariffs, higher labor, and logistics expenses. The first‑phase budget now tops $3.8 billion, pushing the company’s additional funding need to $995 million despite a $2.3 billion DOE loan and GM’s $625 million stake. Shares fell to a record low as lithium prices dip, highlighting broader challenges for U.S. domestic critical‑minerals production amid trade barriers and supply‑chain concerns

Amazon Settles Arizona Lawsuit Over Complex Prime Cancellation Process

Amazon agreed to pay $3 million in refunds to eligible Arizona customers and $3 million in additional costs, including $1.5 million for consumer‑protection investigations, $1.5 million for state legal fees, and $1 million in charitable donations, after the state attorney general sued the company for requiring six steps to cancel its $139‑a‑year Prime membership, a process alleged to deter cancellations and violate “cancel anytime” advertising
