Intel concealed the impact of slowing PC sales

Michigan Pension Fund Sues Intel Over Concealed PC Sales Decline

PCJEWELLER

A Michigan pension fund and other shareholders have filed a class‑action lawsuit accusing Intel of defrauding investors by hiding the extent of a prolonged slowdown in personal‑computer sales and an inventory glut, which triggered a 28.6% share plunge and erased about $140 billion in market value. The complaint also points to a mid‑August $23 billion stock sale, alleges misconduct by CEO Lip‑Bu Tan and CFO David Zinsner, and highlights Intel’s broader challenges including PC demand weakness, memory‑price pressures, competition from AMD and Nvidia, and a failed push into contract chip manufacturing

Jensen Huang’s Possible Return as Nvidia CEO Amid Board Turmoil

YESBANK

Former Nvidia co‑founder Jensen Huang, forced to resign on 12 Nov amid personal scandal and compliance probes, is reportedly discussing a comeback with close allies. A “war room” of Silicon Valley executives is being assembled to lobby for his reinstatement and seek White House support. Interim CEO CFO Colette Kress runs the company while the board searches for a permanent leader. Investor Scott Bessent and Treasury officials urge an external hire, citing cultural and compliance concerns, whereas tech figures like Elon Musk, Tim Sweeney and major investors back Huang’s return. The board faces mounting pressure as employees and an online petition also demand Huang’s reinstatement

Ola Electric’s Cash Crisis Deepens as Market Share Plummets

ATHERENERG OLAELEC

Ola Electric’s cash reserves have shrunk 87% from ₹5,918 cr post‑IPO to ₹750 cr in just over two years, with average monthly burn of ₹215 cr. Sales fell 26% in October, dropping its market share to 5.4% from a peak of ~35% in 2024, while revenue fell 39% YoY to ₹421 cr and losses remain high. The firm raised ₹5,500 cr in its Aug 2024 IPO, ₹780 cr via a June QIP, and is halfway through a ₹1,000 cr rights issue, yet still carries ~₹1,800 cr of debt and a ₹350 cr quarterly cash‑flow outflow. Founder Bhavish Aggarwal has repeatedly pledged shares to fund financing. Analysts say sales must reach 25,000 units/month for cash‑breakeven. Ola is shifting to a dealer model to cut working‑capital needs, targeting 25 k scooters produced in December and 20 k sold via dealers. In contrast, rival Ather has boosted cash to ₹1,893 cr and turned profitable. Crisil flagged inadequate board approvals for ₹40.96 cr of QIP‑raised “general corporate purpose” spend, raising governance concerns

Transasia Bio‑Medicals seeks up to 20% stake sale to raise $150‑200 million

KRSNAA AHCL 5PAISA DBOL MOTILALOFS BCG

Mumbai‑based Transasia Bio‑Medicals, a leading Indian in‑vitro diagnostics manufacturer, is exploring a primary‑plus‑secondary sale of up to 20% of its equity to raise $150‑200 million, attracting interest from private‑equity firms such as TPG, Warburg Pincus, ChrysCapital, Motilal Oswal and Quadria Capital. The fundraise, overseen by O3 Capital, aims to support growth in India’s rapidly expanding med‑tech market, projected to grow from $12 bn in FY24 to $50 bn by 2030, driven by rising incomes, health‑insurance coverage, medical tourism and tier‑2/3 infrastructure development. Transasia, founded in 1979 and now led by former Cipla executive Nishant Saxena, competes with domestic and global players and is part of the Erba group with factories in India, the Czech Republic and Russia

IIFCL Loans ₹2,948 Cr to Yotta with Ownership Clause on Nvidia GPUs

CARERATING AXISBANK MAHABANK RELIABLE SREINFRA INDIANB SBIN CRISIL BCG

India Infrastructure Finance Company (IIFCL) has approved a ₹2,948 crore (≈$315 million) 15‑year loan to Yotta Data Services for its Noida D2 data centre, covering about 80% of the ₹3,879 crore project cost. The loan is contingent on Yotta’s promoter, Darshan Hiranandani, eventually buying the Nvidia B300 GPUs currently rented from Gorilla Technology and on proof of client contracts before the final 20% tranche is disbursed. The clause allows IIFCL to seize the GPUs if Yotta defaults. With this funding, Yotta’s long‑term borrowings rise to ₹13,488 crore (≈$1.4 billion), prompting rating agencies to flag a mismatch between the long debt tenure and the shorter commercial life of GPU assets. Yotta argues the loan follows standard infrastructure financing, with the facility’s infrastructure funded by IIFCL and GPU equipment financed separately. The deal comes as India’s data‑centre capacity is set to triple by 2030, driving heightened capital demand for firms like Yotta

ULA Vulcan Returns with First Amazon Leo Payload

USHAMART SHBAJRG BLUESTARCO

United Launch Alliance successfully launched its Vulcan rocket from Cape Canaveral, its first flight in nine months and its first mission carrying Amazon’s Leo low‑Earth‑orbit internet satellites. The rocket delivered 40 Leo satellites, marking the start of 38 planned Amazon launches on Vulcan after previous delays, solid‑booster issues, and broader industry concerns about a launch‑capacity shortfall as competitors face retirements and test failures. This launch underscores Vulcan’s role as a key alternative to SpaceX’s Falcon 9 and highlights ongoing challenges for Amazon’s satellite network deployment

AHL
