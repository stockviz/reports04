Intuit Lowers Revenue Forecast to Focus on Customer Growth

INVENTURE

Intuit projected fiscal 2027 revenue of $23.28‑$23.51 billion, missing the $23.72 billion Wall Street estimate and signaling a slowdown from 14% growth in fiscal 2026. The deceleration stems from weaker marketing platform sales, declining desktop products, and lower average revenue per TurboTax customer as the company seeks to attract more users. CEO Sasan Goodarzi said the firm will prioritize “big bets,” accelerate customer acquisition, and make deliberate choices for durable long‑term growth. TurboTax revenue is expected to grow only 2‑3% in FY 2027, Mailchimp revenue to be flat to down 1%, and earnings per share are forecast at $22.88‑$23.12, well below analysts’ $27.32 expectation. The outlook reflects a strategic shift toward expanding the customer base despite near‑term sales pressure

ONGC to Invest $200 Million to Boost Venezuela’s San Cristobal Oilfield

ONGC

India’s Oil and Natural Gas Corp (ONGC) plans to spend about $200 million over the next year to revive the San Cristobal field in Venezuela, where output has fallen to 4,000‑5,000 barrels per day from a peak of 45,000‑50,000 bpd. ONGC Videsh has secured a U.S. OFAC licence and will fund both its 40% share and PDVSA’s 60% stake, recouping the investment from future production. The goal is to restore the field to its former peak output

ITC Wins Stay on FSSAI Licence Cancellation Over ‘100%’ Label Claims

ITC

The Delhi High Court has temporarily blocked the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) from cancelling ITC’s licence for its “100% Atta, 100% Madhya Pradesh Wheat, 0% Maida” claims on Aashirvaad MP Chakki Atta labels. The court granted interim relief until the next hearing on September 9, pending a response from the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare on ITC’s petition challenging FSSAI’s advisory.

The court will first assess its jurisdiction over the case, noting that FSSAI’s notices originated from Delhi and Kolkata. ITC has contested: 1. A May 28, 2025 advisory banning “100%” claims on food labels. 2. An August 10 show-cause notice (Kolkata) alleging non-compliance. 3. An August 13 improvement notice (Kolkata) demanding label changes or risking licence suspension.

ITC argues the advisory is arbitrary, violates Article 14 (right to equality), and exceeds FSSAI’s powers under the Food Safety Standards Act, 2006. The court directed both parties to submit briefs on jurisdiction before proceeding

ITAT overturns Rs 11,000‑cr tax disallowance for Reliance Jio, citing lack of nexus to new asset

JIOFIN

The Income Tax Appellate Tribunal in Mumbai dismissed the tax authorities’ claim that Reliance Jio’s Rs 11,003 crore of operational expenses, capitalised as CWIP, should be treated as capital expenditure for tax purposes. The tribunal held that merely recording costs under CWIP does not automatically make them capital in nature; the expenses—covering interconnect charges, employee costs, network upkeep, etc.—were incurred for assets already in use and did not create a new enduring asset. Consequently, it upheld the CIT(A)’s deletion of the entire tax disallowance, emphasizing that each expense must be examined individually for a demonstrable link to asset acquisition or creation

GMRAIRPORT TIMESGTY

Rising Trend: Indians Sell Old Jewellery for Cash Amid Soaring Gold Prices

KALYANKJIL

In August 2026, old-gold-for-cash transactions surged to 20% of jewellers’ business—up from just 5% a year ago—as Indians liquidate household gold due to record prices (₹1.62 lakh per 10g in Mumbai) and rising living costs. Major brands like Kalyan Jewellers, Tanishq, and Joyalukkas have expanded cash-for-gold schemes, traditionally limited to smaller retailers.

Key drivers: - Inflationary pressure forces consumers to unlock liquidity from gold. - Uncertainty over gold rally longevity pushes sales before prices potentially drop. - Higher gold value makes existing holdings more attractive to sell.

Jewellers report increased footfall as buyers rush to capitalize on current prices. Internationally, gold prices pulled back slightly but remain supported by dollar debasement concerns and Federal Reserve signals on inflation. The trend reflects a shift from gold exchanges to outright sales, benefiting jewellers by sourcing recycled gold amid weak demand for new purchases

SpaceX Announces $100 Billion Louisiana Spaceport for Starship Launches

SpaceX plans to build a massive new rocket launch site in Louisiana, investing $100 billion on 125,000 acres near Vermilion Parish (200 miles west of New Orleans). The site, the company’s fourth and largest, aims to support thousands of annual Starship launches, leveraging natural gas access and diverse trajectories. Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry called it a “transformational” deal, comparing its impact to the Louisiana Purchase.

Construction begins in 2027, with the first launch possible by 2029, creating 3,000 jobs over a decade. However, environmental concerns dominate criticism: advocates like the Louisiana Bucket Brigade warn of irreversible damage to fragile coastal ecosystems, despite SpaceX’s promises to restore land and engage the community via town halls. Gwynne Shotwell (SpaceX COO) emphasized conservation efforts, but critics demand slower, more transparent planning.

The project follows SpaceX’s record-breaking IPO in June 2026, briefly making Elon Musk a trillionaire
