SpaceX Announces $100 Billion Starbase Louisiana Spaceport

Elon Musk’s SpaceX plans to invest $100 billion in a 125,000‑acre launch complex in Vermilion Parish, Louisiana, to become the world’s largest spaceport. The “Starbase, Louisiana” will feature over a dozen launch towers capable of 30+ Starship flights per day, propellant production, power generation, deep‑water shipping, vehicle processing facilities, and an airport, with construction starting in 2027 and the first launch slated for 2029. The project is expected to create more than 3,000 jobs and boost the state’s economy, while also raising concerns about environmental impacts. It will be SpaceX’s fourth and largest launch site, supporting a high cadence of Starship missions and broader ambitions such as Mars colonization and space‑based data centers

Bombay HC to Rehear Volkswagen’s $1.4 bn India Tax Dispute

The Mumbai high court has ordered a fresh panel to rehear VW’s challenge to a ₹ 1.4 billion tax demand alleging the German automaker undervalued auto‑component imports over a 12‑year period. The judges said the verdict could not be delivered within the Supreme Court’s stipulated time limit, prompting a postponement. The delay adds uncertainty to VW’s talks on a potential Indian partner and raises broader investor concerns about protracted tax disputes in India

Mid‑Cap Indian IT Firms Outpace Large Caps in BFSI Revenue Growth

MPHASIS WIPRO TECHM LTM TCS COFORGE INFY HCLTECH PERSISTENT HEXT

Indian mid‑cap IT services firms are growing BFSI revenues 9‑23% CAGR (FY20‑FY26), markedly higher than the 5‑8% CAGR of the big five. Coforge and Persistent doubled their BFSI earnings, while LTM, Mphasis and others posted 10‑25% CAGR. Analysts attribute this to mid‑caps’ lower revenue bases, focus on tier‑II/challenger banks in the US/Europe, faster, more flexible engagement models, and higher share‑of‑wallet in existing clients. Large caps face high‑base effects, insourcing by global banks, and longer‑tenure mega‑deals that dampen growth. Consequently, mid‑caps posted 6‑29% full‑year growth in FY26, outpacing the modest or negative growth of TCS, Infosys, Wipro and peers. The shift suggests mid‑caps may capture a structural share of new discretionary BFSI spending

ITAT overturns Rs 11,000‑cr tax disallowance for Reliance Jio, citing lack of nexus to new asset

JIOFIN

The Income Tax Appellate Tribunal in Mumbai dismissed the tax authorities’ claim that Reliance Jio’s Rs 11,003 crore of operational expenses, capitalised as CWIP, should be treated as capital expenditure for tax purposes. The tribunal held that merely recording costs under CWIP does not automatically make them capital in nature; the expenses—covering interconnect charges, employee costs, network upkeep, etc.—were incurred for assets already in use and did not create a new enduring asset. Consequently, it upheld the CIT(A)’s deletion of the entire tax disallowance, emphasizing that each expense must be examined individually for a demonstrable link to asset acquisition or creation

SpaceX Announces $100 Billion Louisiana Spaceport for Starship Launches

SpaceX plans to build a massive new rocket launch site in Louisiana, investing $100 billion on 125,000 acres near Vermilion Parish (200 miles west of New Orleans). The site, the company’s fourth and largest, aims to support thousands of annual Starship launches, leveraging natural gas access and diverse trajectories. Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry called it a “transformational” deal, comparing its impact to the Louisiana Purchase.

Construction begins in 2027, with the first launch possible by 2029, creating 3,000 jobs over a decade. However, environmental concerns dominate criticism: advocates like the Louisiana Bucket Brigade warn of irreversible damage to fragile coastal ecosystems, despite SpaceX’s promises to restore land and engage the community via town halls. Gwynne Shotwell (SpaceX COO) emphasized conservation efforts, but critics demand slower, more transparent planning.

The project follows SpaceX’s record-breaking IPO in June 2026, briefly making Elon Musk a trillionaire
