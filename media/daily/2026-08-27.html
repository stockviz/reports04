MIIL IBULLSLTD BCG ZEEL

Only about Rs 6.25 crore will be recovered from Chandra’s estate, while corporate borrowers remain liable for roughly Rs 1,494 crore. The case is an exceptional personal‑guarantor resolution, distinct from typical corporate insolvency outcomes under the IBC. Overall, IBC‑driven recoveries have been strong, with creditors recovering about Rs 4.32 lakh crore (≈115 % of liquidation value) up to March 2026
