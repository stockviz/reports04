India’s Snack Revolution: A ₹1 Lakh Crore Opportunity

RUBICON TIMESGTY 3MINDIA NFL NESTLEIND JPASSOCIAT YATRA

India’s food landscape is shifting from the traditional three‑meals‑a‑day pattern to a dynamic snack‑driven economy, fueled by rising incomes, time‑poor lifestyles, smaller households, and health consciousness. Snacks now span beyond chips to protein bars, makhana, flavored nuts, yogurts, ready‑to‑eat meals, and functional beverages, with health‑focused products growing rapidly (protein snacks up 300%, protein yogurts up 280%). The snack segment is projected to grow from ₹50,590 crore in 2025 to over ₹1.04 lakh crore by 2034 (CAGR 8.28%). Quick‑commerce platforms (Instamart, Blinkit, Zepto) are accelerating demand, delivering snacks within minutes and expanding into café‑style offerings. Consumers increasingly prioritize convenience, protein, and natural sweeteners, yet indulgence remains strong, creating a split basket of affordable, premium, and health‑oriented items. While domestic snack growth is robust, exporting Indian snacks faces challenges in processing, standards, and branding, limiting global expansion despite potential in ingredients like makhana, millets, and spices. The sector’s future hinges on building scalable processing capabilities, strong branding, and meeting international quality norms
