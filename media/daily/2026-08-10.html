SAMHI VENTIVE CHALET ITCHOTELS THELEELA EIHAHOTELS INDHOTEL

Indian listed hotel operators saw occupancy rise to 63‑66% and RevPAR up 17‑24% YoY in Q1‑Q2 FY27 despite weaker foreign arrivals due to West Asia tensions. KAYNES AAATECH

Exxon Links $80 B Kazakh Oil Investment to Settlement of Long‑Running Dispute

DELTACORP

ExxonMobil has offered to invest $80 billion in a joint venture with Kazakhstan’s KazMunayGas to develop the western part of the Kashagan field, which could add up to 600,000 barrels per day. The proposal ties the investment to the resolution of a $150 billion arbitration dispute over lost revenues and a $5 billion environmental fine. While KazMunayGas appears receptive, political approval remains uncertain, and other partners such as Eni, Shell, TotalEnergies, CNPC, and Inpex are being offered minority stakes or the option to exit the claims. Development costs could reach $250 million for initial studies and $2 billion for engineering, with a final investment decision projected for 2030. The move aims to unlock about 10 billion barrels of oil from the untapped western reservoir, boosting Kazakhstan’s production capacity
