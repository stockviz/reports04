Large FMCG firms outpace smaller rivals amid slowing rural demand and rising costs

GODREJCP DYNPRO HINDUNILVR MARICO NESTLEIND

FMCG volume growth slowed to 3.6% YoY in Q3, down from 4.7% a year earlier, as rural demand moderates and input costs rise. Listed giants such as Hindustan Unilever (7% volume growth), Marico (10% in India), Godrej Consumer Products (8%) and Nestlé India (double‑digit) posted strong gains, while regional players struggle with higher procurement costs, supply‑chain disruptions, and limited ability to absorb price hikes. MIAL Proposes Shifting 14 International Flights to Navi Mumbai Airport by Nov 3

ADANIENPP1

Mumbai International Airport (MIAL) has asked the civil aviation ministry to help move flights to 14 international destinations from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport to the new Navi Mumbai International Airport. In a letter from chief airport officer Vishnu Mohan Jha, MIAL seeks voluntary slot surrender from airlines by 3 Nov, aiming to relocate about five million passengers (≈9% of current traffic) to free capacity for demolition and reconstruction of Terminal 1. If airlines do not submit transition proposals, MIAL will temporarily shift all services to the identified 14 destinations—Bahrain, Dammam, Dhaka, Doha, Fujairah, Jakarta, Jeddah, Kathmandu, Kuwait City, Madinah, Muscat, Ras Al Khaimah, Riyadh, and Sharjah—consolidating international flights at a single airport in the Mumbai region. This move raises concerns about airlines’ historic slot protection and regulatory procedures for relocating foreign carriers
