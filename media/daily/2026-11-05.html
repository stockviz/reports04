CERT‑In flags high‑severity flaws in Tata Motors iRA connected‑vehicle apps

TATAMOTORS

CERT‑In has identified four high‑severity vulnerabilities in Tata Motors iRA 2.0 (≤ 26.7.3) and iRA.ev (≤ 26.7.2) apps, including hard‑coded service credentials, insecure WebView URL handling, logging of authentication tokens, and weak SSL certificate pinning. These issues could enable credential theft, user impersonation, data tampering, and traffic interception. Users are urged to upgrade to iRA 2.0 v26.8.2 and iRA.ev v26.9.1. Tata Motors has not responded to an inquiry

Large FMCG firms outpace smaller rivals amid slowing rural demand and rising costs

GODREJCP DYNPRO HINDUNILVR MARICO NESTLEIND

FMCG volume growth slowed to 3.6% YoY in Q3, down from 4.7% a year earlier, as rural demand moderates and input costs rise. Listed giants such as Hindustan Unilever (7% volume growth), Marico (10% in India), Godrej Consumer Products (8%) and Nestlé India (double‑digit) posted strong gains, while regional players struggle with higher procurement costs, supply‑chain disruptions, and limited ability to absorb price hikes. The gap is widening in rural markets, where smaller manufacturers lack national distribution and bargaining power. Inflation (retail 5.5%, food 7.7%) and erratic rainfall further pressure household budgets, threatening future rural consumption. Analysts warn growth could dip below last year’s level in the next quarter, highlighting a shift toward larger organised players and a challenging environment for smaller FMCG firms

FitLab Files Chapter 11 After Losing Nike Deal and Tariff‑Hit Supply Chain

FitLab Inc., a boutique fitness studio and cardio‑equipment operator, declared Chapter 11 bankruptcy after its partnership with Nike ended, leaving it with ten costly, now‑unbranded studio leases. The loss coincided with supply‑chain disruptions and higher import costs from Trump‑era tariffs that forced its Assault Fitness line to switch manufacturers, sidelining the brand during the 2025 holiday season. Combined with landlord issues, a reduction in operating studios, and recent acquisitions, these factors drove liabilities above $100 million and led to the filing

MIAL Proposes Shifting 14 International Flights to Navi Mumbai Airport by Nov 3

ADANIENPP1

Mumbai International Airport (MIAL) has asked the civil aviation ministry to help move flights to 14 international destinations from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport to the new Navi Mumbai International Airport. In a letter from chief airport officer Vishnu Mohan Jha, MIAL seeks voluntary slot surrender from airlines by 3 Nov, aiming to relocate about five million passengers (≈9% of current traffic) to free capacity for demolition and reconstruction of Terminal 1. If airlines do not submit transition proposals, MIAL will temporarily shift all services to the identified 14 destinations—Bahrain, Dammam, Dhaka, Doha, Fujairah, Jakarta, Jeddah, Kathmandu, Kuwait City, Madinah, Muscat, Ras Al Khaimah, Riyadh, and Sharjah—consolidating international flights at a single airport in the Mumbai region. This move raises concerns about airlines’ historic slot protection and regulatory procedures for relocating foreign carriers

Mercedes‑Benz Aims for Deeper India Footprint with Increased Localization and Supplier Integration

Mercedes‑Benz plans to boost its Indian presence by investing an additional ₹200 crore, raising cumulative investment to ₹3,200 crore, and targeting its highest annual sales yet after selling 15,190 cars Jan‑Sept 2026 (an 8% YoY rise). The automaker assembles 11+ models in Pune using CKD kits and sources engines, axles and other parts locally, with a view to eventually supplying components to global operations. Its R&D centre in India, the second‑largest outside Germany, will see expanded roles in engineering, software and powertrain development as the company eyes further growth in manufacturing, technology and sourcing over the next five years
