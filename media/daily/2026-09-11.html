Coca‑Cola CEO says it won’t overreact to rivals’ low‑price tactics as Campa Cola gains ground in India

GROBTEA CRISIL RELIANCE

Coca‑Cola chief Henrique Braun emphasized that the company will ignore “irrational” pricing moves by competitors like Reliance’s Campa Cola, focusing instead on core fundamentals—packaging, brand mix, revenue‑growth management and its bottling network. While Campa Cola has become the fourth‑largest carbonated soft‑drink brand, boosting market share of low‑price entrants to 6‑7% in FY26, Braun said Coca‑Cola will stick to its long‑term strategy, leveraging a locally‑tailored portfolio and distribution strength rather than chasing short‑term price wars

GST Boost Fuels Surge in India’s Commercial Vehicle Sales

COMSYN ASHOKLEY TATAMOTORS

India’s top commercial vehicle makers—Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, and VE Commercial Vehicles—have raised their FY sales growth outlook to 10‑15%, up from an earlier 4‑6% forecast, after demand outpaced expectations. CV sales rose 18% in Q1 and surged up to 40% in August, driven by GST rationalisation, fleet replacement, lower interest rates, easier financing, and renewed construction, mining, and infrastructure activity. Executives anticipate continued momentum, projecting high single‑digit growth for medium and heavy trucks and double‑digit growth for light commercial vehicles through FY27

Fairfax Explores Stake Sale in IIFL Finance Amid IDBI Bank Deal

IIFLCAPS DHUNINV ADVENTHTL IDBI AHL CHOLAHLDNG CSBBANK IIFL

Fairfax Financial Holdings, led by billionaire Prem Watsa, is in talks with Advent International and Temasek Holdings to sell its 13.7% stake in IIFL Finance (valued at ~₹3,500 crore) as part of its plan to acquire IDBI Bank. The move aligns with regulatory requirements for the IDBI deal and may partially fund the acquisition.

Fairfax previously reduced its stake from 15.2% to 13.7% in July via a ₹374 crore sale to Capital Group. Blackstone is also reportedly in discussions for the stake.

IIFL Finance’s shares rose 0.32% (BSE), while the company clarified it cannot comment on Fairfax’s negotiations. Fairfax may merge CSB Bank (where it holds 40%) and IIFL Capital (where it plans to raise its stake to 51%) into IDBI Bank post-acquisition. Temasek and Advent declined to comment on speculation

Indian Oil Overhauls Leadership Succession and Recruitment Amid Diversification Drive

IOC

Indian Oil Corp, the nation’s largest refiner, is revamping its leadership and succession framework to build a larger pool of executives for future senior roles, aligning with the oil ministry’s plan to remove three director‑level board posts. The move comes as the firm diversifies beyond refining into data centres, nuclear power, shipping, critical minerals and battery manufacturing. A new talent‑identification program, part of the three‑year Project Sprint launched in April 2025, earmarks high‑potential staff below the board for structured leadership development, supporting IOC’s goal of $1 trillion revenue and net‑zero operational emissions by 2047. Concurrently, IOC has overhauled entry‑level recruitment, replacing the GATE‑based process with its own examination to broaden the applicant pool, improve geographic representation and reduce early‑stage attrition, with the first cycle already showing a marked improvement

Canara HSBC Life fined Rs 1 crore for selling an out‑of‑age annuity to an 88‑year‑old

CANHLIFE CANBK

The IRDAI penalized Canara HSBC Life Insurance ₹1 crore for mis‑selling a deferred annuity (₹2 lakh annual premium for four years) to an 88‑year‑old via Canara Bank, despite the product’s entry age limit of 30‑80. Deficiencies were noted in verification calls, proposal forms, disclosure, and solicitation. The insurer refunded ₹4.09 lakh
