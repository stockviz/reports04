FCNR Inflows Push Indian Banking Liquidity to Four‑Year High

SOUTHBANK JSFB CSBBANK

Banking system liquidity surged to ₹6.65 lakh crore by August 31, the highest since May 2022, as FCNR(B) deposits flowed in. Excess funds are largely parked in RBI’s variable‑rate reverse repo (VRRR) operations, with banks preferring 1‑3‑day tenures at 5.24% over longer slots. VRRR bids have been weak (e.g., 7‑day ₹6 lakh crore operation received ₹1.14 lakh crore bids). The weighted average call rate fell to 4.98%, well below the repo rate. Treasury heads note liquidity is unevenly distributed, mainly benefiting large banks, and expect excess cash to normalize to ₹1‑1.5 lakh crore by December

India’s Car Sales Boom: 5 Million Units Milestone in Sight

DAMCAPITAL MARUTI TATAMOTORS M&M ACGL HYUNDAI

India’s car sales are projected to surpass 5 million units in the current fiscal year, making it the third country after China and the US to achieve this milestone. Despite global uncertainties—including the US-Iran war and rising oil prices—sales grew 22% year-on-year (April–August), driven by lower GST rates (effective Sept 22, 2025) and strong demand in urban and rural markets.

Key highlights: - Maruti Suzuki is expanding capacity (new Gujarat plant with ₹35,000 crore investment) and revising growth targets to 6.1–6.3 million units by FY31, with small-car demand surging faster. - Hyundai aims for 8–10% growth, citing upcoming model launches and plant expansions. - Industry leaders attribute success to broad-based demand and festive season momentum, though long-term growth to 15 million units remains uncertain. - Tata Motors and Mahindra also target 10%+ growth, defying earlier conservative estimates

India’s EV Market Booms in August: 51% YoY Growth Despite Sequential Dip

OLAELEC TATAMOTORS M&M TVSMOTOR BAJAJ-AUTO JSWCEMENT ATHERENERG

In August 2026, India’s electric vehicle (EV) sales surged 51% year-on-year, driven by strong consumer demand, though registrations dipped 12% sequentially due to regional transport office holidays for festivals like Onam and Rakshabandhan. Electric car registrations reached 29,687 units, while electric two-wheelers (2W) from top five companies grew 69% YoY to 160,000 units.

Bajaj Auto and TVS Motor led 2W growth with 235% and 91% YoY increases, respectively, while Ola Electric was the only major player to decline (29% YoY). Tata Motors remained the top EV carmaker (12,984 units), followed by Mahindra & Mahindra and JSW MG Motor.

Despite the sequential drop, EV penetration for 2Ws slipped slightly to 10.7% (from 11.2% in July) but stayed well above last year’s levels. Manufacturers are expanding capacity to meet sustained demand, with all major players—including Tata, Mahindra, Bajaj, TVS, and Ather Energy—ramping up production
