US Suspends Cognizant’s New PERM Green‑Card Filings Amid Visa‑Fraud Probe

The U.S. Department of Labor’s Inspector General halted Cognizant’s (and Cloudera’s) new permanent labor certification (PERM) applications following whistle‑blower allegations of H‑1B and PERM fraud. The suspension, coordinated with the White House Fraud Task Force, targets only fresh filings and does not affect existing approvals or Cognizant’s H‑1B program. The action is part of a broader Trump‑era crackdown on alleged abuses in employment‑based immigration programs

ONDC Shifts Focus to Logistics, Mobility, and Farmer‑Producer Aggregation

NFL PNB SBIN BSE PAYTM

The Open Network for Digital Commerce is moving away from a broad consumer‑retail push toward targeted initiatives in logistics, mobility, and agricultural supply chains. Its new “ONDC 2.0” strategy centers on three platforms—DigiDukaan, DigiHaat, and DigiBazaar—to make local retailer inventories searchable, aggregate farmer and artisan produce (via DigiHaat’s Swadeshi marketplace), and connect merchants to multiple delivery providers through FleetConnect. The network now emphasizes interoperable infrastructure for fragmented sectors such as micro‑fleet logistics, public‑transport ticketing, and auto bookings, aiming to reduce integration complexity and give makers‑of‑India broader access to buyers. Recent leadership changes and a ₹430 crore funding round (including investors like Zoho, Uber, Paytm, and major banks) support this pivot, though concerns remain about achieving sustainable volumes and consistent business models for smaller operators

LIV Golf Files for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy and Plots a Restructured Future

BFINVEST

LIV Golf entered Chapter 11 in New Jersey with $500 million‑plus in debt after Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund withdrew its backing. The league has agreed to a restructuring plan led by BC Partners, secured $49.6 million in debtor‑in‑possession financing, and aims to emerge by 2027 with a scaled‑down “LIV 2.0” featuring player‑majority ownership, a 75‑player field, a 54‑hole cut, Monday qualifiers, and a focus on markets in Australia, South Africa and Asia. Top players such as Jon Rahm and Bryson DeChambeau remain under contract, but their future participation is uncertain. The filing lists $100‑$500 million in assets versus $500‑$1 billion in liabilities, with many creditors, including players, awaiting payment. The league hopes the court‑supervised process will give it time to resolve obligations, attract new investment, and build a more sustainable model

Air India Shifts Small‑City Service to Low‑Cost Arm Air India Express

TCIEXP BCG TENNIND

Air India is withdrawing from several tier‑II and tier‑III markets—Coimbatore, Bhopal, Aurangabad, Madurai, Tirupati, Jodhpur, Jamnagar, plus routes like Mumbai‑Kochi and Kochi‑Delhi—handing them to Air India Express, which now operates 46 domestic and 17 international stations with 474 daily flights in FY26. The move, driven by outgoing CEO Campbell Wilson and part of Tata’s two‑airline strategy, positions Air India as a premium, long‑haul and metro‑focused carrier while Air India Express serves thinner, price‑sensitive domestic routes and feeds passengers to major hubs. The transition has been ongoing since 2024, accelerating in 2025. Analysts say the reallocation makes economic sense if routes are matched correctly, improving fleet utilization and hub‑spoke efficiency. Both airlines posted a combined FY26 loss of ₹22,238 crore on revenues of ₹71,870 crore. New CEO Tewolde Gebremariam, former Ethiopian Airlines chief, will soon take over Air India, shifting focus from turnaround to execution and expansion

360ONE GENESYS PWL

JSW‑Volkswagen ink MoU for 51:49 India JV

ATULAUTO BCG HYUNDAI

JSW Group and Volkswagen’s Indian arm (Skoda Auto Volkswagen India Pvt Ltd) have signed a non‑binding MoU to launch a 51:49 joint venture, with exclusive talks on valuation and structure aimed at a binding deal by end‑2026. The JV will house all eight Skoda and VW brands (with future EV roll‑outs) and may later add Audi, Porsche, Lamborghini and Bentley. JSW will fund an initial equity stake, with much of its consideration deferred, while Volkswagen’s potential customs‑duty tax exposure (≈₹20,000 cr) will be factored into the valuation. The partnership seeks deeper localisation, shared platforms, higher plant utilisation (400,000‑unit capacity vs 100,000‑unit sales) and to use India as an export hub for EVs. The deal follows Volkswagen’s three‑year search for a local partner and aligns with its broader cost‑cutting restructuring

Festive Season Boosts Hotel Bookings and Rates in India

YATRA EASEMYTRIP MITTAL HOTELRUGBY TIMESGTY

Hotel demand is surging as Indian travelers plan longer 5‑9 day trips for combined festivals, school holidays and leaves. Bookings are 10‑15% above last year, with average daily rates up 8‑10% and occupancy expected to rise from the Q1 FY2026 base of 67‑69%. Travelers are spending more on family‑focused experiences, wellness, local cuisine and resort activities. Search traffic on EaseMyTrip is up 40‑45% month‑on‑month, indicating early planning for the October‑December festive and wedding period. Industry heads from Radisson, Ibis, EaseMyTrip and Yatra predict strong occupancy, revenue per available room growth, and heightened demand on both leisure and visiting‑family segments

Air India losses not a threat to Singapore Airlines services

TENNIND

Singapore’s transport minister affirmed that Air India’s current losses pose no risk to Singapore Airlines’ ability to operate essential services in Singapore. He noted that SIA’s substantial cash reserves, undrawn credit facilities, and independent financing mitigate any impact, and that regulatory concerns would only arise if losses materially constrained SIA’s fleet, maintenance, or network. The minister also defended the strategic rationale for SIA’s 25.1% stake in Air India, emphasizing the need for overseas expansion to sustain growth

Gold Prices Surge Push Consumers to Exchange Old Jewellery, Slashing Advance‑Purchase Deposits

JSWCEMENT RCOM TITAN KALYANKJIL THANGAMAYL 3MINDIA DPABHUSHAN

Gold prices jumped 56% in FY26, prompting Indian jewellers to see a sharp decline in advance‑purchase scheme deposits—Titan’s Tanishq down 39% to ₹2,116 cr, Reliance Retail and Jos Alukkas each down ~25% YoY. Sandoz Targets Massive Biosimilar Growth, Aims to Double Sales by 2035

Swiss drugmaker Sandoz plans to more than double its net sales by 2035, with the majority coming from biosimilars. It will expand its biosimilar portfolio to 100 products by 2040, targeting mid‑to‑high single‑digit annual sales growth (constant currency) from 2025‑2030. The company excludes generic GLP‑1 drugs from its forecasts but sees a “billions” dollar upside. Investments include $1.1 bn in Slovenia and an additional $300 m for a new Ljubljana manufacturing site. Sandoz sees a “golden decade” for biosimilars as many branded drugs lose patents and is seeking U.S. investment despite tariff threats

Government Sub‑Committee Backs Doorstep Drug Delivery Rule, Calls for Stricter Implementation

SWIGGY

A government sub‑committee recommends retaining the 2020 notification (GSR 220(E)) that permits doorstep delivery of medicines, warning that its removal would hurt elderly, chronic‑ill patients and those in rural areas. The panel urges tighter enforcement of existing provisions to prevent unauthorized online sales and ensure digital platforms obtain valid prescriptions, rather than scrapping the rule as demanded by the All India Organisation of Chemists and Druggists

India’s DCC Approves Satellite Spectrum Pricing for Jio, Starlink, and OneWeb

JIOFIN

The Digital Communications Commission (DCC), the top decision‑making body of the Department of Telecommunications, has cleared a proposal to charge satellite communication firms a 5% adjusted gross revenue (AGR) fee for spectrum use, with a 1% reduction for services in rural and remote areas. This move paves the way for allocating spectrum to Starlink, Eutelsat OneWeb, and Jio Satellite. Although the operators have obtained satcom permits and space regulator authorizations, they have been unable to launch services due to pending spectrum allocation and security clearances. A cabinet note is being prepared, and a final decision is expected soon

Firms are aggressively returning to the US loan market to reprice existing debt and secure financing for leveraged buyouts and acquisitions. Second Round Launched for India’s Coal Gasification Scheme

ADANIENPP1 SHYAMTEL GALLANTT NTPC SHREEPUSHK

The government has opened the second application round for its ₹37,500‑crore coal gasification programme, after receiving seven proposals in the first round from Adani Enterprises, NTPC, Talcher Fertilisers, Gallantt Ispat and Shyam Sel & Power. Projects include urea production, direct reduced iron and synthetic natural gas, aimed at scaling domestic feedstock and fuel output to cut import dependence on LNG, urea, ammonia and methanol. Coal stocks stand at 123.7 million tonnes—enough for about 51 days of power‑plant consumption—with an additional 100 million tonnes in transit
