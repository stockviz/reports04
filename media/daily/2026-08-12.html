Glenmark Pharma US Subsidiary Settles $15.28 Million Antitrust Dispute with Humana

GLENMARK

Glenmark’s U.S. unit agreed to pay $15.28 million (including interest) in two installments to resolve Humana’s antitrust claims over alleged price‑fixing and market allocation of generic drugs. The case stems from broader litigation dating back to 2016, involving over 35 complaints by payers, purchasers and state attorneys general. Glenmark denies any wrongdoing and emphasizes the settlement does not constitute an admission of liability. This agreement follows a previously disclosed $29.63 million settlement with state attorneys general. Glenmark’s U.S. operations remain significant, with 220 approved generics and 52 pending FDA applications

CSB Releases Final Report on 2025 Clairton Coke Works Explosion

JINDALSTEL

The U.S. Chemical Safety and Hazard Investigation Board (CSB) issued a final report on the August 2025 blast at U.S. Steel’s Clairton Coke Works that killed two workers and injured 11. The explosion occurred when workers used pressurized water to flush a 70‑year‑old cast‑iron gas valve, which ruptured and released combustible coke‑oven gas. The CSB found that U.S. Steel lacked written procedures, training, and safe work practices for flushing gas valves, and that an industrial cleaning contractor also had no formal policies. The board recommends both parties develop detailed written procedures and train employees for such operations. OSHA had previously fined U.S. Steel $118,214 for inadequate safety documentation. The report also highlighted the plant’s history of accidents, underinvestment, and prior explosions, noting that better siting evaluations and equipment upgrades could have reduced the severity of the incident
