Unresolved Questions

  • Will the board prioritize stability (Huang’s return) or risk mitigation (outsider CEO)?

The union’s certification came via remedial certification after allegations of Amazon’s anti-union tactics, including a hiring spree to delay certification.

Broader Context: This follows Amazon’s first U.S. unionization in Staten Island (2022), though negotiations remain stalled. Amazon opposes third-party unions, advocating for direct worker input, while Unifor celebrates the “historic win” for labor rights.

Amazon’s Stance: The company insists on direct negotiations without unions or dues, calling the arbitrated contract “defying common sense.” Unifor and workers argue it’s a rare victory against corporate resistance
