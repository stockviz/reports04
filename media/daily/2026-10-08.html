Supreme Court Rules Electricity Subsidy to Existing Businesses Taxable

AARON

The apex court held that electricity subsidies given to companies that do not fund new capital assets are revenue receipts and therefore taxable under the Income Tax Act. In Mepco Industries v. Commissioner of Income Tax, the Court affirmed lower‑court rulings that the subsidy was intended to offset operating costs, not to contribute to capital formation, despite the scheme’s broader objective of encouraging industrialisation in backward regions. Consequently, the ₹16.20 lakh subsidy received for FY 1997‑98 is includable in taxable income
