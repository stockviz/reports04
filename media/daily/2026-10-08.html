Sharjah aims to attract $3 billion in Indian FDI by 2027, leveraging the $2 billion expansion of Khorfakkan port—situated outside the Strait of Hormuz—to draw Indian manufacturers, logistics and transportation firms. Supreme Court Rules Electricity Subsidy to Existing Businesses Taxable

AARON

The apex court held that electricity subsidies given to companies that do not fund new capital assets are revenue receipts and therefore taxable under the Income Tax Act. In Mepco Industries v. Commissioner of Income Tax, the Court affirmed lower‑court rulings that the subsidy was intended to offset operating costs, not to contribute to capital formation, despite the scheme’s broader objective of encouraging industrialisation in backward regions. Consequently, the ₹16.20 lakh subsidy received for FY 1997‑98 is includable in taxable income

India’s Hotel Transactions Surge to $493 Million in H1 2026, Tripling Year‑on‑Year

In the first half of 2026, Indian hotel deals reached $493 million—almost three times the amount recorded a year earlier—according to JLL data. The luxury segment led with 52% of the volume, followed by upscale (35%), upper‑upscale (12%) and economy (1%). Tier‑II/III cities accounted for about 60% of transactions, while Tier‑I cities made up 40%. JLL now projects total hotel transactions for calendar 2026 at roughly $1.1 billion, a 94% rise from 2025’s $567 million. In Q1 2026, India posted $345 million in deals, placing it fourth in the Asia‑Pacific region behind Japan, China and South Korea, and representing 13% of the region’s quarterly transaction volume
