Monsoon Outlook 2024: El Niño Peaks Late, Positive IOD Offers Relief

In an interview, Skymet’s Vice‑President of Meteorology Mahesh Palawat explains that the current El Niño is among the strongest in 76 years but has not yet reached its peak, which is expected in October‑November. Temperatures over the central‑eastern Pacific are about 1.4 °C above normal, suggesting a delayed but potentially significant impact on the Indian monsoon, especially in the latter half of August.
