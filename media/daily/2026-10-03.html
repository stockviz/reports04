L&T eyes Itochu partnership and Kandla hub for green ammonia export

LTF LT BIRLACORPN

Larsen & Toubro is negotiating with Japan’s Itochu Corp to sell a minority stake in its clean‑energy unit, L&T Energy GreenTech Ltd, and to secure off‑take of its planned 300 ktpa green‑ammonia plant at Kandla, Gujarat. The project, earmarked as a strategic export hub for green hydrogen derivatives, will support Itochu’s bunkering operations and broader marine‑fuel transition. NCLT Admits Kotak Mahindra Bank’s Insolvency Petition Against Unity Realty

KOTAKBANK LOTUSDEV KARURVYSYA CAPACITE

The National Company Law Tribunal has admitted Kotak Mahindra Bank’s Section 7 insolvency petition against Mumbai‑based Unity Realty and Developers for a default of ₹68.49 crore, allowing the corporate insolvency resolution process to begin. The claim stems from a corporate guarantee tied to loans originally sanctioned in 2008, restructured in 2015, and invoked in 2019. A Delhi Debt Recovery Tribunal had earlier ordered the guarantors to pay ₹39.45 crore plus interest, later escalated to ₹55.94 crore through a recovery certificate. Unity Realty’s objections on limitation, procedural standing, and restructuring documents were rejected, with the tribunal holding the DRT judgment and recovery certificate constitute a fresh cause of action within the limitation period, and confirming that a guarantor can be pursued under Section 7 of the IBC

JKC Sports to Invest ₹100‑150 Crore in Sports Infrastructure Expansion Over Next 4‑5 Years

JKCEMENT SUPTANERY TOYAMSL

JKC Sports, a Singhania family venture, plans to inject another ₹100‑150 crore in the next four to five years, matching its past four‑year investment, to develop sports infrastructure, technology, and new IP across 8‑10 sports including cricket, bridge, squash, shooting, swimming, badminton, wrestling, and powerlifting. The company owns the Gwalior franchise in the Madhya Pradesh T20 League, partners with the Bridge Federation of India, and holds ties with Lucknow Super Giants, Kashi Rudras, American Gambits, and the Sheru Classic Universe fitness platform. It evaluates franchise deals based on federation stability, governance, financial transparency, and fan‑base potential, while also building its own grassroots IP and a dedicated capability centre to serve the broader sports ecosystem

FAA Says Boeing 737 Max Software Glitch Not a Safety Hazard

DELTACORP TCNSBRANDS

A Federal Aviation Administration Corrective Action Review Board concluded that a software issue affecting the autopilot’s pitch‑control mode on the 737 Max does not compromise safety, noting pilots retain full control and clear indications. The finding supports Boeing’s push to certify the larger 737 Max 10, which has been delayed pending safety review, while the smaller Max 7 was already certified. Boeing’s stock rose modestly, and the company recently secured a union agreement and won a Navy fighter‑jet contract

Anup Bagchi’s Test: Restoring Investor Trust at HDFC Bank

ICICIPRULI HDFCBANK ICICIBANK

Incoming CEO Anup Bagchi, appointed to lead HDFC Bank from 27 Oct, faces the urgent task of rebuilding investor confidence after loan growth and ROE fell to industry averages. Analysts stress the need for fewer, clearer performance metrics—such as faster loan growth, stable NIM, a 30 % cost‑to‑income ratio, and a 90 % loan‑to‑deposit ratio—and transparent timelines. Bernstein warns that the current spread of targets makes progress hard to gauge, while Macquarie highlights Bagchi’s retail/wholesale banking and insurance experience but flags potential senior‑leadership attrition. Five operational priorities are identified: revive retail (especially unsecured) lending, boost low‑cost deposits, enhance technology, improve service, and leverage subsidiaries for cross‑selling. Successful execution and stable senior management are seen as key to regaining the bank’s valuation premium
