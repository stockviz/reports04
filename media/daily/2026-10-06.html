Madhya Pradesh’s Massive Aviation Expansion Plan

GMRAIRPORT

Madhya Pradesh is launching an ambitious aviation drive under its Civil Aviation Policy 2025, targeting an airport every 150 km, an airstrip every 75 km, and a helipad every 45 km, plus permanent helipads in all 230 assembly constituencies. Currently, the state has eight operational airports, over 20 airstrips, and ~220 helipads; two more airports (Ujjain and Shivpuri) are under construction, bringing the total to ten. Five additional airport proposals await central approval, with Singrauli prioritized. Ten existing airstrips (including Singrauli, Sagar, Neemuch, Chhindwara, etc.) are slated for upgrade under UDAN. The new Ujjain airport (Datana‑Matana) will support Simhastha 2028. The government plans to acquire its own aircraft to cut official travel costs. The PM Shri Air Ambulance Service, launched in May 2024, has already moved 186 patients. Heli‑tourism links key religious, heritage, and wildlife sites, serving over 5,200 passengers. International connectivity expands with Indore‑Dubai and Indore‑Abu Dhabi flights, the latter being the first under the new policy. Overall, the initiative aims to boost tourism, emergency response, medical access, and economic growth across the state

Smartphone and consumer‑electronics makers such as Samsung, Vivo, Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo and Nothing are lengthening no‑cost or low‑cost EMI tenures to as long as 30 months—up to six‑eight months longer than usual—to offset steep price rises and revive demand, especially in mass‑to‑mid‑range segments. The brands are absorbing interest costs (8‑18% for 8‑30 month plans, up to 24% for longer loans) to keep monthly payouts low, even offering such schemes for entry‑level phones and appliances like TVs and refrigerators. Industry insiders say finance schemes now drive about a third of mobile and electronics sales, expected to rise to half during the festive period, with consumer‑finance share in smartphone sales climbing to 42‑43% this year. The strategy aims to protect margins while maintaining volume growth amid a 30‑35% rise in handset prices and a 12% drop in sales year‑to‑date

MAXHEALTH

Max Healthcare MD Abhay Soi emphasized that Indian private hospitals should not be judged solely on the price of drugs, injections, or consumables.
