Crypto Rug Pulls Explained: How to Spot and Avoid the Scam

A rug pull is a crypto investment scam where project creators hype a token, gather funds, then abandon the project, draining assets and leaving investors with worthless tokens. While similar frauds exist in other markets, crypto rug pulls often involve hype through social media, false promises of high returns, and lack of transparent founders or documentation. To protect yourself, avoid hype-driven investments, verify project teams and use cases, beware of guaranteed returns, and stick to reputable, regulated platforms while conducting thorough personal research
