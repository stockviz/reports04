BMW India Plans Fourth Price Hike This Year Amid Rupee Depreciation and Rising Commodity Costs

BMWVENTLTD

BMW Group India will implement another price increase, the fourth in 2026, after already raising prices by up to 5% across its BMW and MINI lineups. CEO Hardeep Singh Brar cited a roughly 18% depreciation of the rupee since January and higher commodity costs as the main drivers. The announcement came alongside the launch of the new BMW 7 Series and locally produced i7 models, with prices ranging from ₹1.95 crore for the 740 M Sport to ₹2.65 crore for the i7 M70. Deliveries are set to begin in October 2026

Ultraviolette targets 20% electric‑motorcycle share in India within 2–3 years

BMWVENTLTD BIRLACORPN ASKAUTOLTD TVSMOTOR

Ultraviolette Automotive, backed by TVS Motor and Qualcomm Ventures, says electric motorcycles could capture 20% of India’s market by 2029, up from the current 1%. Riding on demand for its F77 sports bike and X47 crossover, the firm is expanding capacity with a new plant near Hosur, Tamil Nadu, capable of 250,000 units a year (expandable to 500,000) and aims for 300,000‑500,000 units total capacity in the next five years, backed by a ₹779 crore investment. Production is rising from 600‑700 units/month to about 1,000 soon, with expectations of 10,000 units/month as new models – the Shockwave motorcycle and Tesseract scooter – launch at sub‑₹2 lakh pricing. Investors include TVS (26%), Lingotto, TDK Ventures, Qualcomm Ventures, Zoho and Speciale Invest. Ultraviolette already sells in 20 European markets and plans to enter North America and Latin America via distributors
