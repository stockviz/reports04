Regulatory Crackdown on FMCG Label Claims in India

ITC ABBOTINDIA 3MINDIA HALEOSLABS NESTLEIND

Indian food and beverage companies are facing heightened scrutiny over front‑pack wording such as “100%”, “no added sugar”, and “organic”. The FSSAI has issued over 150 notices to major brands like Nestlé, PepsiCo, and ITC for misleading claims and is proposing a red front‑of‑pack warning label for products high in saturated fat, sugar, or salt, to be rolled out in phases. Legal experts warn that absolute claims must be backed by independent verification, and courts are already challenging regulator directives, as seen in the ITC “100% Atta” case. Companies are incurring costs to revise packaging, withdraw ads, and bolster compliance teams, with senior executives shifting focus from marketing to quality checks. A recent study found 33.6% of examined claims were non‑compliant, especially in plant‑based drinks and snacks. Compared internationally, India’s approach is more reactive than the EU’s precautionary model, but the proposed FoPNL marks a significant shift. Brands are urged to prioritize accurate ingredient lists and nutrition facts over flashy front‑pack claims

Mumbai’s Redevelopment Surge Reshapes Housing Market

MAHSCOOTER

TIMESGTY

The recent Delhi hostel collapse underscores the need for a regulated, institutionalized student housing sector.
