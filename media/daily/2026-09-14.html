India’s August Inflation: Key Takeaways from Nirmal Bank’s Deputy Head

AXISBANK

Consumer inflation rose to 4.82% in August, slightly below expectations, driven mainly by food price increases (food inflation at 5.66%). Core inflation edged up to 4.4%, influenced by higher petroleum diesel and gold prices. Rural inflation (5.23%) outpaced urban (4.31%) due to food’s larger weight in rural baskets. Structural factors such as higher sugar and onion prices—linked to lower crop output and a weak monsoon—are adding pressure, though the full impact of El Niño remains uncertain. Services inflation shows pockets of pricing power, especially in restaurants and accommodation (8.38%), reflecting spill‑over from food and LPG costs. Imported inflation risk is modest for now; domestic fuel prices are stable, but sustained crude oil spikes above $100 could trigger future price hikes. Overall, inflation remains broadly in line with RBI’s 4.7% target, and while vigilance is needed, an immediate rate hike is not warranted

AI‑Generated Answers Can Cite Correct Sources Yet Still Be Wrong

Retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG) helps enterprises pull information from internal documents, but merely retrieving a relevant source doesn’t guarantee the AI’s answer is accurate. Models can misinterpret, combine facts, or over‑generalize, producing plausible‑sounding responses that the cited material doesn’t actually support. This creates a new risk for high‑stakes tasks such as legal, financial, and compliance work. Experts argue that next‑generation RAG systems must add verification steps—checking each claim against the evidence—rather than just providing citations. The focus is shifting from capability (search and summarize) to reliability (evidence grounding, claim validation, and clear traceability) to preserve productivity gains while reducing the need for extensive human fact‑checking
