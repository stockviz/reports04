Bathla Group Faces Imminent Liquidation Without Longer‑Term Funding

BCG

Australian builder Bathla Group, burdened with A$3.4 billion in debt, secured only A$4 million in emergency financing from five private lenders, buying just two weeks of limited operations. With A$130 million owed to unsecured creditors and A$3.1 billion to secured lenders, the company must obtain longer‑term funds within days or risk wholesale liquidation, project suspensions, job losses, and broader spillover in Australia’s strained property and private‑credit markets. Regulators are probing private‑credit exposures as the crisis highlights hidden‑debt risks and the vulnerability of real‑estate financing amid rising rates

Vi Plans ₹35,000‑crore, 10‑Year Consortium Loan for Decade‑Long Capex

PNB CANBK IDEA UNIONBANK ABCAPITAL BANKINDIA HDFCBANK BANKBARODA SBIN ICICIBANK

Vodafone Idea (Vi) is assembling a consortium of 8–10 lenders, led by State Bank of India (SBI) which will take about 20% (≈₹7,000 crore) of a ₹35,000‑crore term loan to fund its next‑decade capital expenditure. The remaining amount will be split among other public and private banks, each committing at least ₹1,500 crore. SBI’s conditions include oversight of cash flows, a guarantee that any investment shortfall beyond the loan be covered by Vi, and that Kumar Mangalam Birla remain chairman for the loan tenure while the Aditya Birla Group maintains its equity stake after warrant conversions. The loan aims to support ₹60,000 crore of planned upgrades, supplemented by internal accruals and equity infusion. Major participants expected include NBFC‑Fin (≈₹4,000 crore), Punjab National Bank, Canara Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, ICICI, and HDFC, with final terms due by mid‑October

Experts Call for Regulated Student Housing Ecosystem in India

TIMESGTY

The recent Delhi hostel collapse underscores the need for a regulated, institutionalized student housing sector. With over 43 million higher‑education students in India, organized accommodation serves less than 0.5 % of enrolments, leaving millions in unsafe, unregulated facilities. Experts propose recognizing student housing as a distinct real‑estate asset class, introducing unified safety certifications, space‑per‑bed norms, and single‑window municipal licensing. Integrating student housing into urban master plans via transit‑oriented development, higher floor‑area ratios, and mixed‑use zoning near metro lines and university clusters would make compliant housing financially viable for developers. Public‑private partnerships using Design‑Build‑Finance‑Operate‑Transfer frameworks can help institutions upgrade hostels without straining capital. Transparent regulatory standards and organized investment are essential to bridge the demand‑supply gap and ensure safe, high‑quality student living nationwide

HDFC Bank CEO Hunt: Insider vs. Outsider Contenders

ISEC 3MINDIA HDFCBANK ICICIPRULI ICICIBANK

HDFC Bank has shortlisted internal candidate deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha and an external candidate (chosen from a pool that included ICICI Prudential Life CEO Anup Bagchi and Citi India CEO K Balasubramanian) for its CEO role, seeking RBI approval for a six‑month tenure relaxation so Bharucha can serve a full three‑year term. The bank received over 150 applications, narrowed to ~50, and interviewed fewer than 10 bankers. Bharucha, with the bank since 1995, offers continuity but faces a RBI 15‑year tenure cap in 2029. Bagchi brings three decades of ICICI Group experience across banking and insurance, while Balasubramanian offers corporate‑banking expertise and prior stint at HDFC Bank. The regulator‑submitted external candidate’s identity remains unconfirmed

Thermax Refocuses on Growth After Recent Missteps

THERMAX

MD & CEO Ashish Bhandari explains how Thermax is restructuring around four core segments—industrial products, industrial infrastructure, green solutions, and chemicals—to streamline operations and exit low-margin, high‑civil‑work government projects. The company acknowledges setbacks in bio‑CNG and renewable platform First Energy, citing execution challenges, labor shortages, and supply issues. It emphasizes a shift toward biomass boilers, build‑own‑operate models, and strong international demand, especially from US data centres, Middle East oil & gas, and Africa. With a Rs 14,000 crore backlog and a 30% share of orders coming from abroad, Thermax aims to improve project execution, product capability, and innovation to sustain top‑line and bottom‑line growth
