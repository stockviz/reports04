Succession Crisis at Tata Sons Highlights Governance Gaps

GODREJCP PAISALO GODREJAGRO TENNIND NSIL BCG

Tata Sons chairman N. Chandrasekaran’s decision not to seek reappointment has exposed the group’s lack of a formal succession plan, despite repeated prompts from Noel Tata and the Tata Trusts over the past 19 months. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee, led by Harish Manwani, failed to provide clear answers or conduct a structured process, leading to uncertainty as Chandrasekaran steps down and Noel Tata prepares to exit the board. Parallel leadership changes at Godrej Consumer Products illustrate the benefits of having a ready successor plan. Tata Sons now faces four major challenges: a potential stock‑market listing, an exit strategy for Shapoorji Pallonji’s 18.38% stake, mounting losses in new ventures such as Air India and Tata Digital, and the urgent need to appoint a new chairman. The situation underscores the importance of proactive governance and succession planning in India’s largest conglomerate
