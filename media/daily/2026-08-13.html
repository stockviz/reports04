Cut the BS: Understanding Business Cycles

TATASTEEL INDIGO

The episode explains that business cycles are periods of growth followed by slowdown, affecting profits across industries. Cyclical sectors like metals, real estate, and airlines experience sharp profit swings due to demand, commodity prices, and high fixed costs, while defensive sectors like consumer staples and healthcare are more stable. Operating leverage amplifies profit changes in cyclical firms. Investors should avoid valuing companies at peak earnings and instead monitor capacity additions, inventory, commodity prices, margins, and debt to anticipate cycle shifts. Timing and cycle awareness are crucial for accurate valuation and investment decisions

RBI’s Draft Loan‑Pricing Guidelines: Impact on Banks

The RBI has proposed draft guidelines to make loan pricing more transparent, capping floating‑rate benchmarks to resets no longer than three months. Existing loans may be transitioned into this framework, and public comments are open until Sept 11. While NBFCs are exempt, banks can set PLR and spread components per internal policies, limiting future spread repricing. The rules favor PSU banks, which already align with many of the requirements, whereas private banks may face modest compliance costs and yield caps, especially on high‑yield small tickets. Brokerages hold mixed views, but overall impact is expected to be slightly more positive for PSUs than private lenders
