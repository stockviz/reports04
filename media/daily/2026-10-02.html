VEDL

NCLT Dismisses Uniton Infra’s Insolvency Petition Against Shapoorji Pallonji

AGIIL TCNSBRANDS

The National Company Law Tribunal rejected Uniton Infra’s Section 9 petition to start bankruptcy proceedings against Shapoorji Pallonji & Co over a claimed Rs 4.31 crore default. The tribunal held that one invoice was dated during the COVID‑era ban on filing insolvency cases, and the remaining claim fell short of the Rs 1 crore threshold required to trigger proceedings. Eli Lilly CEO David A. Ricks on Anti‑Obesity Innovation, India’s Biotech Potential, and Future Strategies

TIMESGTY

David A. Ricks highlighted Eli Lilly’s surge to a $1 trillion market cap, driven by blockbuster GLP‑1 drugs like Mounjaro and upcoming candidates such as retatrutide, bimagrumab, and the oral pill Foundayo. He noted strong growth despite patent expiries, emphasizing that generics expand patient access rather than erode demand. Recent trial data showed retatrutide’s record weight‑loss results and a 53 % reduction in cardiovascular mortality for the oral formulation, while a triple‑acting combo (eloralintide + tirzepatide) is in development. Ricks described an acceleration in biotech innovation, aided by AI tools that improve experimental efficiency but are not yet delivering breakthrough molecules outright. Regarding India, he sees a “tremendous” biotech promise, with opportunities in manufacturing partnerships, research collaborations in Bengaluru, and potential acquisitions, though the ecosystem lags behind China. Pricing remains a challenge; Lilly aims for tiered pricing aligned with income levels and has launched the “Lilly Change the Course Commitment” to improve access to obesity therapies in low‑ and middle‑income countries, including India

Vedanta to Invest $200 Million to Boost Rajasthan Oil Output

VOGL

Vedanta Oil and Gas will spend about $200 million in 2026‑27 to enhance production in its northern Rajasthan fields, focusing on the Mangla field to raise output from 80,000 to over 150,000 barrels per day using polymer flooding and other enhanced‑recovery techniques. India’s September Power Demand Hits Record 269 GW Amid Deficit Monsoon

NTPC COALINDIA

In September, India’s electricity consumption surged 11.3% YoY to 162 billion units, driven by cooling and irrigation needs during a weak monsoon, and set a new monthly peak demand of 269 GW. NTPC Ltd produced 38.8 billion units, up 12.5% YoY. Coal India’s total fuel supply rose 12.5% to 61.2 million tonnes, with coal deliveries to power plants increasing 10.6% YoY to 48.9 million tonnes and to non‑regulated users up 19.4%. Coal production grew 9.2% to 53.5 million tonnes, and the company expects further increases post‑monsoon. Coal stocks at power plants fell sharply due to transportation disruptions from rain‑affected coal‑bearing regions. Coal India’s targets for the fiscal year are 815 million tonnes of production and 850 million tonnes of supply
