Oil Supply Tensions and AI Bubble: Market Outlook from David Roche

David Roche explains that Middle‑East oil disruptions are creating a significant supply gap, worsened by reduced Russian exports and a rebound in Chinese oil imports, leaving little buffer for inventories and setting up price pressure in the autumn. He sees no immediate global slowdown to offset demand, as AI‑driven capital investment remains strong. Roche recommends a defensive stance: overweight real assets such as copper, gold (as an inflation hedge against higher oil and food prices), and select Chinese AI equities; remain short the US dollar; and be cautious on equities, especially in the US AI sector.
