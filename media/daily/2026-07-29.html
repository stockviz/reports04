The Importance of Diversification Across Asset Classes and Within Equities

MAXESTATES TV18BRDCST

In a CNBC TV18 “Let’s Talk Money” episode, host Surabhi Upadhyay and Access Mutual Fund’s Chief Business Officer Vandana Trivedi discuss why investors should avoid “putting all eggs in one basket.” They explain that spreading investments across equity, debt, and gold reduces portfolio volatility because these asset classes historically show low or negative correlations, especially over long horizons. While gold’s recent correlation with other assets has drifted toward zero due to central‑bank buying and geopolitical factors, it remains a safe‑haven component (ideally ≤15% of a portfolio). Fixed‑income delivers steady 6‑7% returns and acts as a stabilizer, often topping performance in certain years. A diversified blend of the three can achieve returns close to pure equity (≈12‑13% annually) with 30% lower volatility, leading to fewer “pothole” drawdowns and less emotional stress for investors. The conversation also covers behavioral pitfalls like recency bias and chase‑the‑winner flows, emphasizing that diversification mitigates such biases. Within equity, they advocate a multi‑cap approach—combining large, mid, and small‑cap exposure—since it offers better risk‑adjusted returns and lower intra‑sector correlation than single‑cap or index‑focused portfolios. Multi‑cap funds provide built‑in guardrails, smoother performance, and less need for frequent rebalancing, making them a practical choice for long‑term Indian investors
