India’s Festive Smartphone Sales Face Sharp Decline Amid Rising Prices

RUBICON VIVOBIOT

Online and offline smartphone sales during Navratri have plunged, with unit volumes down 16% YoY and brick‑and‑mortar retailers seeing a 20% drop. Smaller discounts (10‑25% vs 25‑40% last year) and a 35‑40% rise in phone prices—driven by soaring memory‑chip costs, rupee depreciation and freight hikes—are squeezing affordability. Premium phones (>₹45,000) remain relatively resilient, supporting a modest 4% rise in sales value despite falling volumes. Retailers are extending sale periods to clear inventory, while consumers shift toward financing, refurbished devices, or repairs. The downturn is expected to continue through Diwali, with further price hikes likely to pressure sales

Higher Crop Insurance Claims Expected in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh Amid Drought

GICRE

Premium pools have dropped sharply, and total claims will depend on upcoming yield assessments uploaded to the National Crop Insurance Portal. Fairfax Proposes Plan to Resolve IDBI-CSB Bank Ownership Overlap Ahead of RBI Approval

CSBBANK CHOLAHLDNG HDFCLIFE IIFLCAPS IIFL IDBI

Prem Watsa-backed Fairfax Financial Holdings has submitted a regulatory undertaking to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), outlining a two-year glidepath to resolve its overlapping ownership in IDBI Bank and CSB Bank, likely through a merger. The move follows the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs’ approval of Fairfax’s Rs 52,878 crore acquisition of a 60.72% stake in IDBI Bank—the largest cross-border banking deal in India.

Key Points:

  • Ownership Conflict: Fairfax already holds ~40% in CSB Bank and is set to acquire IDBI Bank, creating a regulatory concern as RBI restricts single-entity control over two universal banks (even without explicit legal prohibition).
  • Proposed Solution: A merger of CSB Bank into IDBI Bank would consolidate operations under one license, leveraging IDBI’s larger branch network, deposits, and lending while retaining CSB’s franchise.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Fairfax’s plan aligns with RBI’s discretion under Section 12B of the Banking Regulation Act, which allows scrutiny of substantial shareholdings.
  • Strategic Consolidation: Fairfax is also exploring selling its 13.7% stake in IIFL Finance to fund the IDBI deal and simplify overlapping financial services investments.
  • Background: Earlier attempts to sell CSB Bank shares failed due to undervaluation concerns, making a merger the preferred path.

The development underscores Fairfax’s push to streamline its Indian banking and capital markets portfolio amid regulatory scrutiny

Goldman Sachs Dominates Q3 Equity Underwriting Revenue Amid AI Boom

STANLEY

In the third quarter of 2026, Goldman Sachs led its four largest U.S. equity underwriting rivals in revenue, earning $666 million—a 43% increase year-over-year—thanks to strong AI-driven stock sales (e.g., SK Hynix’s $26.5B IPO, Intel’s $23B offering). Morgan Stanley was the only rival to report a decline (19% drop), missing out on key deals like SK Hynix. Citigroup saw the biggest year-over-year growth (77% to $343M), benefiting from its SK Hynix role.

The five banks collectively earned $2.5B in equity capital markets (ECM) fees—a 13% rise from 2025 but still below the record $3.6B from Q2 (driven by SpaceX’s IPO). AI infrastructure spending (projected at $1T/year) remains a key growth driver, though volatility persists. Anthropic’s potential IPO (led by Morgan Stanley) could set another record. Executives highlighted strong pipelines, with Goldman Sachs’ David Solomon and Citigroup’s Jane Fraser expressing optimism about stable macro conditions
