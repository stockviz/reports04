KEI Industries Q1 Earnings Call Highlights – Capacity Expansion, Margin Guidance, and Export Outlook

POLYCAB KEI

KEI Industries posted a 25% revenue growth in its cable‑and‑wire segment, driven by margin expansion rather than volume, as volumes lagged peers due to limited capacity. The Sanand Phase‑1 plant, commissioned in December, contributed about ₹200 cr in Q1 and is expected to boost revenues further. Management reiterated FY27 volume growth guidance of 17‑18% and a 25% top‑line increase, with margin targets of 11‑12% for cables and wires (up from 10.5‑11%). Phase‑2 of Sanand is on track for March 2027 commissioning, aiming to add ₹3,000 cr to FY27 revenue. The company emphasized ongoing efficiency gains, a robust data‑center order pipeline in the US, and confidence in meeting its growth targets

Chandan Taparia Explains Nifty’s 200‑Point Closing Jump and What Traders Should Do Now

AXISBANK TCS RELIANCE

On the first day of NSE’s new Closing Auction System (CES), participation was low (≈ ₹1.27 trn in regular market vs ₹1.10 trn in the auction). The auction uses the volume‑weighted average price from 3:15 pm to set a reference price, then allows bids within a ±3 % band for a 5‑minute transition (3:20‑3:35 pm). Many traders placed orders at the upper 3 % limit, creating a sharp 200‑point rise in spot indices (e.g., ICICI, Reliance, TCS) while futures remained flat, leading to a price gap between spot and futures. Taparia advises traders to stay cautious: keep positions light in the last 10‑15 minutes, let the market settle near the reference price, and exit option positions before the 3 % band is reached. The spot market is expected to correct toward futures in the next session
