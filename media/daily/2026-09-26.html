India’s Emerging Wealth Creators: Diversification Redefines Investment Strategies

TIMESGTY BCG

India’s growing affluent class is shifting from traditional deposits and domestic equities to a broader mix of assets—including private markets, alternatives, and global opportunities—driven by rising wealth (projected >$2 trillion by 2030). Investors are reassessing portfolios amid interest‑rate changes, geopolitical risks, and market volatility, integrating equities, debt, gold, real estate, private equity, venture capital, and foreign assets for better risk‑adjusted returns. This complexity heightens demand for professional advice from private banks, PMS providers, AIF managers, and wealth advisors. Financial brands can tap this audience through Economic Times platforms such as the Alpha Wealth Summit, leveraging ET’s trusted news reach to engage wealth creators and decision‑makers

Supreme Court Clears Forensic Audit of Fortis‑IHH Deal Involving Singh Brothers

AHCL YESBANK CAPTRUST RELIGARE ALKEM FORTIS AXISBANK

The apex court dismissed Fortis Healthcare’s plea, permitting the Delhi High Court‑appointed forensic auditor to probe transactions among Fortis, IHH Healthcare and the Singh brothers’ RHT Health Trust. The audit, ordered amid a 3,500‑crore arbitration award enforcement by Daiichi Sankyo, will independently trace assets and examine share dilution, pledged‑share sales and potential concealment by former promoters Malvinder and Shivinder Singh. The court noted its earlier observations were tentative and will not bind the auditor, while separate appeals by Yes Bank and Axis Bank will be heard later

India’s Car Sales Surge Thanks to GST Cuts and Rising Incomes

M&M MARUTI HYUNDAI TATAMOTORS

Car prices rose ~3% YoY, but a 9%+ boost in household disposable income and GST reductions (to 18% for sub‑4 m cars and 40% for larger models) kept affordability high. Despite 3‑4% price hikes by manufacturers, cumulative increases stayed under 5%, preserving demand. Sales of lower‑tax vehicles jumped 29% YoY (1.24 m units) and higher‑tax SUVs grew 21% in Apr‑Aug. Tata Motors expects 10% industry growth Oct‑Mar and aims to outpace peers with new products and multi‑powertrain options, while noting a resurgence in hatchback and sedan demand despite a consumer tilt toward SUVs

Tata Cliq Cuts FY26 Net Loss by 19% as Revenue Grows 20

AERONEU BCG TATACAP FRETAIL

Tata Cliq’s parent, Tata UniStore Ltd, reduced its FY26 net loss to ₹253 cr from ₹314 cr, a 19% decline, aided by a 20% rise in revenue to ₹354 cr and an 80% drop in finance costs. Both its Fashion and Luxury segments posted growth of 7% and 26% respectively, improving overall profitability. The company aims to boost contribution margins and monetize further as it pursues aggressive growth targets, positioning itself as a leading omnichannel multi‑brand e‑retail player in India

Vitol Denies Signing Fake Contracts, Prompting Deutsche Bank Fraud Claim

BCG CELLO AXISBANK

Vitol informed Deutsche Bank in early August that contracts and invoices purportedly signed by its CFO for sales to Radiant World were falsified. After verifying with Vitol and Glencore, Deutsche Bank concluded several receivables it bought from Radiant were fraudulent, demanding repayment and seizing funds. Radiant World disputes the allegations, while Vitol, Glencore, and Deutsche Bank declined further comment. The Singapore court placed Radiant under interim judicial management following a petition by Mizuho Bank

CCI Requests Additional Details on Adani‑IntelliSmart Deal

AFCONS AIIL ASIANENE ADANIENSOL

The Competition Commission of India has flagged the proposed ₹3,050‑crore acquisition of IntelliSmart Infrastructure by Adani Energy Solutions as “information incomplete,” seeking further data two months after the July 27 filing. Adani Energy, aiming to become India’s largest smart‑metering platform with a portfolio of over 47 million meters, plans to buy IntelliSmart—a joint venture of NIIF and EESL that operates about 22 million smart meters across five states. The deal, pending antitrust clearance within 180 days, raises CCI concerns over horizontal overlaps in advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) for electricity, and vertical overlaps in smart gas meters and cloud services. Regulators note that response time to such requests does not affect the official review period, and such inquiries are routine

ShareChat’s Recommendation Engine Becomes the Gatekeeper of India’s Micro‑drama Boom

TVVISION

ShareChat co‑founder and CFO Manohar Singh Charan says falling production costs, AI‑driven speed and a ten‑fold rise in micro‑drama volume (especially in China) are flooding the market with similar short‑form serials. With up to 100 crore episodes served daily, the platform’s algorithm now decides which stories users see, influencing viewership, ad revenue and future monetisation. Charan notes that 25% of Moj users are filtered out as uninterested, while the algorithm correctly predicts interest 75% of the time, leading to 90‑95 episodes watched per day for engaged users. The shift from subscription to ad‑supported (AVOD) models could expand daily active users to 140‑150 million by FY32, with ad revenues projected to hit ₹5,000‑₹5,500 crore. ShareChat’s ad revenue already exceeds its cloud costs, making each user profitable. The company envisions “content‑to‑commerce” integrations and sees a potential ₹5,000‑₹6,000 crore advertising opportunity, while remaining cash‑flow positive and not actively seeking new capital

Converse paused all paid and social media advertising following criticism that a recent ad resembled KKK imagery and lynching. The move aims to stem a 14‑quarter sales decline ahead of Nike’s upcoming earnings release
