Foreign Airlines and Adani Airports at Impasse Over Mumbai–Navi Mumbai Shift

GMRAIRPORT INDIGO TCIEXP SKYWAYS

Foreign carriers and Adani Airports failed to reach an agreement on moving international operations from Mumbai’s Terminal 1 to Navi Mumbai Airport. Airlines demand a phased transition, adherence to the Civil Aviation Ministry’s slot guidelines, and a business case for the 50 km shift, while Adani seeks a transition plan by Nov 3 and aims to start T1 demolition in April 2027. Indian airlines have already scheduled moves, and Adani offers slot availability and landing‑charge waivers, but foreign carriers insist on a later timeline (up to Oct 2027), leaving the redevelopment and shift unresolved

Akasa Air Increases Pilot Pay by Up to 15% to Retain Talent

INDIGO TCIEXP SKYWAYS

Akasa Air will raise the monthly salaries of pilots across all eight grades by up to 15%, effective Jan 1 2027, to keep its crew amid rapid expansion. The carrier, now operating 45 aircraft with plans to add eight more this fiscal year and 181 on order, is backed by investors including the Jhunjhunwala family, Azim Premji’s family office, and Ranjan Pai. Despite high fuel costs, Indian airlines are gearing up for a wave of new aircraft deliveries. Akasa emphasizes retaining existing pilots and recruiting new ones, noting the lengthy training required—six months for trainees to become first officers and a minimum of 1,500 flight hours before promotion to captain. The DGCA mandates at least three pilots per aircraft, with higher staffing needs for high‑utilisation carriers like IndiGo

Bandhan Bank Faces Rs 200 Cr Hit from Proposed Insurance Commission Cut

BANDHANBNK

Bandhan Bank may lose about Rs 200 crore in annual income due to a proposed IRDA rule lowering commissions on third‑party insurance distribution. To offset the impact, the bank will shift focus to mutual fund distribution and introduce new product areas. In Q2, it earned Rs 156 crore from third‑party products, mainly insurance. The bank also noted minimal exposure to drought‑prone Karnataka and Maharashtra, with only 1% and 2.6% of its unsecured emerging‑enterprise business respectively, posing no significant risk

Power Grid Calls on Cable Makers to Ramp Up Production as Transmission Capex Soars

HAVELLS POWERGRID

State‑owned Power Grid plans to exceed its FY26 transmission spend of ₹40,000 cr, with a higher capex likely this year. Chairman Vamsi Rama Mohan Burra urged wire and cable manufacturers to boost capacity, improve quality, and meet tight timelines, citing rapid growth in renewable energy, urbanisation, data centres, underground/submarine cables, and the Green Energy Corridor‑III (≈₹1.86 lakh cr) as key demand drivers. The transmission sector, responsible for adding ~5,000 km of lines and 72,000 MVA of transformation capacity last year, is a major consumer for the >₹1 lakh cr Indian wires‑cables industry, with some firms deriving up to a third of sales from it. The need to synchronize transmission development with renewable generation, especially solar projects with high cable usage, was emphasized

Apple Unveils Touch‑Screen MacBook Pro with OLED and Dynamic Island

Apple has introduced new 14‑ and 16‑inch MacBook Pro models featuring the company’s first touch‑screen, OLED displays and a Dynamic Island UI, powered by M5 Pro and M5 Max chips. Weighing less than previous versions, the laptops start at $2,899 (14‑inch) and $3,399 (16‑inch), with a fully specced variant costing $14,549. The move overturns Steve Jobs’ long‑standing opposition to touch‑enabled Macs and follows a leadership change to CEO John Ternus. Apple hopes the premium features will boost revenue despite a broader laptop market slowdown and rising component costs

DOJ Probes Nvidia’s $12.9B Hugging Face Acquisition Amid Broader AI Antitrust Scrutiny

The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) is investigating Nvidia’s $12.93 billion deal to acquire Hugging Face, an open-source AI platform, over concerns it could stifle competition in the AI sector. The probe—part of a longer-running antitrust inquiry into Nvidia’s business practices—examines whether the acquisition would let Nvidia control a critical AI distribution point, potentially harming rivals like AMD, Google, and Broadcom.

Key details: - DOJ’s focus: Nvidia’s acquisitions (e.g., SchedMD, Groq, Run:ai), sales tactics, and influence over customers. - Hugging Face deal: Expected to close in H1 2027; Nvidia claims it will maintain open access. - Broader probe: Includes scrutiny of Nvidia’s licensing deals (e.g., Groq) and whether it bundles products to dominate markets. - Political context: Investigation spans Biden and Trump administrations; no evidence of wrongdoing yet. - Competitors’ pushback: AMD, Google, and Broadcom are challenging Nvidia’s AI chip monopoly with specialized hardware.

The DOJ’s inquiry—reportedly the first public mention—could reshape Nvidia’s expansion strategy and set a precedent for AI industry consolidation. Nvidia and Hugging Face declined to comment
