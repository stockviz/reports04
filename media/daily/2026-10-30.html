Supreme Court Hears Reliance’s Appeals Over $1.73 Billion ONGC Gas Dispute

RELIANCE ONGC

The Supreme Court is hearing three appeals filed by Reliance Industries and partners challenging a Delhi High Court ruling that overturned a 2018 arbitration award in a $1.73 billion dispute with state‑run Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC). The case centers on alleged migration of gas from ONGC’s adjoining blocks into Reliance’s Krishna‑Godavari basin fields. The High Court deemed the arbitration award contrary to legal principles, accusing the consortium of “insidious fraud” and “unjust enrichment” and ordered a demand notice for $2.81 billion. The dispute, dating back to 2016, involves claims that the consortium extracted and sold migrated gas, which a 2018 tribunal had ruled was permissible. After the High Court set aside that award in February 2025, Reliance is now seeking reversal at the Supreme Court
