India’s hospitality sector plans to add 161,074 branded rooms over the next five years—about 77% of the current inventory—with roughly 80% of new rooms slated for tier‑2 and tier‑3 markets such as Tirupati and Varanasi. Despite recent travel disruptions, FY26 saw steady occupancy (68.1%), a 6.4% rise in ADR to ₹8,962, and a 6.2% increase in RevPAR to ₹6,099. Delhi NCR is poised as a hotspot, being the only major market to exceed 80% occupancy in both FY25 and FY26. While RevPAR growth slowed, four major markets (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad) still drove national performance, with Bengaluru leading RevPAR growth at 14.8% and remaining the largest branded hotel market

Luxury Car GST Benefits Erode as Prices Rise

AZADIND LANDMARK BMWVENTLTD

A cut in GST for luxury vehicles (from 48‑50% to 40%) initially lowered prices, but successive hikes by Mercedes‑Benz, BMW, Audi and others—driven by a weaker rupee, higher import costs and logistics expenses—have wiped out about five percentage points of the tax relief. Luxury sales are projected to grow 11% to ~58,000 units in 2026, still lagging behind the broader passenger‑car market’s 13‑14% growth. Automakers are absorbing much of the cost pressure and offering lower EMIs, but further price increases are expected

OBT Sports & Entertainment Partners with World Chess Champion D Gukesh

TIMESGTY TOYAMSL

OBT Sports & Entertainment Pvt. Ltd. has entered an exclusive partnership with reigning World Chess Champion D Gukesh to manage his commercial ventures and global brand building. OBT will handle endorsements, commercial strategy, career and brand advisory, content collaborations, and intellectual property, focusing on long‑term, values‑aligned partnerships in India and abroad. Gukesh expressed interest in meaningful, authentic, and sustainable opportunities beyond chess, while OBT’s Head of Talent & Servicing, Yash Kharote, highlighted the potential to elevate Gukesh’s global profile through authentic collaborations, leveraging OBT’s expertise in talent management, brand partnerships, and public relations
