State‑run General Insurers to Submit Turnaround Plans

NIACL HDFCLIFE NATIONALUM

The government has asked the four public‑sector general insurers—Oriental, United India, National and New India Assurance—to present board‑approved plans for scaling back or exiting loss‑making segments. Despite New India Assurance’s recent profit, it reported a ₹1,267 crore loss in Q2 FY27. A parliamentary committee urged a time‑bound roadmap to achieve organic solvency, improve underwriting, technology, reinsurance, and linkages with state banks, and curb fraud via AI and digital inspections. Solvency ratios of three insurers remain below IRDAI norms, prompting quarterly capital reviews and stricter KPI monitoring

India’s Spirits Market Slows, Mass Brands Feel Inflation Pressure

MCDOWELL-N ABDL

Growth in India’s spirits market decelerated to 4.6% YoY in the September quarter, down from 6.4% a year earlier, with the mass‑market segment hit hardest by inflation and weaker consumer sentiment. Premium and prestige spirits grew faster (5.7% vs. 3.7% for mass brands). Executives cite lingering West Asia tensions, soft monsoons, and policy headwinds in states like Maharashtra and Karnataka as contributing factors, while lower‑income consumers are feeling budget strain. United Spirits saw a 6.3% rise in premium sales but slower overall growth, and Allied Blenders posted an 11.1% volume increase to 10 million cases

Gulf Oil Lubricants Expands Globally, Eyes Acquisitions and IPO Ahead of 2027 Push

DELTACORP GULFOILLUB HGS

Gulf Oil Lubricants, owned by the Hinduja Group, is accelerating its international expansion, targeting a return to Mexico (2027)—where the Gulf brand originated—and a U.S. re-entry via a master-licensing deal covering 11 states. The company, which was excluded from the U.S. market post-Chevron acquisition in 1984, is also scouting acquisitions in South America (Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay, Bolivia) and Southeast Asia (Vietnam) to bolster its global footprint.

A potential global listing for Gulf Oil International is planned within 3–5 years, though details remain undecided. The group’s marine lubricants business—now the world’s second-largest—is a key growth driver, serving over 1,000 ports globally.

In India, Gulf Oil Lubricants India (listed, ₹5,113 crore market cap) reported a 34.48% YoY net profit jump (Q2 FY27: ₹117 crore) and 44% revenue growth (₹1,379 crore), with plans to invest ₹55 crore to expand manufacturing capacity by 70% at its Silvassa and Chennai plants. Leadership emphasizes ongoing expansion, acquisitions, and growth before pursuing a public offering

McDonald’s Boosts North‑East India Investment to $80 Million Amid Rising Costs

BCG 3MINDIA DELTACORP HINDUNILVR PLAZACABLE DEVYANI BURGERKING

McDonald’s India (North & East) has raised its planned investment to about $80 million over the next 2.5 years for store expansion and digitisation, targeting 500 outlets by 2028. Andhra Pradesh Secures Rs 1,614 crore from Hyundai‑Kia Suppliers, Outpacing Tamil Nadu

HYUNDAI

Three South Korean auto‑component makers—Hwashin (₹850 cr, 100 jobs), Seoyon (₹514 cr, 918 jobs) and Iwamet (₹250 cr, 400 jobs)—have signed MoUs with Andhra Pradesh to set up plants in Anantapur, creating a total of 1,418 jobs. Reliance Retail Targets Higher Margins as E‑commerce Investments Pay Off

RCOM

Reliance Retail’s CEO Jeyandran Venugopal said the company expects EBITDA margins to improve from the next fiscal year once its current wave of spending on dark‑stores, quick‑commerce capabilities and store remodels begins to bear fruit. The push has pressured profit, with net profit falling 24.5% YoY in the September quarter after declines in the prior two quarters, but its digital commerce arm grew 42% YoY, driven by higher order values and volumes at JioMart. The retailer plans to continue expanding its online footprint while pursuing growth in a “measured and meaningful” way rather than matching competitors’ heavy discount spending

AB InBev Sets Aside $30 Million for Telangana Dues Amid Brewing Industry Payment Delays

UBL

AB InBev has provisioned $30 million for unpaid dues from the Telangana government, reflecting broader challenges in India’s state‑controlled alcohol market where delayed payments and price caps strain brewers. Telangana owes roughly $400 million to alcohol companies, and manufacturers rely on government depots for distribution, leaving them exposed to payment delays. CFO Bernardo Novick said the provision reflects outstanding receivables, but the company expects no further one‑off charges. United Breweries faced similar issues, halting supplies to the state’s beverage corporation after operating losses and overdue payments caused it to lose market share

India’s Pharma Sales Surge on Chronic‑Care Demand

MANKIND INDOCO CIPLA SPARC ERIS SYSTMTXC TORNTPHARM

Medicine sales in India have posted double‑digit growth for four consecutive quarters, driven by rising demand for chronic‑care drugs targeting heart disease, hypertension and diabetes. PharmaTrac data shows a 12% YoY rise to Rs 70,180 crore in the September quarter, up from 11% and 10% in the prior two quarters. Factors include shutdowns of non‑compliant small‑medium units shifting sales to larger firms, a GST cut on medicines from 12% to 5%, and greater health awareness boosting multivitamin and supplement sales. Major players reported strong gains: Mankind Pharma (+15% to Rs 3,657 crore), Torrent Pharma (+20% to Rs 2,181 crore) and Sun Pharma (+17.3% to Rs 5,552 crore). Experts note growth is concentrated in chronic‑therapy segments (cardiology, diabetology, oncology) while anti‑infectives lag, and suggest the trend may continue for the next 2‑3 years

Julius Baer CEO flags AI bubble as top market risk, banks safe, India’s wealth shift

Stefan Bollinger says the greatest threat to global markets is an AI‑driven bubble, not banking, noting that banks remain “very safe.” He observes India’s equity valuations have corrected to 16‑16.5 × earnings, keeping its growth story intact. Wealthy Indians are diversifying offshore, with foreign assets rising from 2‑5 % to 15‑20 % of wealth, favoring hedge funds, private‑equity deals with sector focus, private credit and structured products that limit downside. On the US Fed, Bollinger notes limited appetite for another rate hike before the midterms and that a December hike is already priced in. He sees the next crisis more likely from off‑balance‑sheet AI exposures than banking failures. Rising yields could strain pension funds or insurers, but not pose systemic risk; higher rates are also prompting Japanese investors to repatriate capital

DGCA Requires Two‑Pilot Crews for Char Dham Helicopter Flights, Operators Threaten Service Halt

INDIGO

The Directorate General of Civil Aviation has mandated that all charter helicopters on Char Dham routes must carry a qualified co‑pilot from November 2, after a fatal crash in Uttarakhand. Operators must also obtain destination and en‑route clearances from Sahastradhara control and submit post‑flight pilot reports. Eleven operators warned they may suspend services, citing the added crew and logistical costs as commercially unviable. The measures were announced following a meeting with operators and state aviation officials in response to the October 30 crash that killed six people
