Navi Mumbai Airport Offers Full Waiver on International Landing Charges for First Year

ABMINTLLTD BCG TCIEXP

Navi Mumbai International Airport will waive 100% of landing charges for international flights for the first year of operations, as approved by the Airports Economic Regulatory Authority (AERA) on September 8, 2026. The move aims to attract and sustain international routes from the new airport.

Key incentives include: - First year: Full waiver (100%) for all international flights. - Second year: 50% discount for short-haul (≤5,000 km) and long-haul routes. - Third year: 25% discount for long-haul flights only. - Additional frequencies: 50% discount for short-haul and 75% for long-haul in the first year. - Freighters: 90% waiver in the first year, dropping to 50% in the second year.

The eligibility criteria have also been revised—new international routes from Navi Mumbai (not just the Mumbai Metropolitan Region) now qualify, even if the same route exists from Mumbai Airport. The airport, inaugurated in October 2025 and operational since December 25, 2025, launched international services on July 15, 2026, with an Air India Express flight to Abu Dhabi.
