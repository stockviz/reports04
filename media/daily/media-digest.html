Brookfield’s ₹2,500 Cr Bikaner Solar Portfolio Attracts Inox Clean Energy and Purvah Green Power

AGL BCG GAJA IWEL ATHERENERG BHAGYAPROP BORORENEW IREDA IOC HINDUNILVR GREENPOWER RPSGVENT

Domestic renewable firms Inox Clean Energy and RP‑Sanjiv Goenka‑owned Purvah Green Power are leading bids to buy Brookfield Asset Management’s 550 MW Bikaner renewable portfolio, valued at roughly ₹2,500 crore. The IFC‑backed project serves C&I customers, including Hindustan Unilever (45 MW PPA) and Brookfield Properties’ campuses in Gurugram and Noida. Earlier reports listed other interested parties such as Actis, InoxGFL, IndiGrid, RPSG, Jindal Renewable and Blueleaf Energy. Brookfield, which declined comment, is reshuffling its Indian assets after selling a 1.6 GW portfolio to Gentari Renewables. The Bikaner assets were acquired via Brookfield’s Global Transition Fund I, part of its $5 bn renewable AUM in India and $32 bn total Indian AUM across sectors

IRDAI penalizes ICICI Lombard Rs 1 crore for outsourcing and vendor‑management lapses

ICICIGI

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India imposed a Rs 1 crore fine on ICICI Lombard General Insurance for failing to properly classify and report outsourcing activities. During a 2019 inspection, the insurer was found to have used agents of other insurers for event‑management services without adequate documentation, omitting these expenses from its outsourcing returns and thereby evading regulatory scrutiny. The penalty relates to Rs 709.57 crore spent on “sales, marketing and business support” in FY19, including Rs 35‑37 crore paid to external agents

Supreme Court Dismisses GST Claim Against Vodafone Idea Over Tower Sale

SHYAMTEL IDEA

The Supreme Court upheld the Bombay High Court’s decision to quash a ₹363 crore GST demand against Vodafone Idea (Vi), ruling that the tax proceedings were filed against Vodafone Mobile Services (VMS)—a non‑existent entity after the 2018 merger of Vodafone India and Idea Cellular. The court held the case lacked jurisdiction and was void ab initio, rejecting the government’s argument that GST provisions differ from income‑tax rules in handling mergers

Private Banks’ Profit Margins Recover as FCNR(B) Inflows Ease Funding Pressure

CARERATING

Indian private banks are seeing improved profit margins after a “deposit war,” with fresh lending spreads widening to 3.13% in July—up 22 basis points month‑on‑month—thanks to a surge in foreign currency non‑resident (FCNR(B)) inflows that reduced reliance on costly wholesale funding. Private lenders cut fresh deposit rates by 25 basis points to 5.96% while lending rates stayed sticky, whereas public sector banks saw narrower spreads. Analysts caution that sustaining these margins may be challenging once FCNR(B) inflows fade, given ongoing competition for deposits and potential pressures from higher bond yields and deposit repricing. System‑wide, fresh spreads rose to 2.62% as deposit growth slowed relative to credit growth, though the credit‑deposit gap narrowed

Amazon Cargo Jet Overruns Miami Runway, Killing Five

SKYWAYS

Federal investigators are probing the Miami International Airport crash of an Amazon Prime Air Boeing 767 that overran the runway after landing late, striking vehicles and catching fire, killing five and injuring several. The NTSB will examine touchdown point, speed, braking, weather, wind, mechanical condition, and maintenance history, including possible tailwind and lack of an arresting system. The incident halted flights, caused numerous cancellations and delays, and raised safety concerns about runway overruns

Fairfax to Exit IIFL Finance, Using Proceeds for IDBI Bank Takeover

Fairfax Financial plans to sell its remaining ~13.7% stake in IIFL Finance (worth ~₹3,800 crore) to fund its bid for a 60.7% stake in state‑owned IDBI Bank. Alternative Powertrains Overtake Petrol in India’s August Auto Sales

TENNIND

Retail vehicle sales jumped 18% to 2.42 million units in August, with CNG (25.28%), hybrid (9.04%) and electric (7.63%) collectively capturing 41.95% of passenger‑vehicle sales—surpassing petrol’s 40.85% for the first time. Strong rural demand drove growth across all segments despite a 13% monsoon deficit. EV sales rose 53% to 298,448 units, lifting overall EV penetration to 12.3%. Dealers remain cautiously optimistic ahead of the festive season, though concerns linger over monsoon‑linked rural demand and upcoming price hikes
