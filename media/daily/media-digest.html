Canada Pension Plan Investments Commits $215 Million to NIIF Infrastructure Fund II

ASTRON AIIL

CPP Investments has pledged about ₹2,070 crore ($215 million) to the National Investment and Infrastructure Fund’s second India‑focused infrastructure fund. The fund will target assets in urbanisation, digitalisation and energy transition sectors. CPP’s James Bryce highlighted the partnership’s success and the aim for long‑term, risk‑adjusted returns, while NIIF’s Sanjiv Aggarwal praised the deepening relationship and plans to deploy capital at scale for India’s infrastructure growth

Income-Tax Department Raids Dentsu Media Group Over Alleged Tax Evasion in India

CYBERMEDIA PARACABLES AEGISCHEM PRESSMN BCG MUFTI ONEPOINT

The Income-Tax Department (I-T) conducted searches at multiple premises of Dentsu Media Group across Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, and Bengaluru as part of a suspected tax evasion probe. The raids targeted 20 entities, including key subsidiaries like Dentsu Aegis Network India, Dentsu Advertising, and Dentsu Media India, as well as some amalgamated units.

In its 2025 annual report, Dentsu disclosed an investigation into unverified claims of ₹522.8 crore for goods/services allegedly provided to an Indian subsidiary. The group rejected the claims, arguing no legitimate transactions occurred, and stated it was cooperating with authorities while defending its position. Dentsu did not immediately comment on the raids

Blackstone Exits EPL with Full 26.38% Stake Sale for ₹2,032 Cr

BMWVENTLTD 3MINDIA BCG EPL

Blackstone’s affiliate Epsilon Bidco Pte Ltd sold its entire 26.38% holding in EPL Ltd (formerly Essel Propack) — 8.44 crore shares — at an average of ₹240.57, netting ₹2,032.33 crore. Domestic mutual funds, insurers and foreign investors bought about 3.37 crore shares (10.52% of the company). Quant Mutual Fund was the largest buyer, increasing its stake to 6.95%. Canadian Public Sector Pension Investment Board and Abu Dhabi Investment Authority also participated. EPL’s shares fell 8.47% to ₹239.90. The sale precedes EPL’s planned merger with Indovida India, which will create a $1 bn revenue entity valued at $2 bn, giving Indorama Ventures a 51.8% stake and Blackstone a 16.6% stake in the merged firm

Permira to Acquire 25.71% Stake in Cloudnine Hospitals Parent

3MINDIA BLUESTONE VIJAYA APOLLOHOSP 5PAISA ASIANHOTNR HALEOSLABS

London‑based private‑equity firm Permira will buy a 25.71% stake in Kids Clinic India Ltd (parent of Cloudnine Hospitals) via Luxembourg‑incorporated NuageLux BidCo, valuing the chain at roughly ₹11,000 crore. The deal, outbidding TPG Capital, involves acquiring the stake held by True North Capital and is pending Competition Commission of India approval. A minor indirect link exists between a Permira‑controlled pharma entity and the target’s healthcare services. KCIL also plans to acquire Apollo Specialty Hospitals and Apollo Fertility Centre, subject to the same regulatory clearance
