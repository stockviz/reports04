DRAGARWQ

Allahabad High Court Bars Forceful Vehicle Repossession by Finance Firms

JPASSOCIAT HDBFS

The court ruled that a finance company’s right to repossess a financed vehicle must be exercised through legal forums—not by goons or armed agents. In a case where a borrower’s truck was seized at gunpoint after paying 31 of 45 installments, the bench ordered the vehicle’s immediate return, imposed a ₹10,000 cost, and directed the lender to restructure the loan without penal interest, leaving any default issue for adjudication by a competent authority.
