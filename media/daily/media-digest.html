Johnson & Johnson Settles Major Talc Lawsuits for $5.5 Billion

PRSMJOHNSN

Johnson & Johnson announced a $5.5 billion settlement that resolves about 69,000 federal cases and related state lawsuits—covering 99.75% of remaining talc claims—alleging its baby powder caused ovarian cancer. The deal follows a recent court ruling questioning plaintiffs’ ability to prove causation. J&J, which stopped selling talc‑based baby powder in the U.S. in 2020, has denied the claims, asserting its talc is safe and asbestos‑free. The litigation had been paused since 2022 during J&J’s unsuccessful attempts to resolve the suits via a shell company bankruptcy

PayU Expands Into UPI Credit and Infrastructure Ownership

ARIS MOBIKWIK

PayU CEO Anirban Mukherjee says the fintech is eyeing UPI‑based credit lines—potentially via a bank partnership or, if RBI permits, directly through its NBFC—offering flexible pre‑sanctioned limits to consumers.
