User Development Fee Cuts at Hyderabad and Bengaluru Airports: Benefits for Passengers

INDIGO

India’s Airport Economic Regulatory Authority (ERA) has slashed the User Development Fee (UDF) for domestic passengers at Hyderabad airport, following a similar reduction at Bengaluru. Under a new tariff framework, UDFs are being lowered because airports will now recover infrastructure costs only after projects are completed and usable, reducing the risk of over‑recovery and easing the financial burden on travelers. Bengaluru’s domestic UDF dropped from ₹550 to ₹300, and Hyderabad’s from ₹750 to ₹515, with corresponding cuts for international departures. Future infrastructure investments remain planned, but additional tariffs will apply only when new assets, such as Hyderabad’s northern runway, are operational. This shift aims to protect passengers from paying for unavailable facilities and is welcomed by airlines and consumers alike
