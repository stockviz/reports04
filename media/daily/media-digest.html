India’s Ad Spend Growth in High Single Digits Amid Cautious Festive Optimism

HTMEDIA

India’s advertising expenditure is projected to grow in the high single digits in 2026, driven by cautious optimism among advertisers despite lingering economic uncertainties. While the festive season—key for sectors like automobiles, electronics, beauty, and home decor—is expected to match or exceed last year’s spending (boosted by GST cuts), external factors like the aftereffects of the Gulf war and approval delays are tempering enthusiasm.

A shift in ad spending is notable: celebrity-driven campaigns are declining, while investments in creators, quick commerce (food, beauty, clothing), and Connected TV (CTV) are rising. Agencies now face pressure to solve business challenges beyond cost efficiency, including future-proofing strategies.

Critically, ad spend as a % of GDP (0.5%) is low, but ad spend per capita is the more relevant metric. India’s 50–60 million affluent consumers and 100–140 million middle-income buyers drive demand, though ads often target audiences beyond their purchasing power (e.g., Rs 2 shampoo sachets). Frequency capping on platforms and content-commerce integration remain challenges.

Publicis Media led India’s H1 2026 new business rankings ($218M total, $157M net), reflecting the industry’s evolving dynamics

AI Data Center Investments Confront Cost, Cash‑Flow, and Chip‑Obsolescence Risks

POWERGRID

Private credit lenders like Blackstone have poured billions into “neocloud” AI data center operators, betting on double‑digit yields and equity upside. Recent setbacks—Firmus Grid’s failed $30 billion IPO and AirTrunk’s delayed REIT listing—highlight investors’ growing doubts about the sector’s ability to generate shareholder value. Massive capex (up to $30‑$39 billion) to build gigawatts of capacity, weak free‑cash flow, and the rapid depreciation of expensive processing chips create a precarious mix of high costs, cash‑flow strain, and technology‑obsolescence risk that threatens the profitability of AI data center investments
