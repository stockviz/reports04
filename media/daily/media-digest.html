India’s Commercial Vehicle Market: Heavy Trucks & EVs Surge, Buses Lag in 2026 Growth

SMLMAH EICHERMOT JBMA HGS TATAMOTORS AZADIND M&M ASHOKLEY FORCEMOT

India’s commercial vehicle (CV) market is experiencing a dual growth trend—conventional heavy trucks and electric CVs are booming, while buses trail behind, reflecting shifting demands in infrastructure, logistics, and urban mobility.

Key Highlights:

  1. Freight Truck Boom:
    • Goods-carrier registrations jumped 21.4% (Jan–Oct 2026) to 9.05 lakh, driven by infrastructure, mining, and freight needs.
    • Heavy trucks saw a 28.95% rise in October, while medium CVs grew 21.16%.
    • Dealers expect 50.41% of CVs to see further growth in Nov–Jan 2026–27.
  2. Electric CVs Accelerate:
    • Electric CV registrations surged 162.3% in October to 4,942 units (4.29% of total CV sales).
    • Tata Motors (35.2% share), Euler Motors (25.7%), and Mahindra Last Mile Mobility (10.9%) dominated, capturing 72% of the market.
  3. Bus Segment Struggles:
    • Bus registrations grew only 1.8% to 1.13 lakh, with Ashok Leyland’s bus volumes declining 11.3% in October.
    • Tata Motors led bus sales (36,656 units), but overall demand remains weak.
  4. Manufacturer Strategies:
    • Ashok Leyland expanded its cargo portfolio to compete with Tata, with medium/heavy truck dispatches up 40.6%.
    • Tata Motors retained leadership in CVs (34.67% market share) and buses, with wholesale dispatches up 27% (backed by 225% growth in international sales).
    • Mahindra saw 82% dispatch growth in trucks/buses but faced 3.7% domestic sales decline in SML Mahindra.
    • Volvo Eicher reported 34.9% dispatch growth in CVs but declining bus volumes.
  5. Market Dynamics:
    • Heavy trucks thrive on infrastructure and industrial freight.
    • Light CVs face uneven demand, with a 9.57% decline in October.
    • Electric CVs gain traction in urban logistics, though adoption remains niche.
    • Diversified portfolios (e.g., Tata, Ashok Leyland) are better positioned to navigate fluctuating demand.

Outlook:

The CV market is operating at “three speeds”—heavy trucks leading, smaller cargo vehicles fluctuating, and EVs making inroads. Manufacturers with broad product lines are adapting to these shifts, while bus demand remains sluggish
