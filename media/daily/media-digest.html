Apple Cuts 200+ Jobs, Refocuses on AI and Smart Glasses

Apple is eliminating more than 200 positions across its Siri, Vision Pro, and Intelligent Systems Experience teams as it pivots toward a new AI‑driven Siri, broader AI integration, and upcoming smart‑glass hardware. The restructuring scales back immersive‑video and gaming work on Vision Pro, keeps the headset in the lineup, and creates new roles to support the revised priorities

China’s Largest Automotive Recall Over Door‑Handle Safety

Tesla and eight other automakers will recall about 4.3 million vehicles in China due to emergency door‑handle designs that can be hard to locate or operate after a crash. Remedies include warning labels, software updates that automatically lower windows, and improved handle markings. The recall covers 2.98 million Tesla Model 3, Y, S and X vehicles, plus hundreds of thousands from Xiaomi, Leapmotor, Xpeng, Geely and others. The move follows heightened Chinese safety oversight and a planned ban on concealed door handles from 2027, after incidents of occupants being trapped in burning cars. Modern over‑the‑air fixes are expected to keep costs low, though U.S. regulators have not announced a similar recall

TikTok Agrees to $400 Million Settlement Over Child Privacy Violations

TikTok and its parent ByteDance have reached a $400 million settlement with the U.S. Justice Department to resolve claims that the app illegally collected personal data from users under 13 without parental consent. The deal includes an immediate $300 million payment and an additional $100 million after a prior FTC consent decree is vacated. The settlement follows earlier FTC action against TikTok’s predecessor Musical.ly, which paid $5.7 million for similar violations. The agreement highlights TikTok’s new age‑verification measures, increased under‑age moderation staffing, and its recent restructuring—including a majority‑U.S. owned joint venture—to address privacy concerns. The DOJ emphasizes the settlement’s role in protecting children online while avoiding prolonged litigation

Gov. Newsom Open to Settlement of Paramount‑Warner Merger Lawsuit

LGBBROSLTD PARACABLES

California Governor Gavin Newsom said he would favor a settlement of the state attorney‑general’s antitrust suit against Paramount Skydance’s $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, provided the deal proves beneficial for California. The merger has cleared regulators in 68 jurisdictions, but California, led by AG Rob Bonta, argues it could harm film‑TV distribution and cut jobs. Newsom warned that blocking the deal might trigger Paramount CEO David Ellison to relocate the company out of California, potentially shifting many studio jobs elsewhere. Democrat Xavier Becerra also urged a settlement, calling for both sides to act as adults. Newsom emphasized his concern for the state’s reputation and the future of Hollywood

Sabyasachi Expands into Fragrances, Footwear and Small Leather Goods

Sabyasachi Mukherjee announced that his luxury label, which posted ₹650 crore revenue in FY2026, will launch fragrances within 18 months and roll out small leather goods, footwear, and an expanded eyewear line. Banks Ride RBI Swap Wave to Raise $12 bn via Overseas Debt

HDFCBANK ICICIBANK IDFCFIRSTB BANKBARODA KOTAKBANK

Indian banks have tapped RBI’s one‑time swap facility to raise $12 bn abroad this year, with $4.4 bn issued in a single week by major lenders such as HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, IDFC First and Bank of Baroda. The funds, largely for FCNR‑B deposits before the Aug 31 deadline, are being used to support foreign‑currency client leverage. Despite the surge, bond spreads have widened by only about 5 bps, and deals have been oversubscribed nearly 3‑times on average. HSBC and MUFG expect further issuance of $5‑7.5 bn by year‑end, though supply may taper after the swap windows close in August and December

Counterpoint Research forecasts that Samsung will achieve modest 0.8% growth in 2026, outpacing Apple and becoming the world’s leading smartphone vendor despite a projected 14.3% global shipment decline.
