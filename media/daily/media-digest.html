ChrysCapital Acquires Controlling Stake in Novartis India

DAMCAPITAL NOVARTIND KOTAKBANK SHARDUL TCNSBRANDS

Private equity firm ChrysCapital has purchased a 70.68% stake in Novartis India Limited from its Swiss parent for about ₹1,446 crore, marking its first majority‑controlled pharma investment. The deal aims to build a leading branded‑generics platform in India, leveraging NIL’s existing brands (Voveran, Calcium Sandoz, Tegrital) across several therapeutic areas. A new corporate identity and leadership team, headed by CEO Dr Vikas Gupta, have been appointed to drive growth. Advisors included Kotak, Freshfields, AZB & Partners, EY, Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas, Alvarez & Marsal, Dhruva Advisors, and PwC

VinFast’s India Surge Fueled by Captive Ride‑Hailing Fleet

OLAELEC TATAMOTORS M&M HYUNDAI

VinFast has become India’s fifth‑largest EV maker in its first year, thanks largely to sales to GreenSM, a ride‑hailing service owned by its promoter. Between April and July, GreenSM bought 21‑33% of monthly units, accounting for about 27% of VinFast’s 5,178 vehicles sold in FY27, keeping the company ahead of Hyundai, Kia and BYD. The strategy mirrors VinFast’s Vietnam playbook, where fleet sales initially drove volume but raised investor concerns over demand concentration. While the captive fleet boost has been critical, VinFast stresses a long‑term focus on building a premium brand for private buyers and expects a balanced demand mix from consumers, fleets and mobility providers as India’s EV market matures

ChrysCapital Takes Control of Novartis India, Names New CEO Vikas Gupta

TCNSBRANDS DAMCAPITAL NOVARTIND KOTAKBANK

Private equity firm ChrysCapital has acquired a 70.68% stake in Novartis India Ltd for roughly ₹1,446 crore, making it the first majority‑controlled pharma investment for the firm. Vikas Gupta was appointed CEO and MD, and three independent directors joined the board. The deal, announced earlier this year, separates the listed company from Novartis AG, which will continue its Indian operations through wholly‑owned Novartis Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. ChrysCapital aims to build a leading branded‑generics platform leveraging NIL’s legacy brands across pain management, calcium, gynecology, neurosciences and transplant immunology. Advisors included Kotak, EY, Alvarez & Marsal, Dhruva Advisors and PwC

Meta Q2 Earnings Miss Expectations Amid Legal Costs and Layoffs

TANLA

Meta Platforms posted Q2 revenue of $60.8 billion, up 28% YoY, but earnings fell 14% to $15.85 billion ($6.18 EPS) due to $2.4 billion in legal expenses and $1.18 billion in severance costs. Qualcomm Forecasts Lower Q4 Profit and Accelerating Apple Revenue Decline

Qualcomm projects fourth‑quarter adjusted earnings of $2.05‑$2.25 per share, below expectations, and revenue of $9.7‑$10.5 billion. CEO Cristiano Amon says rising supply‑chain costs and tighter Apple supply constraints will force a price increase starting Sept. 1 and a faster drop in Apple‑related sales, with the company shifting focus toward data‑center chips. Revenue from Chinese handset makers has bottomed, but handset pricing pressures and inventory issues hurt margins. The firm expects most 2027 chip sales to come from non‑smartphone categories

Airbus Expands A400M Firefighting Role Amid European Wildfires

TCNSBRANDS

Airbus is accelerating work on its A400M military transport to serve as a dedicated firefighting platform for France and Spain, while also leveraging its satellite, drone, plane, and helicopter assets for fire detection and suppression. Seven NATO nations have pledged to create a multinational A400M fleet, reflecting growing demand for integrated aerial and space‑based solutions as Europe faces increasingly severe wildfire seasons
