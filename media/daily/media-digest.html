Trinidad‑Tobago Plans to Process Venezuelan Crude at Reopened Guaracara Refinery with Indian Partners

IOC CHEVIOT BPCL OIL ONGC RELIANCE

Trinidad and Tobago is negotiating with Indian state‑run ONGC Videsh and Oil India to process Venezuelan oil at the 165,000 bpd Guaracara refinery, which is being revived after a seven‑year shutdown. The move aligns with India’s search for diversified energy supplies amid global market volatility and could boost Trinidad’s economy. Feasibility depends on technical compatibility, logistics, pricing, financing and U.S. sanctions, though a July 2026 OFAC general license has eased restrictions. Recent data show a sharp rise in India’s Venezuelan oil imports, while the U.S. has recently eased sanctions on Venezuela, further supporting the potential deal

Indians Embrace Dark Chocolate as Premium Segment Booms

ITC 3MINDIA

Household purchases of dark chocolate rose 45% in the year ending August, driven by rising incomes, health trends, and quick‑commerce availability. Urban consumers account for 87% of home consumption, with penetration at 8.5% and out‑of‑home sales up 19%. Despite low per‑capita intake (≈200 g vs. >10 kg in the UK), the segment’s growth—3.8 times faster than the overall chocolate market—signals a strong premiumisation shift, though rural expansion remains limited by price and distribution challenges

Carlsberg India’s Revenue Declines 2% Amid Rising Volume and Market Share

3MINDIA UBL

Carlsberg India’s FY26 revenue fell 2.3% YoY to ₹8,815 cr, while net profit jumped 22.1% to ₹535 cr, outpacing rival United Breweries. Despite the revenue dip, the brewer saw healthy volume growth and its market share increase to 16.2% (up from 14.6%), maintaining its rank as India’s third‑largest brewer. The results come as the company, newly converted to a public limited firm, readies a planned $700 million IPO

India Extends Aluminium Can Certification Deadline by Six Months

BANKINDIA

The government has pushed back the mandatory BIS quality certification deadline for aluminium beverage cans to April 2027 for large firms, July 2027 for small firms, and October 2027 for micro firms, giving the industry extra time to address supply shortages. Domestic can producers have struggled to meet rising demand for soft drinks and beer, achieving only half of the targeted 600 million additional cans, while the war in the Middle East and lengthy certification processes have limited imports, which are 25‑30 % pricier. The extension aims to help manufacturers build inventory for the 2027 summer season

IRDAI Reviews LIAFI’s Demands Ahead of Distribution Reform Deadline

LICHSGFIN

The regulator will examine seven key demands from the Life Insurance Agents Federation of India (LIAFI) as it finalises a overhaul of insurance distribution norms. LIAFI opposes commission caps, public payout disclosures, and automatic clawbacks, seeking higher first‑year and renewal commissions linked to policy term lengths. It argues that agents’ earnings are already modest and that new rules would increase costs and deter new entrants. IRDAI acknowledges the representation but is unlikely to reverse its broader reform agenda, though it may tweak specific provisions. Stakeholder feedback closes on October 25, and the outcome will balance protecting agents’ livelihoods with lowering costs and preventing mis‑selling for policyholders

DGCA Extends Pilot Rest Rule Deadline to March 2027 Amid Airline Cost Pressures

SPICEJET TENNIND INDIGO

The Directorate General of Civil Aviation has pushed back the full implementation of its revised pilot rest regulations to March 31 2027, granting a third extension after earlier relaxations following IndiGo’s December crew‑shortage crisis. The rules tighten night duty definitions, limit landings and duty hours, and require double rest periods. Airlines argue the measures raise pilot staffing needs by up to 25% and add costs amid high fuel prices, rupee depreciation, and airspace constraints, while pilots’ unions say repeated extensions undermine safety objectives. No further exemptions will be offered after the new March deadline

Airlines Oppose Slot Reductions at Mumbai Airport Amid Terminal 1 Redevelopment

ABMINTLLTD GMRAIRPORT BCG

Airlines operating at Mumbai’s Adani‑controlled airport have rejected any unilateral cuts or cancellations of flight slots proposed for the summer 2027 traffic reallocation plan. They demand that Mumbai International Airport Ltd (MIAL) provide verified capacity data for both terminals, details of Terminal 1’s refurbishment timeline, and updates on the expected 5‑million‑passenger capacity addition at Terminal 2. The Airport Operators Committee (AOC) insists MIAL must follow the 2013 civil aviation slot‑allocation guidelines and IATA’s worldwide slot rules, noting no substantiated justification for slot reductions. While Terminal 1’s current capacity is 15 million passengers (aimed to rise to 20 million), MIAL has suggested airlines could shift operations to the new Navi Mumbai airport, suspend services until Terminal 1 reopens in 2030, or explore other options. A stakeholder meeting is set for October 28 to discuss the transition plan
