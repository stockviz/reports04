Indian Creators Turn to Bilibili Ahead of YouTube Monetisation Changes

3MINDIA

Indian digital creators are experimenting with Bilibili—often dubbed “China’s YouTube”—even though the platform hasn’t officially launched in India. Using VPNs, location spoofing and APKs, creators like travel vlogger Nawnit Kumar Tiwari have quickly amassed followers and views. Their interest aligns with a thaw in India‑China relations and comes as YouTube tightens its eligibility criteria for ad and Premium revenue from February 2027, raising subscriber and watch‑hour thresholds. Bilibili, originally an anime‑gaming community, is expanding internationally (Indonesia, Philippines, etc.) and may attract younger, niche audiences interested in anime, gaming, tech and learning. However, it faces hurdles such as building a creator ecosystem, integrating Indian banking, and competing with established platforms. Analysts see Bilibili as a complementary rather than replacement platform, emphasizing the need for more than financial incentives to succeed in India

Paramount Settlement Talks Over Warner Bros. Merger

LGBBROSLTD PARACABLES

Paramount and California officials are in confidential negotiations to resolve lawsuits challenging Paramount’s acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery. Key proposals include a commitment to keep operations in California, an editorial board to oversee CNN, releasing 30 theatrical films annually, restricting bundled channel distribution deals, and selling certain cable assets such as HGTV, Food Network, or Comedy Central. CEO David Ellison and AG Rob Bonta are directly involved. The settlement would need approval from the 12 Democratic states and the Writers Guild. Shares rose on news of advanced talks, with formal settlement discussions slated for Oct 14‑15 and a trial set for March. Opponents, including Senator Elizabeth Warren and actor Mark Ruffalo, warn against a deal that relies on “empty promises

ENTOD Pharmaceuticals Secures Patent for Insulin Eye Drops

APLLTD

ENTOD Pharmaceuticals announced that its insulin eye drop formulation has received patent approval, marking a step forward for indigenous ophthalmic research in India. Currently in pre‑clinical development, the novel therapy aims to address unmet ophthalmic needs, though further pre‑clinical and clinical studies are required to confirm safety and efficacy. The company’s CEO highlighted the achievement as evidence of India’s growing capability in original drug innovation, while an expert from Sarojini Devi Eye Hospital noted the scientific interest in repurposing insulin for eye treatment and emphasized the need for rigorous validation
