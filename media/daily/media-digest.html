Luxury Hotels and Restaurants Cash In on High‑End Chef Pop‑Ups Across India

ADVANIHOTR TRIDENT ABMINTLLTD THELEELA

Indian luxury hotels and restaurant groups are increasingly hosting short‑term residencies with Michelin‑starred and globally acclaimed chefs—from Massimo Bottura to Andy Beynon—to attract affluent diners willing to pay INR 60,000‑75,000+ per cover. These events sell out in minutes, boost average cover prices (7‑10% CAGR), and help build brand identity, drive repeat visits, and up‑skill culinary teams. Formats are limited to ~50 guests per service over 2‑3 days, keeping supply scarce and pricing premium. Costs vary by chef fee, travel and specialty ingredients, but hotels report no losses. Marriott’s Masters of Bonvoy, The Leela, Oberoi, Trident and others use the model to broaden appeal beyond hotel guests. The trend is spilling into permanent concepts—e.g., Gaggan Anand’s Rga—though sustaining such restaurants poses higher risk than pop‑ups. Overall, chef collaborations are becoming a lucrative, experience‑focused growth strategy in India’s upscale hospitality market
