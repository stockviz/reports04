Goldman Sachs Warns of Long‑Term Copper Supply Crunch

A Goldman Sachs Research report finds that global copper supply faces a structural, not cyclical, shortage due to declining discoveries, falling ore grades and soaring development costs. The median new mine now takes over 16 years from discovery to production, and output from existing mines is expected to fall from 2030 onward. Higher prices alone cannot spur sufficient new supply for two decades, pushing investors to value existing producers and mature projects more. Capital intensity rises ~50 % from pre‑feasibility to operation, and projected costs are 25‑90 % higher than company estimates. The firm estimates about 1 Mt/yr of new capacity could be added by 2035 from a group of growth projects, contributing roughly $20 bn in net asset value, but timing will hinge on execution, investment decisions, and cost management

Musk Announces Special Committees to Explore Tesla‑SpaceX Merger

Elon Musk disclosed that independent special committees of directors at Tesla and SpaceX will assess a potential merger between the two firms, whose combined valuation could exceed $3.5 trillion. Citing a conflict of interest, Musk—who owns ~20% of Tesla and ~45% of SpaceX and controls about 82% of SpaceX voting power—will not take part in the discussions. The committees were formed at shareholders’ request, and no deal terms have been agreed upon; the merger may never materialize. Musk noted possible synergies in AI, superintelligence software, chip design (via the upcoming Terafab venture), robotics, and mass‑to‑orbit capabilities, but emphasized that it is too early to assume a deal will happen

BARC Registration Delay Deepens TV Ratings Market Rift in India

HTMEDIA SITINET HATHWAY DISHTV 3MINDIA CRISIL

The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting has yet to approve fresh registrations for BARC India and rival TAM India, citing concerns over BARC’s ownership, progress on cross‑media measurement, and the lack of an updated Broadcast India study. BARC’s previous registration lapsed in 2025; despite appointing new independent directors and expanding its panel to 80,000 meters, the ministry remains dissatisfied, leading to a ratings blackout since June (news data withheld since March). This hampers broadcasters’ ability to benchmark performance and prove ad value in a TV ad market worth Rs 30,000‑40,000 crore, threatens smaller and free‑to‑air channels, and could impact advertising revenues, production jobs, and distribution platforms like DD Free Dish, Hathway, and Siti Networks. Financially, BARC’s liquidity appears intact per CRISIL, but many broadcasters have stopped paying fees, raising fears of a collapse. The stalemate underscores calls for increased competition in India’s TV ratings sector

Instacart Settles $5 Million Discrimination Claim Over PERM Hiring Practices

Instacart agreed to a $5 million settlement with the U.S. Justice Department and EEOC over allegations it discriminated against U.S.-born job seekers by requiring mailed applications for PERM‑related positions, while allowing electronic submissions for other roles. The company will pay $1 million in civil penalties, create a fund of up to $4 million to compensate affected applicants, and must post PERM jobs on its public career site and accept online applications.
