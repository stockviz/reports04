Biocon’s Pebrilzo Becomes First EMA Tailored‑Pathway Biosimilar Approval

BIOCON

Biocon Ltd has secured European Commission marketing authorisation for Pebrilzo, its pertuzumab biosimilar for HER2‑positive breast cancer, marking the first approval under the EMA’s new tailored clinical pathway. Following a positive CHMP opinion (Sept 17, 2026) and a recent US FDA approval, Pebrilzo is approved as a 420 mg concentrate for infusion, indicated for neoadjuvant, adjuvant, and metastatic settings in combination with trastuzumab (and chemotherapy or docetaxel). This becomes Biocon’s fifth oncology biosimilar approved in both Europe and the US, expanding access to affordable biologics

Nepal Secures 350 MW Power Import Deal with India for Winter 2026‑27

IEX

Nepal’s electricity authority has been approved by India’s Central Electricity Authority to import up to 350 MW of power from the Indian Energy Exchange for up to 17 hours daily between 17 Nov 2026 and 31 Jul 2027, addressing a winter shortfall caused by low hydropower output and flood‑damaged projects. Of this, 300 MW will flow via the Kataiya‑Kushaha, Ramnagar‑Surajpura and Raxaul‑Parwanipur lines, and 50 MW via the New Nautanwa‑Mainahiya line. Seasonal river flow variations and damage to 431 MW of hydropower capacity have reduced domestic generation, prompting reliance on imports, though generation is expected to rebound from mid‑April as water levels rise

Waymo Halts Freeway Robotaxi Service After California DMV Ban

Waymo has paused all freeway robotaxi operations following a California DMV order that barred the company from testing and operating driverless vehicles on state freeways, citing imminent public safety hazards due to repeated incursions into active construction zones and other incidents. The DMV’s restrictions remain until Waymo demonstrates effective corrective actions. While freeway service is suspended, Waymo continues surface‑street rides and has limited its freeway operations in other markets to assess performance. No collisions or injuries were reported in the California incidents, but the ban raises broader questions about the safety of autonomous robotaxis on public roads

Bosch Eyes Sale of India Tech Arm to TCS Amid GCC Off‑loading Trend

HCLTECH TCS INFY BOSCHLTD WIPRO

German auto‑parts maker Bosch is in early talks to divest part of its Indian technology operations, which employ about 3,000 engineers across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Coimbatore. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a leading bidder, potentially absorbing the staff if the deal closes. The move reflects a growing pattern of multinational firms shedding global capability centres (GCCs) in India to cut costs, while Indian IT services firms gain steady revenue streams and access to deep engineering talent. Recent similar transactions include TCS’s acquisition of Best Buy India’s tech centre, HCLTech’s purchase of Guardian Life’s GCC, and Wipro’s buy‑out of Mindsprint. Analysts note that while such acquisitions can pressure margins due to higher GCC salaries, they lock in long‑term contracts and align with shifting AI‑driven investment priorities. The sale is still under discussion, with board‑level talks expected next month
