NRIs’ Income Management: NRE vs. NRO Accounts

TIMESGTY IDFCFIRSTB

NRIs typically use NRE accounts for foreign-sourced earnings such as overseas salaries and business income, benefiting from full repatriation. Indian-sourced earnings—rental income, pensions, dividends, and other domestic receipts—are deposited into NRO accounts, which have repatriation limits. Many NRIs maintain both account types to keep overseas and Indian incomes separate, simplifying record‑keeping and banking. Before opening accounts, they should compare eligibility, documentation, digital services, transfer options, and fees
