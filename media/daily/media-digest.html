India Secures 6.94 Lakh Tonnes Steel Export Quota under EU FTA

TATASTEEL JINDALSTEL

India will receive country‑specific tariff‑rate quotas (TRQs) totalling 1.64 million tonnes annually for steel exports to the EU, of which 694,853 tonnes (6.94 lakh) are under the free‑trade‑agreement component. The remaining 946,616 tonnes fall under the MFN component. The quotas cover a broad range of products—hot‑rolled and cold‑rolled sheets, metallic‑ and organic‑coated sheets, tin‑mill, stainless steel, bars, rebars, wire rods, pipes, etc. The largest specific quota is for non‑alloy and alloy hot‑rolled sheets (509,605 tonnes). The EU’s Steel Overcapacity Regulation, effective 1 July, sets free‑of‑duty limits at 18.3 million tonnes and imposes a 50 % duty on imports beyond quotas. India currently ships about 4 million tonnes of steel to the EU each year. Quota administration will be transparent, with public information, and India may access additional volumes via competition among EU FTA partners. Reviews will begin one year after the FTA enters force, then every five years. The pact is expected to be signed by year‑end and implemented next year

India’s Compressed Biogas Potential Estimated at 62 MMT, Animal Waste Leads

India could generate about 62 million metric tonnes (MMT) of compressed biogas (CBG) annually from an estimated 472 MMT of feedstock. Animal waste offers the biggest share—≈190 MMT of dung could produce ≈25 MMT of CBG (41% of total), though only ~15% is currently collectable and dense rural clusters are needed. Agricultural residues (≈150 MMT) could yield ≈20 MMT of CBG, municipal solid waste (≈62 MMT) about 5 MMT, sewage‑treatment‑plant biomass (≈50 MMT) roughly 10 MMT, and sugar‑mill press mud (≈20 MMT) about 2 MMT. Key constraints are feedstock aggregation, limited collection windows, and low waste‑treatment rates. CBG production would curb stubble burning (≈87 MMT burnt annually), cut 0.8‑1.2 t CO₂e per tonne diverted, capture methane from ~2,400 dumpsites, generate organic manure, and help replace imported gas, potentially supporting ≈230 MMSCMD of biogas
