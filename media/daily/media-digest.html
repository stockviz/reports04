Microsoft Voluntary Exit Offer (VEO) Details – Eligibility, Teams, and Severance

Microsoft is offering a Voluntary Exit Offer to roughly 60,000 employees (about 25% of its workforce) at level 70 and below in select teams across Canada, India, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Eligible divisions include Xbox, Windows, Copilot, and finance, while many data‑center, sales, marketing, cybersecurity, and quantum‑computing staff are excluded. Severance varies by level and tenure: employees at levels 64‑ and below receive one week of base pay per six months of service; levels 65‑67 receive two weeks per six months, plus continued stock vesting (six to 12 months) and health insurance. The offer aligns with Microsoft’s standard US severance caps (up to 39 weeks for levels ≤67, 12 months for level 68). Employees must decide by early December and would depart at year‑end. The VEO aims to give workers a supported transition while allowing business units to adapt to shifting strategic priorities
