HDFC Bank Faces CEO Succession Challenge Amid Thin Internal Pipeline

5PAISA HDFCBANK

HDFC Bank must appoint a new chief executive as Sashidhar Jagdishan steps down, but a limited internal talent pool and RBI’s 15‑year cap on director tenures constrain options. The board is likely to hire a global search firm, with Egon Zehnder, Spencer Stuart and Korn Ferry shortlisted. Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha is a strong internal candidate, yet his tenure would end in 2029, preventing a full three‑year CEO term. Analysts suggest the board may broaden the hunt to external leaders from private banks, NBFCs or foreign institutions, bringing global risk‑management expertise. The transition is seen as an opportunity to reset governance and investor expectations, while Moody’s and other rating agencies consider the bank’s robust franchise and deep senior bench to mitigate succession risk

Sun Pharma Joins 9 Pharma Firms in Trump Administration’s MFN Drug‑Pricing Deal

CSLFINANCE APLLTD SPARC AJANTPHARM

President Trump announced that Sun Pharma and eight other manufacturers have agreed to most‑favoured‑nation pricing for their drugs, covering 89% of the branded market and extending MFN rates to all state Medicaid programs. The deals aim to align U.S. drug prices with those in other developed nations, generate billions in savings, and include commitments to invest $19.6 billion in U.S. manufacturing. Sun Pharma will also add 71.4 tonnes of clindamycin and 6.75 tonnes of doxycycline to the Strategic Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients Reserve. The administration expects the agreements to save Americans $600 billion over the next decade

UBS Secures Partial Compromise on Swiss Capital Demands, But Debate Persists

ASHIKA BCG SWISSMLTRY

Switzerland’s upper house Economic Affairs and Taxation Committee has proposed allowing UBS to use up to 50% convertible debt (AT1 bonds) to meet a $20 billion capital hike—a softer stance than the government’s initial push for full equity backing. The reform, aimed at preventing foreign subsidiaries from dragging down UBS’s domestic core, was approved 10-2 but faces scrutiny from left-leaning lawmakers (e.g., Social Democrats) and the Finance Ministry, which calls AT1s unreliable in crises.

Key concessions: - AT1s would trigger earlier in stress scenarios (e.g., barring dividends, buybacks, and coupon payments if UBS’s CET1 capital drops below thresholds). - The Swiss Bankers Association welcomed AT1 reforms but rejected stricter equity demands. - UBS remains silent on the decision, but the proposal could ease its burden—debt is cheaper than equity, improving profitability metrics.

Next steps: - Upper house vote: Sept 14–Oct 2, 2026. - Lower house may push back, risking reversals.

Criticism: AT1s, while flexible, are seen as riskier than equity for loss absorption (as seen in Credit Suisse’s collapse). The committee’s president called it “no victory for UBS” but a pragmatic solution for Switzerland

India’s Microdrama Market Poised for Consolidation Around Retention Over Acquisition

TIPSFILMS ZEEL

India’s microdrama sector, with ~100 million users and 250 million downloads, faces a stark gap between total users and paying subscribers—estimated at 5‑20 million. Executives warn that heavy reliance on acquisition and aggressive tactics (dark patterns, auto‑renewals) masks weak product‑market fit, making retention, engagement, and sustainable monetisation crucial. The market is expected to consolidate into a few key players: standalone microdrama apps with strong retention, large Indian OTT/short‑video platforms leveraging existing audiences and tech, and global entrants from China and the US. Major players like ShareChat, Pocket FM, Pratilipi, Amazon MX Player, JioHotstar, Zee, and Flipkart are vying for the limited pool of engaged, paying users. Lower production costs (15‑20 % of long‑form content) fuel rapid expansion, but without focused retention strategies, long‑term sustainability remains uncertain

Maruti Suzuki Announces Rs 77,500 Crore Capex Plan to Capitalize on Post-GST Demand Growth

MARUTI

Maruti Suzuki, India’s largest carmaker, has outlined a Rs 77,500 crore capital expenditure (capex) plan over the next five years (FY26–FY31) to leverage the demand surge following GST rate cuts. Chairman RC Bhargava highlighted that GST reforms provided stability amid global uncertainties like the West Asia war, helping the economy and auto sector thrive.

The company expects the Indian car market to grow to 6.1–6.3 million units by 2031, with small car demand rising faster than previous trends. Hisashi Takeuchi (MD) noted broad-based demand across segments (small cars, SUVs) and revealed a 40% capex jump in FY26–27 (from Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 14,000 crore). The funds will support: - Capacity expansion - New model development & R&D - Carbon-neutral initiatives - Logistics & sales infrastructure

Takeuchi also assured shareholders that all Maruti vehicles since 2008 are E20-compatible, addressing concerns over ethanol fuel adoption. The capex exceeds Suzuki Motor Corporation’s earlier Rs 70,000 crore announcement, reflecting aggressive growth plans

Supreme Court Rejects Petition for Ethanol Transparency at Fuel Pumps

HINDPETRO BPCL IOC

The Supreme Court dismissed a public interest litigation filed by advocate Narendra Kumar Goswami that sought mandatory disclosure of ethanol percentages on petrol dispensing nozzles, fuel bills, and a searchable vehicle‑compatibility database. The bench urged the petitioner to approach a competent authority, while the Attorney General labeled the case “proxy litigation,” noting previous rejections of challenges to India’s ethanol‑blending policy. Goswami’s request also included forming an expert committee to examine the issue

GRT Jewellers to Acquire 74% Stake in TBZ for ₹1,034 Cr

TBZ KALYANKJIL

GRT Jewellers India signed an agreement to purchase a 74.12% stake in listed jewellery retailer Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri (TBZ) from its promoters for up to ₹1,033.71 crore (49.46 million shares at up to ₹209 each), subject to regulatory approvals. The company will launch a mandatory open offer for the remaining 26% in line with SEBI norms. TBZ shares closed at ₹305.45, up 1.19%. The acquisition expands GRT’s retail footprint from 68 Indian stores (plus one in Singapore) to 106 stores nationwide. Upon completion, GRT will have sole control of TBZ, and TBZ’s CMD and directors will resign, though some may stay on as consultants
