Delhi High Court Stays FSSAI Ban on Dabur’s “100% Pure” Claims

DABUR

The Delhi High Court has temporarily halted the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India’s (FSSAI) order that barred Dabur India from marketing products with “100% Pure”, “100% Natural” and “100% Purity Guaranteed” claims. Justice Amit Mahajan ruled that the regulator should have given Dabur a chance to be heard before issuing the prohibitory order, issuing a notice to FSSAI and directing a response within 15 days, with the stay remaining until the next hearing on August 24. The FSSAI’s original order targeted several Dabur items—including honey, apple cider vinegar, virgin coconut oil, sesame oil, cow ghee, coconut water and coconut milk—citing that such claims are ambiguous, unverifiable and potentially misleading under the Food Safety and Standards (Advertising & Claims) Regulations, 2018. Dabur contested the order, arguing a breach of natural justice and asserting longstanding use of the labels. The regulator countered that an improvement notice had already been served and that the designated officer was empowered to prohibit non‑compliant sales. Dabur has begun transitioning its packaging away from the disputed claims, while its shares have slipped amid the regulatory clash
