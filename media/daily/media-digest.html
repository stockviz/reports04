DMart Growth Outlook: Same‑Store Sales, Store Expansion, and Margin Pressures

DMART

DMart reported 18% revenue growth, outpacing estimates, thanks to new store openings and modest margin improvement. Same‑store sales rose 7‑8% in smaller cities—below the ~12% nominal GDP growth—raising concerns about slower FY27 revenue growth. Margins benefit from price advantage, higher‑margin private‑label mix, and efficiencies as stores mature, but new‑store roll‑out dilutes profitability in the short term. The festive season is expected to boost sales, while EBITDA and PAT are projected to rise 21% and 28% respectively. Key margin levers include increased volume, private‑label penetration, and cost efficiencies from stores past the 12‑month mark. Store additions have been muted (≈518 stores); further expansion into Tier‑2/3 cities should drive growth, but the “DMart Ready” model may pressure margins due to low‑price competition and higher investment needs. Balancing new‑store sales, same‑store growth, and operational efficiency will be critical to sustain growth and margin expansion

Beyond Reach Report: Indian Marketers Prioritise Reach Over Attention, Yet Seek Reliable Attention Data

CYBERMEDIA MUFTI

A Publicis Media India and MMA India study shows 81% of Indian marketers rank reach or incremental reach among their top three media‑allocation metrics, while only 17% prioritize attention quality. 69% do not incorporate attention into media plans, yet 86% would reallocate budgets if trustworthy attention data proved more effective. The main barrier is the lack of reliable, standardized attention measurement (cited by 92% of respondents). Lumen’s India benchmarks illustrate varying attentive seconds per 1,000 impressions across formats (e.g., 692 s for Instagram Reels, 7,771 s for linear TV). Although 94% view creative as equally important to placement for gaining attention, merely 14% systematically adapt creative to different formats. The report urges integrating attention metrics with reach in media planning, format choice, frequency, creative development, and budget decisions, and calls for industry standards and better data integration

Uniform 5% GST on Cab and Delivery Platforms

ZOMATO SWIGGY OLAELEC

The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIT) clarified that a flat 5% Goods and Services Tax applies to cab aggregators (Ola, Uber) and delivery platforms (Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon, Flipkart) irrespective of their business models, covering both marketplace and direct delivery services. GST rates will be revised annually, taking effect from April 1, with the GST Council deciding ahead of each fiscal year. Future reforms will focus on simplifying compliance, improving refunds and input tax credit processes, and making the Invoice Management System mandatory. The effective GST rate has fallen to 10.84% after rationalisation, boosting revenue growth
