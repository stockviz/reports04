Credit Card Boom in India: Banks Rush to Expand Portfolios Ahead of Festive Season

AUBANK KOTAKBANK FEDERALBNK HDFCBANK BANKBARODA ICICIBANK AXISBANK BANKINDIA SBICARD

In September 2026, Indian banks—led by HDFC Bank and Axis Bank—added 1.26 million net credit cards, marking the second-highest monthly growth this year (after July’s 1.27 million). The surge reflects aggressive expansion ahead of the festive season, with HDFC and Axis alone contributing 43% of new cards (539,878 combined). SBI Cards followed as the third-largest contributor.

Growth was uneven: ICICI Bank and Kotak Mahindra Bank saw muted additions, while others like Bank of Baroda, Slice Small Finance Bank, and Federal Bank also expanded their portfolios. Quarterly data (July–September) showed a 47% YoY rise in net additions (3.72 million), up 26% from Q2 2026, signaling heightened festive-season preparations.

Total outstanding credit cards reached 125.32 million by September—10.4% higher than last year. HDFC Bank led monthly additions (280,353), doubling August’s figures, while Axis Bank’s growth (259,525) was eightfold from the prior month. The trend underscores banks’ focus on capturing holiday spending demand

HRX’s Rise: From Celebrity Concept to Scalable Brand Through Myntra Partnership

IMAGICAA

Launched in 2012 by Hrithik Roshan as a personal fitness‑inspired line, HRX quickly moved beyond mere celebrity branding by partnering with online retailer Myntra. Cipla eyes early launch of Keytruda biosimilar to capture market share

CIPLA APLLTD SUNPHARMA

Cipla has partnered exclusively with Qilu Pharmaceutical to develop and commercialize a biosimilar of Merck’s high‑earning cancer drug Keytruda (pembrolizumab), aiming to be among the first global launches before the patent expires around 2029. CEO Achin Gupta expects a high‑single‑digit to low‑double‑digit market share, citing significant revenue potential. The partnership assigns Qilu to development, regulatory work, and supply, while Cipla USA will handle commercialization, especially in the US. Despite a 35% YoY drop in net profit to ₹878 crore and a 2% decline in revenue for the September quarter, Cipla’s margin showed a sequential improvement

Tata Motors’ Iveco Bid Clears 80% Threshold, Reaches 87.8% Acceptance

YESBANK

Tata Motors’ subsidiary TML CV Holdings BV secured tender offers for 238.16 million Iveco shares, representing 87.81% of the company’s capital, surpassing the 80% acceptance hurdle. The EUR 3.43 billion deal, raised to €14.40 per share, marks Tata’s largest buyout since the 2008 JLR acquisition. Provisional results show Tata now holds 89.42% of Iveco, with final results due before market open on 29 Oct and a reopened offer period slated for 2‑6 Nov, followed by payment on 13 Nov. The acquisition aims to expand Tata’s global commercial‑vehicle footprint
