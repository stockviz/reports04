Narayana Hrudayalaya to Manage Kolkata’s Mission of Mercy Hospital

NH

Narayana Hrudayalaya Ltd has signed an agreement to exclusively operate and manage the 100‑bed Mission of Mercy Hospital and Research Centre on Park Street, Kolkata. The deal, made with the hospital and the Assemblies of God of North India, allows Narayana to apply its healthcare expertise without acquiring ownership or lease rights to the 63,240 sq ft facility. Commercial operations will commence once certain conditions are met, potentially within 12 months

Festive Season Brands Extend EMIs to 30 Months to Boost Sales Amid Price Hikes

VIVOBIOT 3MINDIA GODREJAGRO

Smartphone and consumer‑electronics makers such as Samsung, Vivo, Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo and Nothing are lengthening no‑cost or low‑cost EMI tenures to as long as 30 months—up to six‑eight months longer than usual—to offset steep price rises and revive demand, especially in mass‑to‑mid‑range segments. The brands are absorbing interest costs (8‑18% for 8‑30 month plans, up to 24% for longer loans) to keep monthly payouts low, even offering such schemes for entry‑level phones and appliances like TVs and refrigerators. Industry insiders say finance schemes now drive about a third of mobile and electronics sales, expected to rise to half during the festive period, with consumer‑finance share in smartphone sales climbing to 42‑43% this year. The strategy aims to protect margins while maintaining volume growth amid a 30‑35% rise in handset prices and a 12% drop in sales year‑to‑date

San Diego Sues AppLovin Over Child‑Targeted Explicit Ads

DELTACORP

San Diego County filed a lawsuit accusing ad‑tech firm AppLovin of bypassing parental controls and using fingerprinting to deliver adult‑oriented ads—including dating, alcohol, vaping, and graphic sexual content—to children’s devices, while collecting precise location and personal data. The complaint cites screenshots of such ads shown in kids‑rated games on Android phones with Google’s parental settings enabled. The case is part of three lawsuits by the county’s new Consumer Fairness and Public Protection Unit, which also targets Roblox and Polymaker for related consumer‑protection failures. AppLovin’s policies deny knowingly collecting child data, and the firm has not commented; the SEC previously investigated but closed the probe without enforcement

GST Council to Consider Extending GST to Gold Imports by Nominated Agencies

AUGMONT MMTC STCINDIA

The government may bring agencies like MMTC and STC under the 3% GST regime for gold and precious metal imports to align tax treatment across banks, nominated agencies, and exchanges. The proposal, slated for discussion at the October 8 GST Council meeting, aims to end the preferential exemption granted since 2017, promote a level playing field, reduce classification disputes, and eventually shift more bullion trade to exchanges for greater price transparency, though it could initially raise working‑capital costs and widen local premiums

Hospital Pricing Must Reflect Full Clinical Value Chain

MAXHEALTH

Max Healthcare MD Abhay Soi emphasized that Indian private hospitals should not be judged solely on the price of drugs, injections, or consumables. He argued that these items are part of a broader value chain that includes specialized training, storage, administration, and proper waste disposal, all of which contribute to patient care. Soi noted that hospitals handle thousands of billing items with varying margins, ultimately targeting an 8‑9% net profit. His remarks come amid Maharashtra’s scrutiny of high markups on hospital consumables and a Supreme Court observation about a cancer drug’s ten‑fold price increase when procured through in‑hospital pharmacies

Indian mid-sized restaurant and QSR chains such as Punjab Grill, Mad Over Donuts, Dindigul Thalappakatti, Charcoal Eats and Absolute Barbecues are struggling to raise fresh capital. Investors now demand proven unit economics, profitability and capital‑efficient growth, leaving a funding gap for businesses with consumer acceptance but needing scale. While larger deals continue (e.g., The Belgian Waffle Co, Popo Global, Trimex Foods, Subway India), firms with weaker operating performance find fundraising difficult
