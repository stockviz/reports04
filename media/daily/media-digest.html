Vedanta to Invest $200 Million to Boost Rajasthan Oil Output

VOGL

Vedanta Oil and Gas will spend about $200 million in 2026‑27 to enhance production in its northern Rajasthan fields, focusing on the Mangla field to raise output from 80,000 to over 150,000 barrels per day using polymer flooding and other enhanced‑recovery techniques. Interim CEO Jim Johnny Gast highlighted natural declines of 1‑3% in existing wells and the need to increase recovery rates from 41% toward a 60% target to help close the gap between stagnant national crude production and rising consumption. Rajasthan accounts for 81% of Vedanta’s production, which fell 17% year‑on‑year to 77.7 kboepd in Q1

India’s EV market surges 95% in September, driven by fuel price hikes and new models

TATAMOTORS M&M HYUNDAI JSWCEMENT MARUTI

EV sales jumped to 34,894 units in September, lifting electric vehicle penetration to 8.2% of passenger cars. Tata Motors remained the leader with a 42.2% share, followed by Mahindra (22.2%). New entrants VinFast (8.5%) and Maruti Suzuki (3.1%) gained ground, while BYD and Hyundai lost share. Overall registrations rose 83% YoY to 248,890 units in the first nine months, with Tata and Mahindra together holding about two‑thirds of the market. Kia showed rapid growth, while Toyota entered and Stellantis saw a sharp decline. The boost is attributed to higher fuel prices, expanding charging infrastructure, and a broader model lineup

India’s September Power Demand Hits Record 269 GW Amid Deficit Monsoon

NTPC COALINDIA

In September, India’s electricity consumption surged 11.3% YoY to 162 billion units, driven by cooling and irrigation needs during a weak monsoon, and set a new monthly peak demand of 269 GW. NTPC Ltd produced 38.8 billion units, up 12.5% YoY. Coal India’s total fuel supply rose 12.5% to 61.2 million tonnes, with coal deliveries to power plants increasing 10.6% YoY to 48.9 million tonnes and to non‑regulated users up 19.4%. Coal production grew 9.2% to 53.5 million tonnes, and the company expects further increases post‑monsoon. Coal stocks at power plants fell sharply due to transportation disruptions from rain‑affected coal‑bearing regions. Coal India’s targets for the fiscal year are 815 million tonnes of production and 850 million tonnes of supply

China Halts Oil Product Exports, Driving Crude Prices Above $101

TIMESGTY

Oil prices jumped ~2% after China stopped exporting oil products beyond Hong Kong and Macau, tightening already scarce fuel markets. Front‑month December Brent rose to $101.20 per barrel (+3.2%) and WTI hit $92 (+1.8%). Analysts note the export ban reflects domestic supply concerns and may limit flexible supply amid Middle Eastern disruptions, further pressuring global diesel availability
