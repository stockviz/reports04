U.S. Debt Tops $40 Trillion, Doubling Since 2017

The U.S. Treasury reports total public debt exceeding $40 trillion—$32 trillion held by the public and $7.8 trillion in intragovernmental holdings. Debt has more than doubled from under $20 trillion in January 2017 to today, with $8.4 trillion added under President Biden (infrastructure, clean energy, pandemic relief) and $3.8 trillion under President Trump’s 2025 term. The CBO projects an additional $4.7 trillion from Trump’s One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Watchdogs warn of a fiscal crisis, while markets react to rising bond yields; the Treasury doubled its debt‑buyback cap to $4 billion, temporarily easing yields. The situation puts pressure on the Federal Reserve and policymakers

MMTC

Small Towns Drive Explosive Growth in India’s Connected TV Market

3MINDIA SINDHUTRAD HTMEDIA

Connected TV (CTV) in India is expanding rapidly, now reaching 62‑65 million households and 207 million viewers, with audience size tripling since 2022. While urban penetration sits at about 47%, the strongest growth comes from non‑metro areas: CTV audiences grew 21% in metros, 46% in mini‑metros, 55% in small towns, and a staggering 110% in rural regions. States such as Bihar, Jharkhand, Rajasthan, Kerala, and parts of the Northeast show above‑average growth. Most CTV users (68%) also watch linear TV, and many households are “cord‑cutters” or light linear viewers. Prime‑time activity is high on both screens, with 78% of CTV viewers active between 8‑10 pm on weekdays. Measurement is challenging because CTV is a shared, household experience—average households have 3.3 members, and each impression reaches about 2.5 people—necessitating cross‑screen frequency management and unified measurement for advertisers
