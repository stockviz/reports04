RBI’s 25‑bp Hike Signals “Calibrated Tightening” and a Longer‑Term Rate Cycle

The Reserve Bank of India lifted the repo rate by 25 basis points and announced a shift in policy stance to “calibrated tightening,” the first use of the term since 2018. Growth forecasts were nudged up 40 bps to 7.1 % and inflation to 5.2 %, reinforcing a hawkish outlook and suggesting two to four more hikes may be needed. Banking stocks rallied (Bank Nifty up ~0.2 %) as higher repo rates immediately raise EBLR‑linked loan rates, while no CRR increase was announced, keeping the sector broadly positive. Bond yields slipped to around 7.24 % and the weighted average call rate stayed below the repo rate, indicating excess liquidity that the RBI may drain via OMO sales or reverse‑repo operations. Experts expect the 25‑bp move to have little immediate impact on credit growth, though larger cumulative hikes could temper the 18‑19 % credit expansion with a lag. The stance change, higher forecasts, and continued liquidity management are seen as a defensive measure for the rupee, but the gap between on‑shore and offshore funding costs remains a risk. Overall, markets have priced in the modest hike, but the surprise stance shift keeps the outlook for further tightening and modest upside for banks’ net‑interest margins

GST Council’s Upcoming Agenda: Structural Reforms Across Sectors

ZOMATO SWIGGY

The GST Council’s October 8 meeting will focus on structural reforms rather than rate cuts. The council will also target faster registration, quicker refunds, and de‑criminalisation of GST offenses, with any future rate changes likely limited to an annual review for greater business certainty
