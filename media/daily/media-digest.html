India’s Cab‑Booking Apps Fined for “Tip” Dark‑Patterns

OLAELEC

India’s consumer watchdog, the Central Consumer Protection Authority, fined Ola and Rapido ₹10 lakh each for prompting passengers to add “tips” or “add‑ons” before a ride is confirmed, effectively turning cab booking into a live auction. The apps suggested that higher tips increased the chance of driver acceptance, but data showed acceptance rates were lower when tips were added. Rapido’s UI used green/red cues to steer users toward higher payments, constituting “confirm shaming” and “interface interference.” Regulators demanded proof that the extra amount went fully to drivers, which Ola failed to provide. The fines are modest relative to revenues, but the platforms are now ordered to stop such pre‑ride payment prompts, highlighting concerns over app‑driven price manipulation and power imbalances between riders and ride‑hailing services

AMDIND
