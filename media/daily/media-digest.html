Renault Duster Hybrid First‑Drive Review – Real‑World Performance, Comfort & Value

The Overdrive team finds the new Duster Hybrid, priced from ₹16.99 lakh, delivers a smooth electric‑first drive with a 1.8 L Atkinson‑cycle engine plus a 50 PS electric motor, offering 160 PS total and five drive modes. It feels quiet, EV‑like in city traffic, and maintains power without noticeable gaps, though engine kick‑in adds slight vibration and engine braking is limited. The added 80 kg weight is well‑managed by revised suspension and FDD dampers, giving a composed, European‑style ride. Interior changes are minor—new tan upholstery, same 10.25‑inch instrument cluster and 10.1‑inch touchscreen (still basic). Cargo space drops to 372 L but remains practical. Overall, the Duster Hybrid retains the original’s rugged styling and dynamics, offers solid fuel efficiency, and presents strong value for a mainstream hybrid SUV in India
