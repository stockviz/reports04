Tata Steel Q2 Sales Dip Overall, India Growth Offsets Overseas Decline

TATASTEEL

Consolidated sales fell slightly to 7.96 mt in September from 8.02 mt YoY, driven by weaker overseas markets, while Indian sales rose 8% to 5.97 mt. Output in the Netherlands and UK declined, but Indian crude steel production rose 10% to 6.21 mt. Automotive & Special Products sales jumped 19% to 1.1 mt, and Branded Products & Retail reached 2.2 mt. Downstream segments, especially Colors, saw strong growth, and the Netherlands Direct Sheet Plant resumed full‑capacity operations. UK deliveries were modestly lower due to heavy rains and supply‑chain inventory buildup

India’s Diesel Exports Surge to One‑Year High in September, Driven by Record Margins and Europe Demand

BPCL ATHERENERG RELIANCE MRPL IOC HINDPETRO

India exported about 620,000 bpd of diesel in September, the highest in a year, as record‑high Singapore‑Dubai diesel cracks above $60 / bbl spurred refiners to boost output. Europe received roughly 280,000 bpd, an 80 % jump from August, while Africa got 200,000 bpd, Asia 50,000 bpd, the Americas 40,000 bpd, and 50,000 bpd went to unknown destinations. No shipments went to Russia. Prices were double year‑ago levels due to West Asian and Russian supply shocks; China banned diesel exports and the US faced pressure to follow. A $37 / bbl windfall tax cuts margins but exempts Reliance’s export‑only Jamnagar unit, the main exporter. Increased Gulf and Russian crude supplies, along with Indian refineries’ high middle‑distillate yields, helped position India as a swing supplier to Europe

Foreign Workers Sign €31 Million Settlement Over Labor Abuses at U.S. Milan Consulate

BIGBLOC

More than 60 foreign laborers, primarily from India, signed agreements in Milan to receive an average €45,000 payout as part of a €31 million settlement with U.S. contractor Caddell Construction for unpaid overtime, illegal housing deductions, and other abuses. The deal, the largest labor exploitation settlement in Europe, also includes €11.2 million for Italy’s social security and permits for the workers. The settlement aims to compensate workers, improve their legal status, and highlight systemic exploitation in the construction sector

Supreme Court Rules Electricity Subsidy to Existing Businesses Taxable

AARON

The apex court held that electricity subsidies given to companies that do not fund new capital assets are revenue receipts and therefore taxable under the Income Tax Act. In Mepco Industries v. Commissioner of Income Tax, the Court affirmed lower‑court rulings that the subsidy was intended to offset operating costs, not to contribute to capital formation, despite the scheme’s broader objective of encouraging industrialisation in backward regions. Consequently, the ₹16.20 lakh subsidy received for FY 1997‑98 is includable in taxable income

India’s Hotel Transactions Surge to $493 Million in H1 2026, Tripling Year‑on‑Year

In the first half of 2026, Indian hotel deals reached $493 million—almost three times the amount recorded a year earlier—according to JLL data. The luxury segment led with 52% of the volume, followed by upscale (35%), upper‑upscale (12%) and economy (1%). Tier‑II/III cities accounted for about 60% of transactions, while Tier‑I cities made up 40%. JLL now projects total hotel transactions for calendar 2026 at roughly $1.1 billion, a 94% rise from 2025’s $567 million. In Q1 2026, India posted $345 million in deals, placing it fourth in the Asia‑Pacific region behind Japan, China and South Korea, and representing 13% of the region’s quarterly transaction volume
