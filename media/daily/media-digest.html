Pentagon Scrutinizes Cerberus Ties Amid Conflict‑of‑Interest Concerns

DHANI AVONMORE

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has been appointed to review all Department of Defense transactions involving companies linked to private‑equity firm Cerberus Capital Management, whose deputy secretary Stephen Feinberg brings former Cerberus executives into the Pentagon. Lutnick’s past role at Cantor Fitzgerald, which has long worked with Cerberus, raises questions about his impartiality. The oversight is meant to exceed existing ethics rules, but critics argue it may not fully resolve the potential conflicts of interest surrounding high‑value contracts such as the M1 helicopter‑training deal and Stratolaunch’s hypersonic program

Trump Trade Policy Risks Undermining U.S. Chip Supply Chain

SURANASOL SPELS

A White House proclamation to boost U.S. polysilicon production has driven away the last two customers of Germany’s Wacker Chemie’s Charleston, Tennessee plant, putting the 600‑worker facility at risk of closure. The measures impose a price floor and tariffs on polysilicon and related products, but treat foreign‑sourced material the same as domestic, leaving U.S. polysilicon at a cost disadvantage of up to four times. Industry analysts and advocacy groups warn the policy may fail to spur demand and could weaken efforts to secure semiconductor and solar supply chains against Chinese competitors. Wacker has not yet decided on closure and says it is in talks with the administration, while other U.S. producers like Hemlock Semiconductor are less affected. The outcome remains uncertain as the rules take effect in December

Stellantis Explores Partnerships with Huawei and JAC for Maserati’s Revival

BCG

Stellantis is in negotiations with Chinese tech giant Huawei and automotive firm JAC Group to forge a long‑term industrial partnership aimed at revitalizing its struggling luxury brand Maserati. The talks align with CEO Antonio Filosa’s strategy to secure new manufacturing collaborations to boost production at Maserati’s Cassino and Modena plants in Italy. Maserati, which sold fewer than 8,000 cars last year and posted a €198 million loss, is expected to unveil its long‑term plan in December. Stellantis says such discussions are part of its routine efforts to offer customers better mobility options

Kia India adopts a multi‑powertrain strategy to tackle diverse market demand

Kia India is preparing to launch electric, hybrid, and CNG vehicles simultaneously, acknowledging the difficulty of predicting the dominant powertrain in the country. Managing Director Gwanggu Lee highlighted the “huge burden” on OEMs but stressed the need to respond to real market trends. The company debuted the Sorento, a three‑row SUV offered in hybrid‑petrol and diesel, aiming to capture premium buyers. Kia also announced CNG versions of the Carens and Carens Clavis, noting that CNG now accounts for over 20% of Indian car sales. A hybrid Seltos is slated for 2027, and a hybrid Carnival minivan was showcased. Kia targets 410,000 Indian sales by 2030, with eight of ten new models electrified and expecting EVs and hybrids to make up more than 30% of sales, contingent on affordability

Hindustan Unilever’s Growth Revival Plan

HINDUNILVR

Hindustan Unilever (HUL) aims to revive growth after two muted years by cutting costs, sharpening its portfolio, and boosting spend on brands, distribution, and new categories. Capital expenditure will rise to 3% of turnover, with over 75% earmarked for growth and savings. The company targets a 500‑basis‑point “fuel for growth” via better margins, efficiencies, and savings, redeploying gains into product, packaging, media, pricing, and channel investments. HUL sets a medium‑term EBITDA margin goal of 22‑24% (23.6% in FY26) and seeks higher operating leverage. Growth will be driven by four pools: increasing consumption, premiumisation (each 40% of incremental turnover), expanding categories, and entering new spaces (20%). Focus areas include premium beauty, functional deodorants, and repositioning foods like Horlicks toward lifestyle nutrition, superfoods, and protein, with a heavy emphasis on digital media and premium brand investment

SHALPAINTS

Telco Operators Reject QoS Norms for 5G Network Slicing

IDEA JIOFIN AIRTELPP

Indian telecom giants Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel and Vodafone Idea have opposed TRAI’s draft guidelines that set an 80% radio‑resource‑block utilisation ceiling for 5G slicing, arguing it is an internal metric, not a customer‑facing QoS measure, and would force premature capacity upgrades. While TRAI and think‑tanks see the benchmark as a safeguard against network degradation, industry groups like GSMA and Vi contend that high utilisation alone does not indicate poor user experience and that slicing should remain flexible and technology‑neutral

OAL

Mandatory Co‑Located Storage for New Solar and Wind Projects in India

COSMOFIRST

The Central Electricity Authority (CEA) proposes that all solar and wind plants commissioned from July 2027 must include co‑located storage equal to at least 10% of installed capacity with a minimum two‑hour duration (e.g., a 100 MW solar plant needs 10 MW, 2‑hour storage). The draft amendment to the CEA technical standards, open for comments until 4 Oct, aims to improve dispatchability and revenue stability. A later phase (July 2029‑June 2031) will raise the storage duration requirement. The rule makes what was previously advisory now mandatory, aligning with utilities’ and industry’s demand for firm, continuous renewable power
