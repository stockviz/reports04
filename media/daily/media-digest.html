India’s Edible Oil Supply Under Strain: Black Sea Conflict Drives Sunflower Oil Shortage, Prices Rise

BCG

India imports 58‑65% of its edible oil, and the Russia‑Ukraine war plus Black Sea port attacks have stalled shipments, trapping 150,000 t of sunflower oil and cutting Russian/Ukraine exports to about 200,000 t / month.
