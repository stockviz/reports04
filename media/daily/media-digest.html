Tata’s Agratas Shifts to In‑House LFP Cell Development Amid China Restrictions

PENINLAND ABMINTLLTD ADANIENSOL DELTACORP TMPV BCG RELIANCE

Tata’s battery unit Agratas is building a pilot line for lithium‑iron‑phosphate (LFP) cells at its Sanand, Gujarat factory, using its own technology after Chinese export curbs halted licensing deals. An Indian‑South Korean‑Chinese engineering team will refine processes before commercial rollout. While Agratas continues licensed production of nickel‑manganese‑cobalt (NMC) cells with Japan’s AES, it is developing LFP cells from scratch, adding cost and time but targeting grid‑scale storage and domestic supply. A $400 million R&D center in Bengaluru will support LFP and lithium‑manganese‑iron‑phosphate work. The Sanand plant will also produce NMC cells by early 2027, and its UK plant aims to supply Jaguar Land Rover’s upcoming electric Range Rover
