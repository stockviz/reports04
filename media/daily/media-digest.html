Tata Trusts’ Merger Plan to Strip TSPL of NBFC Status

NSIL DSSL TATACAP

Tata Trusts, holding 66 % of Tata Sons Private Limited (TSPL), proposes merging operating firms Tata Electronics Systems Solutions and Tata Consulting Engineers into TSPL to convert it into an operating entity. Post‑merger, over 64 % of TSPL’s income would come from operations rather than investments, dropping it below RBI thresholds for NBFC classification. The company would then simply notify the RBI for de‑registration, without needing prior RBI permission, though it must still obtain a No‑Objection Certificate under the RBI’s 2025 Voluntary Amalgamation Directions. The move follows board friction after the RBI rejected TSPL’s request to shed its “upper‑layer” NBFC status. Legal expert HP Ranina notes that shareholder majority (the Trusts’ 66 % voting right) can approve such restructurings, and minority shareholders (≈18 % stake) lack legal power to block them. The restructuring aims to keep TSPL private while complying with regulatory requirements

TATAMOTORS M&M MARUTI
