India’s Institutional Banking Surge & Future Outlook

AXISBANK AGL 3MINDIA

Citibank India’s CEO K. Balasubramanian says the bank’s post‑consumer exit focus on institutional banking has driven a 76% rise in institutional revenues (CAGR 21%) and a 56% jump in net income (CAGR 16%) between FY 2023‑26, with client assets crossing ₹1 lakh crore. The bank aims for ~20% YoY asset growth, balancing deposit costs and prudent underwriting

BARC Blackout Spurs Operator-Led TV Viewership Measurement

AIRTELPP TATACAP DAMCAPITAL GTPL 3MINDIA DEN

With BARC ratings suspended, cable and DTH operators are turning to return‑path‑data (RPD) from set‑top boxes as an alternative source of TV viewership metrics. GTPL Hathway has begun publishing data from 10,000 STBs (aiming for 50,000) in partnership with TAM India, while Tata Play has used RPD internally for years. RPD records channel‑wise viewing at the household/device level, offering scale and geographic coverage but lacking individual‑level and demographic detail. Its industry credibility will hinge on sample size, representativeness, transparent methodology, and acceptance by broadcasters and advertisers. Past attempts to scale RPD, including BARC’s pilot with Airtel Digital TV and DEN Networks, faltered due to cost and data‑sharing issues. Experts suggest broader operator adoption could yield regional channel rankings, yet RPD may not replace traditional ratings as the primary trading metric
