INFY TCS 5PAISA

By 2026-10-09, AI-driven automation has reshaped labor markets, eliminating ~12% of mid-skill jobs (e.g., data entry, basic customer service) while creating 18% new roles in AI oversight, cybersecurity, and green tech. The shift demands policy reforms, corporate accountability, and worker-led AI governance to balance innovation with equity
