FIRSTCRY NYKAA ZOMATO LENSKART DMART CELLO TRENT SWIGGY

The cost is borne by merchants, though they cannot directly pass it to consumers. Retailers with average order values (AOV) above the threshold—such as jewellery, electronics, Nykaa, FirstCry, Lenskart and D‑Mart—face EBITDA hits ranging from ~0.5 % to 2.6 %, while quick‑commerce and food‑delivery firms (Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit) remain largely unaffected because their orders stay below ₹2,000. Companies may absorb the charge, adjust pricing, encourage bill‑splitting or installments, or use other monetisation levers (convenience fees, higher commission rates) to offset the impact. Smaller retailers with sub‑₹2,000 baskets, like The Organic World, can likely absorb the cost without altering payment behavior
