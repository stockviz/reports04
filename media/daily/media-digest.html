Putin Orders Seizure of Nestle and Auchan Assets in Russia

NESTLEIND AEGISCHEM ABMINTLLTD

President Vladimir Putin signed a decree placing the Russian stakes of Swiss food giant Nestlé and French retailer Auchan under temporary administration, handing control to the newly formed company AO L.E.V. Management. This follows Russia’s 2023‑2024 practice of using temporary management to take over foreign-owned assets deemed “unfriendly,” a move previously applied to Danone and Carlsberg. While many multinationals have exited or reduced operations since the 2022 Ukraine war, companies like PepsiCo and Mondelez remain active. Nestlé, operating in Russia since the 1990s, had already scaled back its presence, and its spokesperson said it had not been contacted by Russian authorities. Auchan, with over 230 stores, has not yet commented. The decree highlights growing operational risks for foreign firms operating in Russia

Crude Price Surge Threatens OMC Margins, Refining Gains Offer Relief

HINDPETRO BPCL IOC

A sharp rise in crude prices is set to push retail fuel marketing margins into negative territory in September, with estimated losses of ₹7.4 per litre for petrol and ₹10.3 per litre for diesel. However, gains from the earlier part of the quarter and strong refining margins—averaging $24.5 per barrel—are expected to cushion the impact. OMCs (Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum, Hindustan Petroleum) are projected to achieve marketing margins of ₹2.9 per litre for petrol and ₹1.3 per litre for diesel in the September quarter, improving from the June quarter’s deeper losses. Analysts anticipate overall margins could rise to ₹9‑14 per litre this quarter, offsetting the June quarter’s net losses caused by high crude and freight costs
