India’s Monsoon Deficit Likely to Remain Around 13‑15% – Drought Risks Persist

India’s International Tourist Arrivals Likely to Remain Flat in 2025

INDHOTEL CNL

Foreign tourist arrivals in India are projected to stay flat at 8‑9 million in 2025, down 8.1 % from the previous year and still below pre‑Covid levels. Industry insiders cite higher fuel and air‑ticket prices due to the Iran‑Ukraine conflicts and a lack of global marketing as key factors. While some operators remain pessimistic about the destination’s appeal, others, like Cosmos Travels, hope for a 10‑15 % seasonal rise if travel conditions improve and visa issues with China are resolved after the upcoming BRICS summit. The sector calls for renewed international promotional campaigns to boost India’s tourism potential

Weak Green Hydrogen Demand Delays L&T’s 1 GW Electrolyser Expansion

ATHERENERG LTF LT

Larsen & Toubro has postponed plans to expand its electrolyser manufacturing capacity from the current 400 MW to 1 GW at its Hazira, Gujarat plant, citing weaker-than-expected domestic green hydrogen demand. The company had earmarked Rs 450‑550 crore for the expansion by March 2027 and has already invested Rs 293 crore. While the existing assembly line can meet current demand, L&T’s MD says the facility can be scaled to 1 GW within 12‑18 months if more green hydrogen, ammonia, and methanol projects materialize, noting that such projects typically take 30‑40 months to complete. The electrolyser technology is based on pressurised alkaline systems licensed from France’s McPhy Energy
