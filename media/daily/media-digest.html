India’s Air Travel Boom Fuels Airbus Forecast Boost to 9.3

TIMESGTY

Airbus has raised its projection for India’s domestic air travel growth to 9.3% (up from 8.9%), predicting the country will need 3,480 new passenger aircraft over the next 20 years. The Asia‑Pacific region is expected to account for about 45% of the 42,000 new aircraft required globally, with China needing 8,830 and the rest of the region 6,880. Airbus noted no softening in demand or deliveries despite geopolitical tensions and supply‑chain challenges, while China’s growth forecast was lowered to 4.7% from 5.4%. The outlook underscores India’s position as the fastest‑growing air travel market in the region

RBI Files Caveat in Bombay HC Over Tata Sons Listing Issue

NSIL

The Reserve Bank of India has lodged a caveat petition in the Bombay High Court concerning Tata Sons’ mandatory public listing after the RBI denied the firm’s request to voluntarily surrender its Certificate of Registration, which would have allowed it to be treated as an unregistered Core Investment Company. The caveat ensures RBI can be heard in any court challenge or stay application. RBI has communicated this step to Tata Sons and urged compliance with NBFC‑Upper Layer regulations, emphasizing regulatory oversight and financial stability concerns

India, China to See LNG Demand Rebound After Middle East Supply Crunch Eases

GAIL

Executives at the Gastech conference said the current slowdown in LNG demand in India and China is temporary, caused by soaring spot prices ($30/MMBtu) after the US‑Iran conflict disrupted Qatar and UAE shipments through the Strait of Hormuz. GAIL’s Deepak Gupta noted Indian industries are switching to coal and oil, but the company has restored supply to 90‑95% via alternative sources. PetroChina’s Luo Yizhou said Chinese demand will recover when prices fall to $7‑$9/MMBtu. Both expect demand to pick up as new LNG capacity (150‑200 Mt per year) comes online over the next 4‑5 years and the supply crunch eases

India’s Festive Season Drives Record Consumer Demand Across Sectors

Consumer demand in India surged to a five‑year high during the early festive period (Onam, Rakshabandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi), with strong sales growth in white goods, automobiles, apparel, and e‑commerce. Volume sales of refrigerators, ACs and washing machines rose 12‑15% YoY, while value sales grew 18‑23% despite price hikes. Automakers posted record festive sales, with Maruti Suzuki up 61% and Hyundai nearly doubling sales, prompting capacity expansions. Retail chains and e‑commerce platforms, including quick‑commerce players, reported double‑digit growth. Consumer finance usage increased to offset price inflation, and sentiment improved across tier‑I to tier‑III markets, especially in the premium segment. The momentum is expected to continue through Navratri‑Diwali

German Minister Sets Conditions for UniCredit’s Takeover of Commerzbank

Finance Minister Lars Klingbeil met UniCredit CEO Andrea Orcel, emphasizing that any takeover must preserve Commerzbank’s role in financing German SMEs and protect its 40,000 employees. Berlin, holding a 13% stake, insists the bank remain publicly listed in Frankfurt, retain its SME focus, and keep government representation on the supervisory board. The meeting marks the first time the government has spelled out specific conditions, moving the discussion from a hostile bid to a negotiated path, while UniCredit aims to acquire full control by Q4 pending approvals
