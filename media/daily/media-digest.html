Bankers’ Books Evidence Bill 2026 Modernizes Digital Record Handling

TV18BRDCST

The 2026 Bill replaces a 135‑year‑old law, recognizing electronic bank records—cloud, backups, and other digital formats—as admissible evidence. It requires banks to certify records’ authenticity, integrity, and security, allowing electronic signatures for certification. Courts can summon bank officials only for specific cause, and police access is limited to higher‑ranking officers during investigations. The law also enables future extension to other financial entities, ensuring digital records are reliable and protected in legal proceedings

Meta Ordered to Pay $567 Million in Child‑Safety Case

A New Mexico court mandated Meta (referred to as Mehta) to pay $567 million for harms to youth on Facebook and Instagram, with $420 million earmarked for treatment services and the remainder for awareness, prevention, screening, and referrals over five years. This is the second phase of a landmark trial; the first phase awarded $375 million in civil penalties, bringing total liability to $942 million. The ruling also requires deletion of accounts of users under 13 and their data, though federal COPPA limits further age‑verification measures. The case, brought by Attorney General Raul Torres, alleges that Meta’s platforms foster child predators and mislead users about safety. Meta denies wrongdoing and plans to appeal. This verdict is one of the first jury decisions on platform child safety and may precede numerous similar lawsuits nationwide
