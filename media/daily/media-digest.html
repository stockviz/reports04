SpaceX Announces $100 Billion Louisiana Spaceport for Starship Launches

SpaceX plans to build a massive new rocket launch site in Louisiana, investing $100 billion on 125,000 acres near Vermilion Parish (200 miles west of New Orleans). The site, the company’s fourth and largest, aims to support thousands of annual Starship launches, leveraging natural gas access and diverse trajectories. Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry called it a “transformational” deal, comparing its impact to the Louisiana Purchase.

Construction begins in 2027, with the first launch possible by 2029, creating 3,000 jobs over a decade. However, environmental concerns dominate criticism: advocates like the Louisiana Bucket Brigade warn of irreversible damage to fragile coastal ecosystems, despite SpaceX’s promises to restore land and engage the community via town halls. Gwynne Shotwell (SpaceX COO) emphasized conservation efforts, but critics demand slower, more transparent planning.

The project follows SpaceX’s record-breaking IPO in June 2026, briefly making Elon Musk a trillionaire
