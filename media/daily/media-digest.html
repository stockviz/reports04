Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman says UPI MDR won’t hit consumers

TCIEXP VISACHROME

Sitharaman clarified that the 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate on UPI transactions above ₹2,000 is not a tax, cess or surcharge and is not collected by the government. The fee is levied by NPCI, POS providers and merchant banks, and will be borne by merchants, similar to card‑payment MDRs, without being passed on to customers. The finance ministry will coordinate with the Indian Banks Association and traders’ bodies to ensure the charge remains with merchants, while discussions continue on whether GST should apply to the MDR. The MDR applies only to P2M UPI payments over ₹2,000 (capped at ₹300 for large transactions) and exemptes smaller transactions and low‑volume merchants

Radisson’s “India Booster” Plan Aims for 500 Hotels by 2030

HOTELRUGBY ABMINTLLTD

Radisson Hotel Group’s COO Chema Basterrechea announced an “India booster” strategy to accelerate expansion and reach 500 hotels in India by 2030, building on its 30‑year presence and current pipeline of about 250 properties. While other brands target the mid‑scale segment, Radisson relies on its strong brand awareness and owner relationships, emphasizing organic growth and selective affiliations—no public‑market listing or major inorganic deals. The GCC region faces delays due to recent conflicts, though Saudi operations remain relatively stable. The group is increasing investment in AI‑driven content and direct booking technology, but stresses that hospitality remains a people‑centric business

AI Agents Become Fintechs’ Retention Weapon in India

India’s leading payment providers—Razorpay, Cashfree, BharatPe and PayU—are rolling out AI‑driven agent suites to automate merchants’ back‑office tasks such as cart recovery, dispute handling, KYC, loan advisory and chargeback automation. Launched initially for free, these tools aim to make merchants “sticky” by boosting top‑line revenue (15‑30% reported by Razorpay), lowering acquisition costs and enabling cross‑selling of financial products. While they promise efficiency and higher take rates amid thin margins and zero‑MDR UPI, they also risk creating walled‑garden ecosystems that could lock smaller merchants into a single platform. Experts note the trade‑off between convenience and lock‑in will hinge on the interoperability of these AI‑powered ecosystems

Dutch PM Seeks to Thwart New US Export Restrictions on ASML Amid China & Russia Tensions

FEDDERSHOL

Dutch Prime Minister Rob Jetten told Bloomberg that he has discussed the “MATCH Act” with President Donald Trump and is confident a workable compromise can be reached. The proposed legislation would extend U.S. export controls to prohibit sales of ASML’s deep‑ultraviolet immersion lithography tools to China, adding to existing bans on the company’s extreme‑ultraviolet (EUV) machines. ASML, the sole EUV supplier, is critical to the global AI and semiconductor ecosystem, and China’s share of its Q2 sales fell from 19 % to 14 %. Jetten also urged tougher sanctions on Russia to pressure President Vladimir Putin into peace talks, criticizing recent EU moves to ease sanctions on certain Russian oligarchs. The overall message stresses safeguarding national security while maintaining trade relations in the semiconductor sector

Streaming Overtakes TV in India’s Content Investment for First Time

RBA

In 2025, online video captured 46% of India’s content investment, surpassing TV’s 42%, marking the first such shift, per Media Partners Asia’s Asia Video Content Dynamics 2026 report. India’s total video content spend hit $5 billion, part of a $14.8 billion regional pool (India and Korea account for ~80%). Streaming growth is driven by massive demand—420 billion streamed hours—and platforms like Jio Hotstar, which holds 58% of premium VOD viewership and 180 million paying subscribers. Sports, especially cricket during IPL 2026, boosted connected‑TV reach. Local films are also thriving, with India’s box office at $1.41 billion and strong performances in Vietnam and Indonesia. While TV still commands ~60% of regional investment, its budgets are shrinking as streaming and film absorb incremental funds. The industry is moving toward consolidation, with notable M&A activity in India and proposed mergers in Korea, while Southeast Asia lags but presents opportunities. Management quality, cost rationalisation, AI adoption, and protection of differentiated content are seen as critical for future success
