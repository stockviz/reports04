India’s Gold Import Landscape Shifts to Domestic Refining and UAE Channels

CELLO PFOCUS BCG JARO CRISIL PACIFICI

India is diversifying its gold supply by favoring semi‑refined “dor” imports and boosting local refining capacity, spurred by a 0.65% duty advantage for refiners. The India‑UAE CEPA, effective May 2022, grants a 1% tariff concession on UAE gold via a quota that will rise to 200 tonnes by 2027, now covering about a quarter of imports. Consequently, Switzerland’s share of bullion imports has dropped from 42.4% to 21.9% while the UAE’s share rose from 10.8% to 16.5%. Imports of dor from Ghana, Chile and Peru have also increased. Critics warn the UAE route may merely re‑route gold from other countries to exploit lower duties, urging stricter rules of origin and exclusion of precious metals from future FTAs. The shift aims to enhance domestic value addition, support the jewellery sector, and protect India’s trade balance and foreign‑exchange reserves
