SEBI Reverts F&O Expiry Settlement to 30‑Minute VWAP Window

BSE

SEBI has announced that, from early December, the F&O expiry settlement will again use a 30‑minute volume‑weighted average price (VWAP) calculated between 3:30 pm and 3:40 pm, after the cash market closes. Index derivatives will stop trading at 3:30 pm on expiry days (instead of 3:45 pm). The earlier consultation paper’s one‑year interim provision has been dropped, giving SEBI flexibility to adjust the rule later. The change aims to improve premium decay, enhance liquidity, and strengthen cash‑market price discovery, though it may reduce cash‑market volume on expiry days. Exchanges (NSE and BSE) are expected to adapt: BSE’s thin book should benefit, while NSE’s already thick book may see minimal impact. Overall, the move is viewed as a positive step for derivatives traders and market stability
