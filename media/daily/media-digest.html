ICICI Securities’ Oil & Gas Outlook: Crude, Refining Margins & Sector Plays

MRPL ONGC GUJENERGY BPCL GUJGASLTD ISEC IGL OIL MGL

  1. Crude Oil Prices & Near-Term Outlook:
    • Crude prices are rising steadily, with ICICI Securities expecting $90–$95/bbl in the near term due to geopolitical uncertainty (e.g., Gulf disputes, Russia-Ukraine conflict).
    • Full-year average estimate: $85–$90/bbl, with potential moderation in H2 if tensions ease.
    • No clear “cap” on prices amid conflicting views on crude flows through the Strait of Hormuz.
  2. Refining Margins:
    • Diesel spreads hit record highs (near $80–$90 vs. WPI crude at $85), straining product prices.
    • Refining margins remain elevated ($30+ theoretically) but are capped for Indian refiners due to:
      • Export taxes (SAD): Government adjustments to control margins.
      • Unofficial RTP (Retail Selling Price) cap: Negotiated pricing limits margins to $12–$15 (vs. theoretical $30).
    • Q2 refining margins expected to stay in $12–$15 range (e.g., BPCL at $41, HPCL at $23–$24 were outliers excluding tax impacts).
    • Russia-Ukraine conflict reduced global refining capacity by 2–2.5 million bbl/day, tightening product balances.
  3. Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs):
    • Constructive outlook but 12–18 month play; FY27 earnings under stress.
    • Refining margins strong, but LPG losses (Q1 losses ~₹1,000 crore) and tight LPG availability could drag integrated margins.
    • Retail margins improved in Q2 vs. Q1, but high product prices may reverse gains.
    • Key risk: LPG under-recoveries and export tax adjustments limiting upside.
  4. Producers (ONGC, Oil India):
    • Oil India preferred pick due to:
      • Oil production growth: Targeting 1 million tonnes/quarter (vs. ~0.95 MT in Q1), aiming for 4 MT/year (up from 3.2–3.3 MT).
      • Gas production: Potential 50% jump to 12 MMSCMD by FY28 (from ~8 MMSCMD) with Indradhanush gas grid and pipeline expansions.
      • Higher realizations: New gas eligible for $10/MMBTU (vs. $7.2–7.3 domestically) under new pricing.
      • NRL expansion: EBITDA could rise to ₹7,500–8,500 crore at $25 GRM (including excise benefits).
    • ONGC: No specific mention; focus on Oil India’s execution.
  5. City Gas Distribution (CGD) Companies:
    • Government’s 200 SCM gas allocation to boost piped gas connections will help IGL/MGL (struggling with LNG shortages post-Qatar Energy issues).
    • Gujarat Gas (GEL) outlook:
      • Short-term headwinds: Morbi LNG dependence (volumes fell to 3 MMSCMD from 5.7 MMSCMD due to LPG/propane availability).
      • Long-term potential: Valuations attractive; 11–12% CNG growth and domestic penetration (new CGD regulations) could drive recovery.
      • FY27 volatility: Earnings dependent on propane vs. gas economics

INDIGOPNTS BIRLACABLE 3MINDIA ASIANPAINT BERGEPAINT

Indigo Paints Ltd is aggressively pursuing 25%+ annual revenue growth—outpacing the industry’s 13–14% expansion—to gain market share, even at the cost of margins. With just 3% of India’s decorative paints market, the company targets 10 percentage points faster growth than competitors, prioritizing top-line expansion over short-term profitability. Managing Director Hemant Jalan acknowledges a gross margin dip (44.6% in Q1 FY27 vs. industry average 41.5%) due to increased spending on trade promotions and influencers, after cutting marketing costs earlier to manage raw material volatility.

Despite funding expansion without equity raises, the company faces challenges from deep discounting by rivals like Birla Opus and its smaller scale/younger brand (25 years old vs. peers’ century-long presence).

Analysts at ICICI Securities expect Indigo’s market-share-led growth to continue in FY27–28, though consumer price relief may delay until post-Diwali if raw material costs stabilize. Succession planning is underway, with Jalan (68) stepping back from daily operations and a CEO announcement expected in 12–18 months; his son, Parag Jalan, will not take an executive role.

Stock performance has lagged in 2026 (down 3% vs. 4% drop in Asian Paints and 10% in Sensex), reflecting investor skepticism amid its **growth-over-margins strategy
