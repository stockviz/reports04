Key Highlights: - Valuation & Fundamentals: Hindustan Copper is India’s only listed pure copper miner, trading at ~50x trailing EPS but expected to re-rate as earnings grow (EPS projected to rise from ₹10 to ₹30+ by FY27-FY28). Lenders are urged to intensify micro‑level monitoring and credit‑bureau checks to manage potential spillover defaults.
