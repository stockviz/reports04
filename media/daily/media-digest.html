Milky Mist IPO Highlights: Value‑Added Dairy Strategy and Growth Outlook

Milky Mist has built its business around selling only high‑margin, value‑added dairy products—paneer, cheese, ghee, ice cream, chocolates—while never retailing raw milk. This focus has driven 31% revenue CAGR over two years, margin expansion to ~14%, and a 6.5‑times profit jump from FY24 to FY26. The company sources milk from about 75,000 farmers (33,000 women) and operates a single, large integrated plant in South India with 190 t/day paneer capacity, currently running at 35‑45% utilization, leaving ample headroom.

Key advantages include end‑to‑end logistics from villages to retailers, first‑mover packaging of paneer, and a diversified product portfolio that cushions milk‑price volatility. Milky Mist holds ~19% pan‑India paneer market share (60%+ in the South) and sees strong growth in Greek yogurt, specialty cheeses, and clean‑label products.

Future plans: a second 50‑acre facility in Baramati (3‑4 years), continued pan‑India expansion, and leveraging its 140+ franchise stores while maintaining presence across all trade channels (general, modern, HoReCa, D2C). Debt will be reduced by ~₹600 cr from IPO proceeds, targeting ~₹800‑₹850 cr low‑cost debt (3% interest). Promoter holding post‑offer will be ~79%. The company expects sustained ~30% top‑line growth, margin improvement through operational leverage, and aims to capture rising per‑capita dairy consumption (target 600‑700 ml/day) driven by protein‑rich, low‑fat consumer preferences

The contract, awarded in June 2026 to Power Expectations with ERock and Reyes Contractor listed as partners, may be reassigned to Flotek Industries. The board is investigating the misrepresentation, reviewing the contract’s delayed execution, and weighing a referral to authorities
