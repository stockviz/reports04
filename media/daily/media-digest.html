Major Lawsuits Against Social Media Companies Over Youth Harm

States such as California, Colorado, Kentucky, New Jersey and New Mexico have filed multistate actions seeking billions in damages and operational changes, while individual cases—from a $6 million verdict against Meta and YouTube to settlements with school districts—highlight claims of addiction, suicide, and drug‑related harms. Buffett’s Wisdom vs. Advisor Reality: When to Follow and When to Diverge

Financial advisors respect Warren Buffett’s buy‑and‑hold, low‑fee, and compounding principles, but they caution against blindly copying his stock picks or cash‑heavy strategy. While Buffett’s historic success stemmed from value investing in less efficient markets, today’s growth‑driven era often outperforms that style. Advisors like Ben Carlson note Buffett’s influence in encouraging buying dips, yet emphasize tailoring decisions to client goals, risk tolerance, and timing. Samantha Garcia prioritizes client objectives over mimicking Buffett’s Apple holding, and Thomas Kopelman warns that excessive cash positions, even at Berkshire levels, can erode returns and lose to inflation. On personal spending, advisors appreciate frugality but reject extreme thrift that doesn’t suit high‑net‑worth clients. Looking ahead, they doubt another figure will match Buffett’s blend of intellect and morality; integrity remains the key trait for any future “next Buffett

Renewable Energy Developers Get Extension Relief Under New CERC Rules

HEROMOTOCO

The Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) has revised its General Network Access (GNA) regulations, allowing renewable energy developers more time to secure interstate transmission connectivity if they demonstrate progress and pay daily extension fees. Extensions include up to 3 months for land acquisition (with at least 20% land documentation), up to 6 months for financial closure, and up to 12 months for commissioning, with escalating charges of ₹1,000–₹1,300 per MW per day. Failure to meet milestones within the granted extensions will result in revocation of connectivity. The changes aim to reduce regulatory cliff risk and alleviate bottlenecks caused by delayed projects
