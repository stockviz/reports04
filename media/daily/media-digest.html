SDKs Poised to Dominate the Next Wave of AI‑Native Advertising

The Analysys Mason report highlights that SDKs have become the critical control point in the $400 billion in‑app ad market, giving independent adtech firms like AppLovin, InMobi and Liftoff a strategic edge as advertising expands into AI assistants, lock‑screens and agentic commerce. With web‑based targeting hampered by cookie restrictions, ATT opt‑ins, and the retirement of Google’s Privacy Sandbox, first‑party data collected via SDKs offers the most reliable targeting. SDK footprint—covering up to 44 % of Android and 31 % of iOS downloads—now forms the primary competitive moat, as shown by AppLovin’s $5.48 billion ad revenue in 2025 and its $1.05 billion MoPub acquisition focused on footprint rather than tech. While Google, Meta and Amazon still dominate global ad spend, in‑app advertising remains fragmented, with publishers receiving roughly half of ad spend. As AI products scale, their inference costs will likely be subsidized by ads, and because this inventory lacks traditional web ad tags, platforms with extensive SDK presence are best positioned to capture the emerging AI‑native ad supply
