Rising chipset and memory costs have pushed larger brands away, while smaller players fill the gap using lower‑cost RAM chips. AI‑Driven Production Costs Slash Could Upend Traditional Entertainment Studios

BALAJITELE AAATECH

AI is set to transform the Indian entertainment landscape by dramatically lowering production costs, enabling creators and smaller studios to generate more content and retain IP ownership. Companies such as JioStar, Balaji Telefilms, and Kuku Technologies are already integrating generative AI into production pipelines, with JioStar launching a full‑stack GenAI studio and releasing an AI‑crafted Mahabharat series. In audio, Pocket FM reports a drop from $2,500 to $30 per hour of drama, allowing 100 hours of content to be produced in a day instead of a year. Over 550,000 creators now produce 2.5 million hours of AI‑powered audio on the platform. This cost compression could diminish reliance on broadcasters, OTT platforms, and short‑video apps, fostering a surge in innovative, low‑budget long‑form and micro‑drama content across India

Flipkart to Hire 250,000 Workers for Festive Season Surge

Flipkart plans to add over 250,000 direct and indirect employees, including 75,000 first‑time workers, to boost delivery capacity for the upcoming festive sales. About 60% of the new roles will be in last‑mile delivery, with women making up 20% of its supply‑chain staff, and the company will open 900 new delivery hubs and create roughly 140,000 additional last‑mile jobs to support its “Big Billion Days” promotions
