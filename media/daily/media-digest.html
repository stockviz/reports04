Walmart vows no personalized pricing with digital shelf tags

Walmart announced that its new digital price labels will not use personal data such as income or shopping history to set prices, pledging consistent pricing regardless of customer identity or time of day. CEO John Furner emphasized the commitment extends to its AI shopping assistant, Sparky, and aligns with the retailer’s “everyday low prices” model. The rollout aims to replace paper tags, allowing instant price updates across stores, while the FTC has warned that personalized pricing could raise consumer‑protection concerns and must be disclosed if used

Fortis Disavows Involvement in Daiichi‑Sankyo Arbitration Dispute

FORTIS

Fortis Healthcare stated it had no role in the arbitration between Daiichi Sankyo and the Singh Brothers and was never a party to the proceedings. The Supreme Court, in its September 25 order, dismissed Fortis’s Special Leave Petition against the Delhi High Court’s directive for a forensic audit, noting that the High Court’s observations about Fortis were tentative and would not affect the independent audit. The court also clarified that Fortis is not a judgment debtor or garnishee of Daiichi Sankyo’s decree, has no control over share transfers by former promoters, received no proceeds from those transfers, and that the Singh Brothers’ shareholding fell below 1% by March 2018, after which independent directors were appointed by institutional shareholders

Baume & Mercier CEO Declares India as Prime Investment Destination Amid Trade Pact Benefits

Swiss luxury watchmaker Baume & Mercier’s CEO Michael Guenoun says the India‑EFTA trade agreement and tariff cuts create a strong long‑term case for investment despite a weak rupee and strong Swiss franc. Swiss watch exports to India rose 31.7% YoY from Jan‑Aug 2026. The brand now has about 50 stores in ~20 Indian cities, with major sales in Mumbai and Delhi and growing traction in second‑tier markets. Local ambassadors Janhvi Kapoor and Shreyas Iyer underscore the focus, while Indian buyers favor gold aesthetics and automatic/mechanical watches. Guenoun notes industry‑wide pricing pressures and lack of differentiation, but Baume & Mercier aims to boost quality and features without aggressive price hikes

Individual Creators Become Key IPs for Indian Streamers

PFOCUS

Indian streaming platforms are turning to individual digital creators—such as Samay Raina, Bhuvan Bam, and Zakir Khan—as valuable intellectual properties with built‑in audiences. This shift helps streamers mitigate the risk and cost of launching new shows, leverages creators’ YouTube reach for distribution, and diversifies content across regional, youth, comedy, gaming, lifestyle and niche segments. Platforms like Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, and Jio‑Hotstar (through its Sparks vertical) are already airing creator‑driven series, signaling a new wave of content creation driven by creator‑led IPs

India’s TV Industry Faces Shrinking Audience and Regulatory Uncertainty

TATACAP ZEEL GTPL SUNSHINE AIRTELPP

India’s Rs 60,000‑crore television broadcasting sector is grappling with a steep decline in viewers as OTT platforms grow, prompting broadcasters (JioStar, Zee, Sony) and DTH/cable operators (Tata Play, Airtel, GTPL) to seek regulatory forbearance on pricing, packaging, and licence fees. They await decisions from the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting and TRAI on tariff flexibility, renewal of BARC’s licence, regulation of application‑based linear TV (ALTD/FAST) services, and reduction of DTH licence fees. The pay‑DTH subscriber base fell 13.8% year‑on‑year, raising concerns about revenue, content investment, employment, and rural access. Industry bodies urge TRAI to grant parity with OTT platforms, streamline BARC’s registration, and finalize pending fee reductions to provide certainty and support the sector’s survival

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei to Dine with President Trump Amid AI Safety Clash

Anthropic’s chief executive Dario Amodei is scheduled to meet President Donald Trump for dinner, marking the first private encounter between the two after months of friction over AI policy. The meeting comes as AI safety concerns rise following high‑profile security breaches, prompting calls for stricter regulations. Amodei has warned of existential AI risks and advocated for slower development, while Trump dismisses such fears and opposes any slowdown. Their dinner follows earlier disputes involving Pentagon contracts and Commerce Department export controls on Anthropic’s Mythos model, and precedes a larger gathering of AI CEOs hosted by Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson. The contrast between Amodei’s safety‑first stance and Trump’s pro‑development agenda underscores the growing divide over the future of artificial intelligence in the United States

Costly Diwali: Appliance Makers Announce Price Hikes Ahead of Festive Season

LGEINDIA BOSCH-HCIL HAVELLS 3MINDIA GODREJAGRO VOLTAS

Leading Indian appliance manufacturers—including LG, Daikin, Samsung, Voltas, Bosch, Havells, and Godrej—are raising prices by 4‑10% from October 1 due to sharp increases in raw‑material costs (copper up 34%, steel 24%, aluminium 16%, resin 17%) and higher logistics, energy, and FX expenses. This marks the third hike this year, bringing cumulative price rises to 16‑18% since January, and could dampen sales during the crucial Navratri‑Diwali consumption window that typically accounts for 25‑30% of annual demand. Companies cite limited ability to absorb costs and aim to maintain product quality and supply stability
