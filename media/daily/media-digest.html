Weak Green Hydrogen Demand Delays L&T’s 1 GW Electrolyser Expansion

ATHERENERG LTF LT

Larsen & Toubro has postponed plans to expand its electrolyser manufacturing capacity from the current 400 MW to 1 GW at its Hazira, Gujarat plant, citing weaker-than-expected domestic green hydrogen demand. The company had earmarked Rs 450‑550 crore for the expansion by March 2027 and has already invested Rs 293 crore. While the existing assembly line can meet current demand, L&T’s MD says the facility can be scaled to 1 GW within 12‑18 months if more green hydrogen, ammonia, and methanol projects materialize, noting that such projects typically take 30‑40 months to complete. The electrolyser technology is based on pressurised alkaline systems licensed from France’s McPhy Energy
