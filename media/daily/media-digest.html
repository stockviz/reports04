eMudhra Exits SSL/TLS Business After Key Vulnerability, No Data Breach

EMUDHRA

Digital authentication firm eMudhra announced it will voluntarily withdraw from its global SSL/TLS certificate business after a security researcher identified weaker-than‑intended cryptographic keys in eight issuing certificate authorities, which could be reverse‑engineered. Companies are increasingly turning to law firms for guidance on board composition, independent director appointments, and handling disagreements as corporate governance gains prominence and independent director resignations hit a five‑year high. While regulation sets structures, a strong governance culture ultimately depends on board leadership

UBS Wins Arbitration Cases Against Former Credit Suisse Executives Over Deferred Compensation Write-Downs

ASHIKA BCG

UBS successfully defended its decision to write down contingent capital award certificates (CCAs)—deferred compensation tied to Credit Suisse’s additional tier 1 (AT1) instruments—after the 2023 government-brokered merger. Arbitrators rejected claims by former managing directors Martin Ego and Jeffrey Jennings, who argued the write-downs were unjustified since Credit Suisse met capital requirements during its crisis.

  • Key Points:
    • CCAs (modeled after AT1s) were designed to be written down in viability events, but Ego and Jennings contended no such event occurred.
    • Arbitrators ruled broadly that capital adequacy includes a bank’s ability to absorb losses and operate under stress, justifying the write-downs.
    • UBS seeks court confirmation of these rulings, while a separate arbitrator awarded $1M to former exec Matt Mitchell—a decision UBS is challenging.
    • Switzerland’s top court recently blocked payouts to 1,000+ former employees who lost bonuses post-rescue, aligning with UBS’s stance.
    • UBS faces ongoing legal battles over AT1 bondholder claims (dismissed in U.S. courts) and $300M in contingent liabilities (down from $1.5B), with integration nearing completion.

Outcome: Yes, CCAs were correctly written down as part of the rescue
