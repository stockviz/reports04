Jeff Walton Discusses Strive’s Bitcoin‑Backed Perpetual Preferred Equity, Tokenization, and Market Outlook

In a CNBC TV 18 interview, Strive’s Chief Risk Officer Jeff Walton explains how the firm models Bitcoin’s volatility on its balance sheet by issuing a perpetual preferred equity security (SEDA) that underwrites risk and offers low‑volatility exposure. Strive holds over 24,000 BTC, $200 M cash, and $50 M marketable securities, enabling it to pay a daily dividend yielding 13% annually. Two instruments are offered: a stable‑yield preferred equity for investors seeking steady returns and a common equity with roughly 2× Bitcoin beta for higher‑risk, higher‑return exposure. Walton highlights tokenization as the future of finance, noting that SEDA is already tokenized across platforms, allowing algorithmic trading and broader investor access. He notes the firm’s openness to global capital, including Indian institutional investors, and says regulatory clarity—especially around US Senate action and stablecoin rules—won’t alter Strive’s current capital‑market‑compliant structure but would benefit the broader crypto ecosystem. Stablecoins are expected to keep growing, with potential integration of tokenized assets and yield‑bearing features pending regulatory outcomes
