Currently in pre‑clinical development, the novel therapy aims to address unmet ophthalmic needs, though further pre‑clinical and clinical studies are required to confirm safety and efficacy. The company’s CEO highlighted the achievement as evidence of India’s growing capability in original drug innovation, while an expert from Sarojini Devi Eye Hospital noted the scientific interest in repurposing insulin for eye treatment and emphasized the need for rigorous validation
