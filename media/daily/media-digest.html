Reliance’s Massive Coal‑to‑Gas Initiative Targets Fuel Import Reduction

ADANIENPP1 RELIANCE NTPC

Reliance Industries plans to invest about ₹43,000 crore in a coal‑to‑gas plant in Andhra Pradesh, with a first phase of ₹3,000 crore for feasibility studies and a second phase of ₹40,000 crore for full‑scale operation. The project could attract up to ₹2.7 lakh crore in total investments and create roughly 5,000 direct and 35,000 indirect jobs. It aligns with India’s push to expand coal gasification to lower reliance on imported fuels and chemicals, complementing other major investments in the state such as Google’s AI hub and Kia’s manufacturing plant

Rival Gig Platforms Collaborate on Worker Welfare Under India’s New Social Security Board**

The Indian government has formed a 22-member National Social Security Board—including competitors like Swiggy, Zepto, and Eternal—to design welfare schemes for gig and platform workers under the Social Security Code. The board will recommend benefits such as health insurance, maternity support, accident/disability coverage, life insurance, and old-age protection, with platforms potentially contributing 1–2% of turnover (capped at 5% of worker payments).

Key challenges include ensuring portable benefits for workers switching platforms or cities and streamlining record-keeping. The board’s effectiveness hinges on addressing these gaps to guarantee seamless access to benefits. Meetings are mandated at least **three times yearly

Pricol promoters sell entire stake in demerged connected‑vehicle unit to Warburg Pincus

PRICOLLTD DAMCAPITAL

Pricol Limited’s board approved the sale of its 38.51% promoter holding in the newly demerged Pricol Autotech Ltd (DICVS business) to private‑equity firm Warburg Pincus for up to Rs 1,663 crore. Warburg will make a mandatory open offer for an additional 26% of shares, becoming the largest shareholder. The DICVS segment, accounting for about 60% of Pricol’s FY26 revenue (~Rs 2,400 crore), covers driver information and automotive electronics. The demerger aims to give Pricol Autotech operational focus and growth, with Warburg’s expertise and resources supporting its expansion in India and abroad. Veda Corporate Advisors advised the transaction

BYD Gains Indian Homologation, Boosts EV Imports and Luxury Push

BYD India secured ARAI certification for three of its four EV models, lifting the 2,500‑unit annual import cap and allowing greater sales flexibility, especially for the fast‑selling Sealion 7 SUV. FY‑26 EV sales rose 54% to 5,361 units amid a booming Indian market. The move comes as India‑China ties ease, though investment restrictions remain. BYD also launched its luxury Denza brand (D9 MPV and Z9 GT) in six major cities, while deciding later whether to homologate these models. The expanded import freedom gives BYD a commercial edge despite lacking local assembly

Adani Green Refutes Cost‑Reduction Allegations for Tarali Pumped Storage Project

ADANIGREEN

Adani Green Energy Limited denied claims it tried to cut the Tarali PSP’s cost to sidestep statutory and conservation obligations. The company clarified that the Rs 56.75 crore figure shown on the forest‑wildlife portal pertains only to site‑investigation and survey expenses—about 1 % of the total Rs 5,675 crore project cost considered by the State Board for Wildlife. It also rejected assertions that the revision aimed to lower conservation outlays. The firm emphasized that the project is entirely on private, non‑forest land, located 3.2 km from Koyna Wildlife Sanctuary, outside the Sahyadri Tiger Reserve buffer and any notified eco‑sensitive zones, and therefore does not affect protected areas

SpaceX Targets First Full Rocket Recovery with Double Catch Test Flight

BLUESTARCO

SpaceX plans a 22‑hour orbital test of Starship that will attempt to catch both the massive booster and the spacecraft mid‑air using arms on the launch tower, marking the first time every stage of an orbital rocket would be retrieved for reuse. The flight, approved by the FAA, will carry 26 Starlink satellites, complete 15 Earth orbits at about 170 mi altitude, and return for a dual-stage recovery—booster back to its Texas pad six minutes after launch and the spacecraft the following day. Success would demonstrate complete reusability, accelerating launch cadence for lunar bases, Mars missions, and future NASA Artemis contracts, while previous tests have only recovered boosters or lost spacecraft

Nvidia Taps Former Arm CEO Rene Haas as Potential Successor to Jensen Huang

AHL AMDIND

Nvidia’s board is weighing a range of internal and external candidates—including finance chief Colette Kress, engineer Ian Buck, AMD’s Lisa Su, and others—to replace Jensen Huang after his abrupt resignation. Former Arm CEO Rene Haas, who spent seven years at Nvidia and now leads Arm, is among the top prospects. The board must decide whether to prioritize financial continuity, deep technical expertise, or broader global leadership experience as it navigates heightened regulatory scrutiny and AI market pressures. Interim CEO Colette Kress is currently steering the company

Asda Nears End of Losing Streak as New CEO Takes Helm

5PAISA

After a 0.1% dip in comparable sales for the 13 weeks to Sept. 30, Asda’s modest decline hints at a possible turnaround. Online grocery sales rose 6.9% and convenience store sales grew 2.7%, helped by the revamped Asda Rewards loyalty scheme. The chain, now led by newly appointed CEO David Potts, faces fierce competition from Tesco, Sainsbury’s, Aldi and Lidl, and a shrinking market share that fell from 14.3% in early 2021 to 11.3% today. Burdened by debt from its 2021 leveraged buyout and a troubled IT split from Walmart, Asda aims to regain growth by emphasizing value for money amid tightening household budgets
