Airlines Accelerate Premium Seat Expansion as Business-Class Demand Surpasses Economy

MCDOWELL-N DELTACORP

US airlines are rapidly adding premium seating, with a 33% rise in premium seats since 2020—more than double the growth of economy seats. Carriers such as American, United, Delta, JetBlue, and British Airways are introducing upgraded cabins, deluxe meals by Michelin-star chefs, and enhanced lounge experiences to capture higher‑margin passengers. Premium seats occupy more space but fetch five‑to‑six times the price, now generating about half of ticket revenue on roughly 30% of seats. The trend is driven by strong demand for comfort, despite higher jet‑fuel costs, and airlines project that premium travelers could comprise up to 70% of passengers on some flights. New offerings include paid extra‑legroom, middle‑seat‑free Economy Plus, and first‑class products like JetBlue’s BlueFirst. Industry analysts expect the premium segment to continue outpacing economy growth
