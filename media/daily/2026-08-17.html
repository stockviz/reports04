LT

Ola Electric Launches ‘Shakti’ Energy Storage Portfolio for Homes, Businesses, and Grids

OLAELEC

Ola Electric announced its ‘Shakti’ line of energy storage solutions built on in‑house LFP battery technology, covering residential (Shakti Gen2), commercial (Shakti AC/DC Rack) and utility‑scale (Mahashakti) markets. The 4.6 kWh and 9.2 kWh home units are priced at ₹99,999 and ₹1,74,999 respectively, with deliveries from November 2026. Further specs, pricing, and partnership details will be released later

Hands‑On AI Masterclass Empowers Engineering Students with Future‑Ready Skills

HCLTECH TIMESGTY

HCLTech and Economic Times hosted an eight‑hour online AI masterclass for about 300 engineering students from 70 colleges on 8 August. The program covered AI‑assisted coding with GitHub Copilot, fundamentals of large language models, embeddings, Retrieval‑Augmented Generation, and AI agents using LangChain, LangGraph, and AWS Bedrock. Emphasis was placed on problem framing, business context, engineering judgment, and building projects rather than passive learning. Students explored sustainability, data sovereignty, and AI infrastructure, and were required to submit two AI projects for certification. The initiative aims to equip students with practical AI skills and a mindset of continuous learning

KFC denies stale‑chicken claims, cites compliance with FSSAI norms

KFC refuted allegations that it served stale chicken at its Mangaluru outlet, stating that its chicken is sourced from reputable suppliers, stored and prepared daily according to FSSAI and global standards, and undergoes multiple safety checks. Following a consumer complaint, Karnataka food‑safety officials inspected the restaurant, collected samples for lab testing, and ordered the outlet to clear its chicken stock and clean cold storage. KFC said it is cooperating with authorities and invited customers to tour its kitchen

Hero MotoCorp Appoints Srihari Mulgund as Chief Strategy Officer

HEROMOTOCO

Hero MotoCorp Limited announced the appointment of Srihari Mulgund, a veteran with over 24 years in global automotive engineering, product development and strategy, as its Chief Strategy Officer, classifying him as senior management under stock‑exchange rules. Visakhapatnam Airport Closure Sparks Worker Unrest and Mixed Passenger Reactions

INDIGO

The historic Visakhapatnam airport ceased operations at midnight, with flights moving to the new Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport. Long‑serving staff, including 15‑year‑veteran housekeeping workers, voiced anxiety over job security, unpaid wages, and lack of response from authorities and political leaders. Passengers reminisced about the convenience and emotional attachment to the old airport, while others welcomed the shift as a step toward the city’s growth, better infrastructure, and international connectivity, though concerns remain about the new airport’s distance and accessibility

Yotta Targets $1.5 Billion Fundraise Ahead of India IPO

RELIABLE ADANIENPP1 BCG RELIANCE

Yotta Data Services aims to raise $1.5 billion by FY27 through a pre‑IPO round and a potential IPO of up to $400 million in Q4, pending SEBI approval. The company, co‑founded in 2019, plans to shift its listing from the US to India to align with its sovereign‑cloud strategy serving government and private sectors. With 180 MW of data‑centre capacity, Yotta seeks to expand to 800 MW and boost Nvidia GPU installations from 37,000 to 85,000, part of a broader $8.5 billion capital plan through 2029. The raise comes amid a surge in Indian data‑centre investment driven by regulatory mandates, 5G rollout, and AI demand, with peers like Nxtra and CtrlS also securing large funds. Yotta’s model focuses on GPU‑intensive AI infrastructure, requiring significant investment beyond traditional data‑centre assets

Mahindra’s Global Expansion Boosted by Chinese Carmakers’ Market Inroads

SONACOMS MOTHERSON M&M EICHERMOT MARUTI HYUNDAI

Mahindra & Mahindra’s automotive head says the growing presence of Chinese brands in markets such as Africa, Latin America and Europe has softened consumer attitudes toward new entrants, creating an opening for Mahindra’s improved safety‑rated and cleaner‑powertrain vehicles. The company is targeting South Africa, the UK, Australia, New Zealand, Brazil and other Latin American markets with its new Scorpio Lifestyler pickup and upcoming EVs, leveraging its global EV platform to meet fuel‑efficiency and emission rules. While the strategy relies on organic growth rather than partnerships, experts caution that high standards for safety, quality, after‑sales service and local market insight remain essential for success
