CBI Files FIR Against Anil Ambani and Reliance Capital Over EPFO Loss

RELCAPITAL

The Central Bureau of Investigation has lodged a First Information Report against Reliance Capital Ltd. and former chairman Anil D. Ambani for allegedly causing a loss of ₹1,816.22 crore to the Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). The loss stems from EPFO’s investment of ₹2,500 crore in secured non‑convertible debentures (NCDs) issued by Reliance Capital in 2013‑14, which later turned sour amid suspected fraudulent activities. The Ministry of Labour and Employment, acting on evidence from the Enforcement Directorate and a BDO India audit, alleges cheating, criminal conspiracy, and diversion of funds by the company’s management. Ambani, who served as non‑executive chairman until the RBI superseded the board in 2021, denies any wrongdoing. The FIR follows a complaint that cites irregular lending, fund diversion, and impairment of security that may have contributed to the financial deterioration of Reliance Capital and the EPFO loss

Higher Tobacco Taxes Hit Indian Cigarette Makers’ Q1 Results

ITC

The Indian government’s February 2024 GST hike to 40% and new excise duties (Rs 2,100‑8,500 per 1,000 sticks) have squeezed the country’s three dominant cigarette producers—ITC, Godfrey Phillips India, and VST Industries. While reported revenues rose due to duty pass‑through, underlying net revenues and profits fell sharply. ITC’s gross cigarette revenue dropped 31.5% despite a 73.7% rise in total segment revenue, reflecting lower volumes. Godfrey Phillips saw net profit plunge 44.3% and net revenue fall 18.8% after excluding excise. VST Industries’ net revenue fell 13.5% and volume slipped 14%, with profit down 24.4%. All firms resorted to calibrated price hikes and portfolio shifts to mitigate the impact of the unprecedented tax increase
