REITs vs REIT Mutual Funds: Key Insights for Portfolio Building

SANDESH TVVISION EDELWEISS

The discussion on MF Corner highlighted the fundamentals of REITs—listed trusts owning income‑generating commercial assets—and introduced India’s first REIT‑focused index mutual fund, which blends multiple REITs with real‑estate developer stocks. Benefits of the fund include diversification, lower entry barriers, and a benchmark index, while drawbacks involve sector concentration, interest‑rate sensitivity, and potential overlap with existing REIT holdings. Experts emphasized evaluating occupancy rates, tenant quality, debt metrics, and Net Asset Value (NAV) discounts before investing. REITs serve as a bridge between equity and debt, offering steady rental yields and capital appreciation, but should fit within an investor’s overall risk‑adjusted asset allocation alongside equities, fixed income, gold, and physical real estate. Consulting a registered investment advisor is advised to determine appropriate exposure
