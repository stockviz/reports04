MG Hector Tomahawk EV Review: Spacious 7‑Seater with Premium Feel but Modest Power

HYUNDAI

The Overdrive review praises the MG Hector Tomahawk EV for its bold SUV styling, roomy three‑row cabin, and a high‑tech interior highlighted by a massive 15.6‑inch touchscreen. It packs a 69.2 kWh battery promising 517 km (WLTP) and delivers about 420‑450 km in everyday use, with 204 PS/310 Nm output that feels adequate for family driving but lacks excitement compared to rivals. Charging is slower (90 kW DC, 7.4 kW AC), though the vehicle offers a vehicle‑to‑home power function. Interior quality is generally upscale, though some hard plastics and limited physical controls make the large screen essential and occasionally cumbersome; front‑seat comfort is a weak point, while second‑row space is generous and the third row is usable but cramped. Boot capacity is 192 L with all seats up (610 L with five‑seater) and the price ranges from ₹19.5‑23.5 lakh (or ₹13.99 lakh with battery‑as‑a‑service), making it a compelling value despite its modest performance and refined‑driving shortcomings

KALYANKJIL HDFCBANK TITAN AXISBANK SBIFUNDS MAANALU VEDL

The panel highlighted robust institutional interest in the upcoming NSE IPO while retail participation remains weak. Overall, the discussion combined macro analysis with sector‑specific positioning ahead of the NSE listing
