Kerala High Court Authorizes Government Intervention on Overpriced Patented Cancer Drugs

NOVARTIND

The Kerala High Court ruled that the government can step in when patented medicines are priced prohibitively, directing the Centre to collect nationwide data on the affordability of life‑saving drugs like abemaciclib and ribociclib. The judgment clarifies that Section 100 of the Patents Act, which permits government use of patented drugs without the holder’s consent, can be invoked for public‑health reasons. While the court rejected arguments that generic palbociclib could substitute the patented drugs, it left the decision to invoke Section 100 to the government after it assesses affordability, though no specific timeline was set, raising concerns about delayed patient access

Stelco Idles Hamilton Cold‑Rolled Operations Due to US Tariffs

Cleveland‑Cliffs’ Canadian unit Stelco will indefinitely idle its cold‑rolled and coated lines at Hamilton Works, citing sustained market disruption from U.S. Section 232 steel tariffs that have curtailed south‑bound shipments. The shutdown, slated to begin Oct. 9, will shift production to its Lake Erie Works, change the product mix toward hot‑rolled steel, and cut about 350 jobs. Shares of parent Cliffs fell up to 9.5% on the news, marking the latest blow to Canada’s steel sector, which exports roughly half its output, mostly to the United States

Tata Electronics ramps up funding for iPhone and chip ventures, triples equity investment

SPELS BEL NSIL

Tata Electronics significantly boosted capital and debt support for its subsidiaries in FY26, tripling equity infusion to ₹3,727 cr to fund Apple iPhone production and semiconductor projects. Corporate guarantees rose to ₹61,280 cr, with fresh inter‑company loans of ₹1,017 cr. Investments in Tata Semiconductor Manufacturing and Assembly & Test jumped to ₹1,501 cr and ₹954 cr respectively. Gross debt surged 71% to ₹11,718 cr, offset by a ₹3,000 cr equity injection from Tata Sons. Operating income nearly doubled to ₹1,29,902 cr (consolidated) but net loss widened to ₹1,611 cr; standalone figures showed a tripled operating income of ₹4,015 cr and a narrowed loss of ₹773 cr. The company also launched an employee stock option plan and now ranks as the Tata Group’s fourth‑largest revenue generator
