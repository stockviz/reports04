U.S. Treasury Plans New Bank Sanctions to Isolate Iran

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced that the United States will sanction another bank this week as part of a broader campaign to cut off financial support for Iran. He highlighted a proposed rule to block the Emirati branches of Egypt’s Banque Misr from the U.S. financial system and said the U.S. will pressure G‑20 partners—including China and India—to cease dealings with Tehran. Bessent emphasized that all options, even sanctions on countries that continue Iran trade, remain on the table to enforce economic isolation and curb Iran’s nuclear ambitions

NDTV

Air New Zealand posted a NZ$336 million pretax loss for the year to June 30, driven by NZ$135 million in higher fuel costs and NZ$190 million in engine‑maintenance expenses. The airline cut costs, delayed new aircraft deliveries, and plans to adjust capacity and fares. HDFC Bank’s Post‑Jagdis​han Outlook: Facing Share‑Price Lag as Peers Outperform

HDFCBANK KOTAKBANK SBIFUNDS AXISBANK HDFC ICICIBANK

HDFC Bank’s MD‑CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan is stepping down after six years, leaving a mixed legacy: asset quality improved (GNPA down to 1.15%) and ROA held near 1.96%, but the bank’s NIM fell to 3.38% and its stock is down ~15% since the 2023 merger, underperforming peers. Over the same period, rivals SBI, ICICI, Axis and Kotak improved both GNPA and ROA, delivering strong shareholder returns (SBI +83%, ICICI +53%). HDFC’s future premium will hinge on a swift successor appointment and effective strategies to boost growth, NIM and market performance

Hollywood’s Box‑Office Surge in India 2026

HTMEDIA TIPSFILMS

Hollywood’s Indian box‑office collections hit ₹1,424 crore in the first seven months of 2026, nearing the 2019 record of ₹1,595 crore. A mix of biopics, epics and franchise films—including Obsession, Michael, Project Hail Mary, The Odyssey, Evil Dead Burn, The Devil Wears Prada 2, The Mummy and Spider‑Man: Brand New Day—driven this growth. Analysts predict international films will capture 20‑25% of India’s total box‑office revenue, up from 15% in 2019, with upcoming releases such as Avengers: Doomsday, Dune: Part Three, Godzilla Minus Zero and Jumanji: Open World expected to boost the share further. Social media and streaming platforms are also expanding the Indian audience for Hollywood movies
