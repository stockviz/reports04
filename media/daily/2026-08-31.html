Zydus Saroglitazar Achieves Primary Endpoint in Global Phase II(b) MASH Trial

ZYDUSLIFE

Zydus Lifesciences reported that its drug Saroglitazar Magnesium met the primary goal of the liver‑biopsy‑driven Phase II(b) EVIDENCES‑X trial, which enrolled 189 patients with Metabolic dysfunction Associated Steatohepatitis (MASH). After 52 weeks, the study achieved its primary endpoint of resolving steatohepatitis without worsening fibrosis, and showed favorable secondary outcomes including improvements in steatosis, inflammation and ballooning. The results reinforce the drug’s therapeutic potential for MASH and MASLD, conditions affecting up to 20 % of adults globally. To avoid financial strain, your home loan EMI should fit within the surplus of your take‑home salary after covering essential expenses, existing debt repayments, and savings goals. There’s no fixed RBI‑mandated percentage; instead, aim to keep at least a 10‑15% buffer of your net income. Use a home loan EMI calculator—input loan amount, interest rate, and tenure—to estimate monthly payments, total interest, and overall repayment. Test various loan amounts and tenures (e.g., a Rs 15 lakh loan at 8.5% for 20 years yields an EMI of ~Rs 13,017) and ensure the EMI aligns with your available surplus. If the EMI is too high, consider reducing the loan amount, extending the tenure, increasing your down payment, or clearing existing debts. Before applying, verify that the EMI leaves room for essential costs, emergencies, and savings, and assess the impact of potential rate changes on floating‑rate loans.
