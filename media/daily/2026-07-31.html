Apple’s June‑quarter revenue in India hit a record, driven by strong demand for Mac laptops and desktops. CEO Tim Cook highlighted record sales across multiple regions, but warned that worsening chip‑related supply constraints could curb shipments of iPhones, Macs and iPads in the upcoming September quarter

Mahindra’s EV Three‑Wheeler Arm Hits Unicorn Status

GENESYS M&M 3MINDIA

Mahindra Last Mile Mobility Ltd. raised about ₹322 crore in a Lightrock‑led round, valuing the company at ₹10,822 crore and cementing its unicorn status. MLMML now holds roughly 40 % of the L5 electric three‑wheeler market and posted an 85 % YoY volume increase in the June quarter

Google Pay integrates Gemini AI and expands credit offerings

AXISBANK SBIFUNDS

Google Pay is embedding Google’s Gemini generative‑AI model into its app to let users ask finance‑related questions in 13 languages via voice, text, images and charts, aiming to demystify financial topics while keeping data secure. The “Ask Google Pay” feature is positioned as an informational aid rather than advice. Simultaneously, Google Pay is broadening its credit ecosystem, adding SBI Card as a second issuer for its Flex credit card, planning more card variants, and scaling personal‑loan services with privacy‑preserving models, all while maintaining a strong UPI transaction volume share in India

IndiGo’s Roadmap: Discipline, Cost‑Leadership and Global Expansion

TENNIND TATACAP INDIGO

Rahul Bhatia credits relentless cost discipline and flawless execution for IndiGo’s 65 % domestic share, noting that a third of flights serve unique city pairs with no competition. He warns against straying from the low‑cost model even as the airline adds wide‑body planes, a three‑cabin product, and the BluChip loyalty program, which he expects to become a standalone profit centre within five years. Future growth hinges on hub‑and‑spoke international routes, strategic partnerships (including with Middle‑Eastern carriers, JAL, and a U.S. partner), and a shift from operating‑ to finance‑leases to own aircraft and improve cost structure. Bhatia envisions IndiGo leading Indian diaspora travel, expanding hospitality (targeting 300 Accor hotels by 2030), and cautiously exploring air‑taxi ventures. He stresses execution as the “Everest” challenge, acknowledges recent operational disruptions as a perfect storm, and affirms a long‑term goal of an Indian‑run CEO while valuing current leadership experience. The airline’s robust cash reserves and diversified business portfolio underpin its ambition to scale globally without compromising its low‑cost DNA

IndiGo’s Global Expansion Blueprint

INDIGO

IndiGo aims to transform its domestic dominance into a global network, using India as a hub for international flights. CEO Rahul Bhatia emphasizes densifying the domestic route map before launching long‑haul services to Europe and North America, and envisions third‑country traffic via India. The airline plans to leverage its massive Airbus order (500 aircraft) and expand its BluChip loyalty program and hospitality ventures. While holding about 66% of the Indian market, Bhatia notes that a third of this share comes from serving unique city pairs with no competition, effectively integrating underserved regions into the national network

Amazon Q2 Profit Surge Fueled by Rapid AWS Growth Amid Heavy AI Investment

Amazon reported a strong Q2 with net income of $62.65 billion and sales of $200.6 billion, driven by a 37% jump in AWS revenue—the fastest growth in 18 quarters. CEO Andy Jassy highlighted booming cloud, AI, and chip businesses, each surpassing $25 billion in run rates, and record Prime delivery speeds. The company announced a $200 billion AI, robotics, semiconductor, and satellite investment, up 60% from last year, sparking investor concerns about cash‑flow impact. Amazon’s outlook projects $197‑$202 billion in sales, slightly below analyst expectations. Competitive context includes strong cloud growth at Alphabet and Microsoft, while rising shipping costs and geopolitical factors pose challenges

AI Detects False “Made in USA” Labels but Retailers Hold Back

A study by Columbia Law School finds that Amazon’s Alexa for Shopping and Walmart’s Sparky can spot mismatches in “Made in USA” claims, yet the companies do not flag or remove the misleading listings. The AI chatbots cite business calculations and regulatory limits as reasons for inaction. Both retailers claim they are improving country‑of‑origin information, but critics argue stronger enforcement is needed to protect U.S. manufacturers and consumers

Exelon Cuts Data‑Center Pipeline Amid AI Pushback

PGEL DELTACORP

Exelon reduced its high‑probability data‑center pipeline to 11 GW (from 18 GW) and its overall 2027 pipeline to 25 GW (from 43 GW) as public opposition and investor skepticism over AI‑related power demand grow. The utility is tightening interconnection criteria to protect ratepayers, while rivals like PG&E see expanding pipelines elsewhere. Texas grid operator ERCOT is batching interconnection studies to accelerate advanced projects
