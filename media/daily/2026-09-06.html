Coal India’s Emergency Coal Allocation Amid Falling Power Plant Stocks

COALINDIA

Coal India Ltd (CIL) is permitting power plants with fuel supply agreements to obtain extra coal beyond their contracted limits as thermal plant inventories plunge to critically low levels—only 47% of the recommended stock. Heavy rainfall has hampered mining operations, wetting seams and flooding sections, but conditions are improving, leading to a rise in daily output from 1.36 MT to 1.7 MT and increased overburden removal. The ministry expects mining, loading, and transport disruptions to subside, with CIL boosting road and rail evacuations. Despite the recent setbacks, CIL’s overall coal supplies have risen 6.7% year‑on‑year, with August deliveries up 5.5% and power sector supplies up 4.5%. The move aims to prevent power shortages while the sector works toward normalizing production and logistics

RPG Life Sciences plans Rs 500 crore expansion in manufacturing and API assets

RPGLIFE

RPG Life Sciences will invest Rs 500 crore over the next year, adding to the Rs 200 crore already spent on acquisitions, to boost its manufacturing footprint and API capabilities. Backed by a Rs 700 crore capital infusion, the firm aims for Rs 2,000‑2,500 crore revenue by 2030, targeting 16‑17% growth in domestic formulations and expanding its API business. It focuses on super‑specialty segments and “textbook brands” like Naprocin, Aldactone and Norpace, while expanding internationally to over 60 countries and new markets in West Africa and Saudi Arabia. The company also sees a rising GLP‑1 anti‑obesity market, estimating a Rs 4,000 crore opportunity in India within 3‑4 years

Bold Frames Become the New Workplace Power Accessory

COCKERILL TIMESGTY GKB

Glasses with distinctive, colorful frames are replacing ties, watches, and jewelry as the primary means of self‑expression for professionals aged 35‑50+. Retailers report a shift from subtle, rimless styles to sculptural acetate and angular metal frames in shades like green, navy, oxblood, and burgundy. Consumers now buy multiple pairs for different moods, outfits, and occasions, treating eyewear as a fashion wardrobe rather than a functional necessity. Drivers include post‑pandemic office culture, heightened focus on the upper face, social media influence, and higher discretionary spending, leading older shoppers to adopt bold styles once deemed youthful. The trend signals eyewear’s rise as a key, versatile accessory in the modern corporate dress code

NCLT Halts Further Sale of Byju’s K3 Assets Amid Auction Dispute

GOLDTECH GLOBAL ARIS ZEELEARN

The National Company Law Tribunal ordered a status‑quo on the disposal of assets from Byju’s subsidiary K3 Education after allegations that items valued at roughly ₹150 crore were auctioned for just ₹16 crore. The resolution professional of Think and Learn Private Ltd (TLPL) and the buyer, Comprint Tech Solutions, must maintain the hold until the next hearing on September 21. The tribunal noted concerns over lack of documentation, questionable ownership, and the unknown whereabouts of the assets, which were reportedly moved to Mumbai. It also directed Comprint to submit a sworn inventory of the purchased assets and their locations, and ordered the RP to provide transaction details within a week. The suspended Byju’s directors supported the plea, arguing that the corporate insolvency resolution process should not involve low‑value asset auctions

Rural Two‑Wheeler Mix Crucial for Festive Season Success

HEROMOTOCO

Rural India drives over half of two‑wheeler registrations, but product preferences vary widely across states—entry‑level bikes dominate in Rajasthan, while Maharashtra sees a broader range including scooters and EVs. Manufacturers must forecast not just overall rural demand but the exact model mix for each region, balancing risks from food inflation, erratic rainfall, and uneven EV adoption (5.9% rural share, concentrated in five states). Dealers rely on three‑month sales trends and regional insights to achieve up to 80‑85% inventory accuracy, aiming to meet festive‑season peaks despite external challenges

Punjab Power Crisis Worsens as Coal Shortage Cuts 1,500 MW Generation

ADANIPOWER

Punjab Power Minister Tarunpreet Singh Sond says a severe national coal shortage has reduced the state’s power generation by about 1,500 MW, forcing major thermal plants like Rajpura and Talwandi Sabo to operate at half capacity. The shortage affects 63 GW of supply across India, with many plants holding only 3‑4 days of coal stock. State‑supplied plants have roughly 10 days of coal. Sond urges the central government to act immediately and clarify the timeline for resolving the shortage

Coal India Offers Road Transport to Boost Power Plant Coal Supplies

COALINDIA

Coal India will let thermal plants with existing fuel contracts lift extra coal beyond their annual limits via road transport, aiming to quickly raise supplies amid falling stocks and high summer demand. The ministry noted the miner has 76 million tonnes at pitheads and 46 million tonnes of ready‑to‑mine exposure, with production up 6.7% YoY (322.9 Mt April‑Aug) as monsoon rains ease. The additional road route complements the existing rail supply to help avert shortages
