Kolkata‑Sairang Express Derailment Linked to Faulty Main Braking System

INDIANB

A six‑member probe into the November 11 derailment of the Kolkata‑Sairang Express at Digaru station, Assam, found that the train’s primary brake (the BP angle cock) was left isolated in the locomotive, preventing brake application. The crew reported brakes working at Guwahati and addressed an overheated wheel at Panbari, but likely left the angle cock closed when fixing jammed brake pads, causing the brakes to fail as the train tried to stop at Digaru, leading to an overshoot, collision with a dead end, and derailment. The report does not assign blame, but experts suggest staff may have forgotten to reopen the angle cock, and a thorough investigation is needed
