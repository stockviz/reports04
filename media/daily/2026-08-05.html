United Breweries Q1 FY27 profit falls 9.6% despite revenue rise

UBL

United Breweries Ltd posted a 9.64% drop in consolidated net profit to ₹166.28 cr in Q1 FY27, while revenue grew 10% to ₹5,919.44 cr. Beer sales drove double‑digit growth, with sell‑in volumes up 9% and sell‑out up 13%, and premium volumes rising 17% (Heineken Silver +28%, Kingfisher Ultra +11%). Inventory was cut 20% to improve cash flow. Total expenses rose 11.7% to ₹5,745.66 cr, and a -300 bps hit to gross margin from the Middle East war affected margins. The company expects ongoing inflationary cost pressures and will focus on disciplined pricing, cost management, productivity, and long‑term investments

Codelco Suspends Andes Norte Project Amid New Seismic Threat at El Teniente

Codelco has halted work on the Andes Norte expansion at its El Teniente copper mine after six months of data revealed a distinct seismic risk, separate from known underground hazards. The suspension, described as temporary and precautionary, will not affect current production but underscores challenges in reviving output at aging mines. The company will expand monitoring, conduct further technical analysis, and implement additional controls before resuming construction. The delay could raise investor concerns about Codelco’s multi‑billion‑dollar overhaul, especially as global copper demand rises. The focus now shifts to enhancing safety and operational efficiency before pursuing further expansion

OpenAI Pays $3.2 Million to Settle U.S. Hiring Discrimination Probe

OpenAI and its subsidiary Statsig will pay $3.2 million to resolve Justice Department allegations that they favored foreign workers on temporary visas and discriminated against U.S. applicants by requiring paper applications, posting jobs only on late‑night radio and not listing them online. The settlement includes $1.2 million in penalties, $2 million to compensate alleged victims, revised hiring policies, mandatory training, and DOJ monitoring, citing violations of the Immigration and Nationality Act for fewer than 10 positions involved
