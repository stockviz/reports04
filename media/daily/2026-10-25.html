Rolls‑Royce Eyes Growth in India Amid FTA‑Driven Duty Cuts

HEROMOTOCO TV18BRDCST

Rolls‑Royce’s India sales chief Julian Jenkins says the new India‑UK free‑trade agreement slashing import duty on UK‑built cars over 3 L from 110% to 30% could enable price adjustments, though bespoke pricing will remain. The brand currently operates two showrooms in Delhi and Chennai, with workshops in Mumbai and Hyderabad, and plans further dealer expansion as demand rises. India is a top‑three APAC market, driven by a younger, affluent clientele—now averaging 40 years old—seeking personalized luxury, including increasing interest from women. While the Cullinan remains the most popular model, the Phantom leads in bespoke commissions, accounting for about 20% of sales. Rolls‑Royce will continue offering both V12‑powered cars and the electric Spectre, letting Indian customers choose their preferred powertrain

OMCs Agree to Recommend Interim LPG Distributors’ Commission Hike, Await Ministry Approval

BPCL HINDPETRO IOC

Oil marketing companies (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL) have pledged to jointly recommend a temporary increase in LPG distributors’ commission, though the final approval rests with the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. In a bipartite meeting on Oct 21, distributors highlighted financial strain, rising costs, falling refill sales, new‑connection limits, and unsold stock. OMC directors assured action on forced uplifts of non‑fuel items, changes to indents, SOPs, digital audit trails, and grievance mechanisms. Minutes, an action matrix, and possible ministry intervention were requested, while the OMCs acknowledged the distributors’ dedication and the need for time to normalise operations

Great Eggspectations: Paying for Premium Eggs – What’s Really Inside

FINEORG HAPPYFORGE TRAVELFOOD NATCAPSUQ

India’s egg market is booming, with premium varieties like free‑range, omega‑3, vitamin‑D and high‑protein eggs capturing a small but fast‑growing niche (15‑20% of urban sales). Consumers are drawn by protein awareness, animal‑welfare concerns and the convenience of quick‑commerce platforms, but the higher price (₹25‑30 vs ₹6‑8 for regular eggs) reflects added costs of feed, land, labour, packaging and branding, not necessarily superior nutrition. Nutritionally, brown shells or orange yolks don’t guarantee more nutrients; only larger eggs contain slightly more protein, and enrichment (omega‑3, vitamins) depends on specialized feed. Verification of claims remains weak in India, unlike EU egg‑stamping. While premium eggs appeal to affluent shoppers seeking transparency and welfare standards, most of the country still needs affordable, regular eggs to meet protein goals. Ultimately, pay extra only if you can verify the claimed benefits; otherwise, a trusted source of standard eggs offers comparable nutrition at a fraction of the cost

Chinese Smartphone Brands Lose Ground in India as Memory Chip Costs Surge

VIVOBIOT

Chinese OEMs like Vivo, Oppo and Xiaomi saw their Indian market share fall to around 67‑68% in Q3 2023‑24, the lowest in over eight years, due to record‑high memory chip prices forcing cuts to budget models. This opened space for Indian players such as Lava, Ai+, Mivi and Boltt, whose combined share rose to 7% (up from 2% a year earlier). Overall smartphone shipments dropped 20‑26% YoY to a decade low of 35.8 million units. Experts say Chinese firms are trimming specs and focusing on mid‑ and premium tiers, while Indian brands must invest in R&D, quality, and distribution to sustain growth beyond the current price‑sensitive opportunity
