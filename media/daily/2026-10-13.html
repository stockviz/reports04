IKEA plans to increase its Indian revenue fourfold by 2030, expanding from eight to 30 stores and boosting investment to ₹21,000 crore. Country CEO Patrik Antoni notes that Indian men are heavily involved in home‑furnishing decisions, unlike many other markets where women lead. Indian families make collective purchasing choices, prompting IKEA to tailor products and displays to local living arrangements. FSSAI Plans New Rules to Boost Transparency for E‑Commerce Food Sales

DABUR NESTLEIND ABBOTINDIA ITC SWIGGY

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India is set to amend regulations for ecommerce, quick‑commerce platforms and cloud kitchens, mandating display of manufacturing dates, expiry dates and batch numbers for online grocery items. After stakeholder consultations and inspections of dark stores, the regulator aims to address consumer complaints about missing shelf‑life information. Despite industry resistance, FSSAI has issued notices to over 150 food manufacturers for labeling violations and is pushing ahead with front‑of‑pack warning labels for sugar, salt and saturated fat, pending a Supreme Court decision. The agency emphasizes stronger enforcement and expects large companies to lead by example
