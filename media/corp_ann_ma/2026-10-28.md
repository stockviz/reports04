# Corporate Action Report

As of: 2026-10-28

## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### EMKAY

Investment in Emkay Capital Private Limited (ECPL), a wholly owned subsidiary, by subscription to rights issue of equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each at par, in one or more tranches, not exceeding Rs. 30 Crores; proceeds to be used for purchase of equity shares of Emkay Investment Managers Limited and Emkay Wealth Advisory Limited, investment in EWAL, and other business purposes; shareholding remains unchanged at 100%.

### FORTIS

International Hospital Limited (wholly-owned subsidiary of Fortis Healthcare Limited) acquired the entire business and operations of Suguna Ramaiah Hospitals Private Limited and its land and building in Bengaluru for approx. INR 277 Crores through a business transfer agreement and sale deed; transaction consummated on October 28, 2026.

### HEROMOTOCO

Hero MotoCorp Limited: Additional investment of up to INR 300 Crore in HMCL Netherlands B.V., a wholly-owned subsidiary, for further investment in its subsidiaries HMCL Colombia S.A.S and Hero MotoCorp Do Brasil Ltda; cash consideration; no change in shareholding; HNBV to remain a wholly-owned subsidiary.

### JSL

Jindal Stainless Limited acquired an additional ~7% equity stake in Ampin C&I Three Private Limited, an SPV formed with Ampin C&I Private Limited to develop a 40 MW intra-state solar group captive renewable energy project, for cash consideration of ~₹5.08 crores, taking aggregate shareholding to 24.21%.

### NIPPOBATRY

Indo National Limited acquired an additional 0.39% stake in Medcuore Medical Solutions Private Ltd (MMSPL) for Rs. 25,01,968, taking total shareholding to 63.46%; 178 shares acquired at Rs. 14,056 per share; cash consideration; MMSPL is engaged in manufacturing, distribution and services related to Air Monitoring System and Air Purifiers.

### OSWALGREEN

OSWAL GREENTECH REAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT L.L.C (Dubai, UAE): Incorporation of foreign subsidiary; 95% shareholding (38,000 equity shares of AED 1,000 each) acquired for AED 38,000,000; balance 5% held by promoter group member Mr. Shael Oswal; real estate development in UAE and GCC

### PHOENIXLTD

Island Star Mall Developers Private Limited (material subsidiary): selective reduction of equity share capital by cancelling and extinguishing 3,25,69,360 fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each held by Canada Pension Plan Investment Board; aggregate cash consideration of Rs. 1,350 crore paid by ISMDPL; Company’s shareholding increased from 58.33% to 68.10%; NCLT Mumbai Bench order dated October 27, 2026 approved the capital reduction.

### SKYWAYS

Brace Port Logistics Limited (material subsidiary of Skyways Air Services Limited) to acquire 76% equity share capital of Sageroute Logistics Private Limited; cash consideration not exceeding ₹76,000 plus subsequent capital infusion of ₹75,24,000; Sageroute will become a step-down subsidiary of Skyways Air Services Limited

### TITAN

Titan Company Canada Inc. – 100% wholly owned subsidiary incorporated in Canada; cash subscription not exceeding CAD 5,000; objective: jewellery retailing and other lifestyle businesses

### TVSMOTOR

TVS Motor Company Limited acquired 100% stake (18,89,47,551 equity shares) of STPL Trading and Services Private Limited for Rs. 530.08 Cr cash consideration; STPL will become a wholly owned subsidiary.
TVS Motor Company Limited acquired 100% equity share capital (2,500 equity shares of Rs. 10 each) of Harita ARC Limited for Rs. 25,000/- cash consideration; Harita ARC Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary.
