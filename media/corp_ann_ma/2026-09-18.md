# Corporate Action Report

As of: 2026-09-18

## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### APOLLOPIPE

Apollo Ceramics Limited (Subsidiary of Apollo Pipes Limited) acquired 76% stake and profit sharing rights in Mazzini Tiles LLP for ₹40.42 Crore cash consideration. Mazzini is an established tiles and ceramics manufacturer based in Morbi, Gujarat, with FY2025-26 turnover of ₹87.15 Crore and installed capacity of 72 lakh sq. m. p.a. Acquisition completed; Singhi Advisors acted as exclusive M&A advisors.

### INDOCO

## Merger of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### ACMESOLAR

Scheme of Amalgamation: ACME Pokhran Solar Private Limited (direct wholly owned subsidiary), ACME Sikar Solar Private Limited (direct wholly owned subsidiary), and ACME Eco Clean Energy Private Limited (indirect wholly owned subsidiary via ACME Pokhran Solar) amalgamated with and into ACME Solar Holdings Limited. No cash or share consideration; equity shares held by the Transferee Company in the Transferor Companies to be cancelled and extinguished upon effectiveness. Rationale: simplify group structure, reduce legal entities, achieve cost savings, and consolidate renewable energy assets.

### AVANTEL

Merger of Imeds Global Private Limited (wholly owned subsidiary; Net Worth ₹160.32 Lakhs, Revenue ₹2,182.83 Lakhs for FY ended March 31, 2026) into Avantel Limited (Transferee; Net Worth ₹35,650.45 Lakhs, Revenue ₹22,135.23 Lakhs for FY ended March 31, 2026); no cash consideration or share exchange ratio applicable as Transferor is a wholly owned subsidiary; rationale: rationalise group structure, simplify governance and reporting, consolidate operations and eliminate duplication of administrative/compliance functions.
