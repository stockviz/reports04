## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### EMBDL

Embassy Columbia Pacific ASL Private Limited: acquisition of remaining 50% stake (1,12,44,599 equity shares of face value INR 10 each) from CP Senior Housing (India) LLC for INR 32,65,27,241 in cash; Embassy Columbia will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Embassy Developments Limited

### NIITMTS

NIIT (USA), Inc. (wholly owned overseas subsidiary of NIIT Learning Systems Limited) acquired 100% membership interest in Bluewater Learning, LLC, a Texas-based company; cash consideration; aggregate purchase price up to USD 17.6 million including earnout payable over five years based on performance; transaction completed

### PANACHE

Panache Digilife Limited acquired 22,50,000 equity shares of Technofy Digital Private Limited for cash consideration of Rs. 2.25 crore; Technofy Digital Private Limited remains a wholly owned subsidiary.

### RAMRAT

Ram Ratna Wires Limited acquires 2,21,26,000 equity shares (50%) of Epavo Electricals Private Limited from Epack Durable Limited for Rs. 6.78 per share, aggregating Rs. 15,00,14,280; Epavo becomes a wholly owned subsidiary.

### RBA

Restaurant Brands Asia Limited acquired not exceeding 2,50,000 Optionally Convertible Preference Shares (OCPS) of PT Sari Burger Indonesia (PT SBI), a subsidiary, by subscription for up to IDR 250 billion equivalent in INR; cash consideration; completion up to June 30, 2027; no change in equity shareholding unless OCPS convert to equity.
Restaurant Brands Asia Limited converted an outstanding loan of IDR 371.50 billion advanced to PT Sari Burger Indonesia (PT SBI), a subsidiary, into 3,71,500 OCPS of nominal value IDR 1 million each, and converted 1,75,000 Redeemable Cumulative Non-Convertible Preference Shares aggregating IDR 175 billion along with dividends into OCPS; related party transaction; completion up to March 31, 2027; no change in equity shareholding unless OCPS convert to equity.

### SKYWAYS

Skyways Air Services Limited made a further investment in its foreign subsidiary Skyways SLS Cargo Services LLC (Dubai, UAE) by subscribing to additional equity share capital for an amount not exceeding AED 1,203,840 (USD 327,888 / INR 3,13,29,824.80), divided into 198 shares of face value AED 1,000 per share including premium of AED 5,080 per share, for working capital requirements; shareholding/control: 90%.

### WELCORP

Welspun Special Coating Private Limited (WSCPL), a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of Welspun Corp Limited; acquisition of 49% equity stake (4,900 equity shares of Rs. 10 each) from Welspun DI Pipes Limited for cash consideration of Rs. 49,000; upon completion WSCPL will become a direct associate company of Welspun Corp Limited with 49% holding, while Perma-Pipe Qatar acquires the remaining 51% for Rs. 51,000
