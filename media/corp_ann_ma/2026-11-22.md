# Corporate Action Report

As of: 2026-11-22

## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### APLAPOLLO

APL Apollo Tubes Limited acquired 100% of Tata Steel Colors Private Limited (86,60,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10 each) from Tata Steel Limited and Tata Steel Downstream Products Limited for INR 16,300 crores in cash; Colours will become a wholly owned subsidiary.

## Acquisition of Another Company/Shares

### APLAPOLLO

APL Apollo Tubes Limited acquired the tubes business undertaking of Tata Steel Limited as a going concern by way of slump sale for INR 11,700 crores in cash.

### TATASTEEL

Tata Steel Limited acquires up to 7,38,60,062 equity shares (approx. 26.38%) of APL Apollo Tubes Limited from S Gupta Holding Private Limited in three tranches at INR 2,100 per share, aggregating up to approx. INR 15,510.61 crore; plus mandatory open offer for up to 7,28,06,387 shares (26%) at INR 2,301.40 per share, aggregating up to approx. INR 16,755.66 crore, payable in cash.
