# Corporate Action Report

As of: 2026-11-04

## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### 5PAISA

5paisa Capital Limited acquired 66,148 equity shares of Giskard Datatech Private Limited on November 3, 2026, through a share swap arrangement involving issuance of 20,50,588 equity shares of the Company at ₹380 per share aggregating ₹77,92,23,440; shareholding increased from 19.76% to 57.41% on a fully diluted basis, making GDPL a subsidiary effective November 3, 2026.

### AUROPHARMA

Auro Pharma LLC, Russia: transfer of entire 100% stake from Auroactive Pharma Private Limited to Auropharm Overseas Limited, both wholly owned subsidiaries of Aurobindo Pharma; cash consideration of ~INR 29 crore subject to adjustments; internal reorganization to streamline Russian subsidiary holdings; completion in 60-90 days subject to customary formalities.

### JKPAPER

JK Paper Limited acquired 3,27,604 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each of Quadragen Vethealth Private Limited (QVPL), representing 5.46% of its paid-up share capital; further acquisition of 92,396 equity shares (1.54%) in process; shareholding will increase to 72.00% upon completion

### JSLL

Jeena Sikho Lifecare Limited proposes to incorporate Ayugenomics Private Limited as a subsidiary, subscribing to 60% equity share capital (30,000 equity shares at ₹10 each) for a cash consideration of ₹3,00,000; the subsidiary will engage in genomics and Ayurveda-based research, diagnostics, clinical services, and herbal/nutraceutical products.

### MAXESTATES

Incorporation of a wholly-owned subsidiary (name being finalised); proposed authorised share capital ₹10,00,000 divided into 1,00,000 equity shares of ₹10 each; proposed paid-up capital ₹1,00,000 divided into 10,000 equity shares of ₹10 each; 100% cash subscription; real estate industry; to carry out real estate activities

### PWL

Physicswallah Limited acquired 25,599 equity shares (12.25% fully diluted equity share capital) of Utkarsh Classes & Edutech Private Limited, a subsidiary, for INR 2,55,990, increasing shareholding from 75.50% to 87.75% under Tranche IV of the Share Acquisition Terms Agreement.

### SOMANYCERA

Somany Ceramics Limited acquired up to 25% of paid-up equity share capital (up to 24,75,000 equity shares) from existing shareholders of M/s Somany Sanitary Ware Private Limited (SSWPL), a subsidiary, increasing shareholding from 51% to up to 76%; cash consideration; cost to be informed at the time of acquisition; completion within 120 days from the board meeting date.

### SUNDARMFIN

Sundaram Alternate Assets Limited (SAAL): Acquisition of 100% stake (3,90,53,158 equity shares of ₹10 each) from Sundaram Asset Management Company Limited (SAMCL), making SAAL a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of Sundaram Finance Limited; cash consideration not exceeding ₹75 crore; subject to regulatory approvals.

## Acquisition of Another Company/Shares

## Merger of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### AUROPHARMA

Auroactive Pharma Private Limited with Apitoria Pharma Private Limited: both wholly owned subsidiaries of Aurobindo Pharma; Scheme of Amalgamation filed with NCLT, Hyderabad; share exchange ratio of 97 equity shares of Apitoria Pharma (INR 10 each) for every 10,000 equity shares of Auroactive Pharma (INR 10 each), resulting in issuance of 23,95,900 equity shares; rationale: consolidate API businesses under a single entity, improve operational efficiency, and simplify group structure.

### KIMS

KIMS Hospitals Private Limited (wholly-owned subsidiary) merged with Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences Limited (holding company); no cash consideration or issue of new shares; shareholding pattern unchanged

### NH

NH Integrated Care Private Limited (wholly owned subsidiary) demerger of Clinical Services undertaking into Narayana Hrudayalaya Limited; no shares issued as consideration; NCLT Bengaluru Bench sanctioned Scheme of Arrangement under Sections 230-232, Appointed Date April 1, 2025
