## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### BIRLANU

BirlaNu Limited incorporated a wholly-owned subsidiary (name pending MCA approval) for Rs. 15,00,000 (1,50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10 each at par), operating a digital platform for product discovery, sampling and professional engagement in the architecture, interior design and building materials sector.

### BONDADA

BTM Mobility Private Limited (formerly Thuraga Motors Private Limited) acquired by Atpole Technologies Private Limited, a subsidiary of Bondada Engineering Limited; 5,100 equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each acquired for Rs. 15 crore, representing 51% shareholding; target engaged in manufacturing and assembly of electric two-wheelers and associated components

### KAYNES

Kaynes Technology India Limited incorporated KAYNES ARABIA ELECTRONICS COMPANY (KAEC) as a Wholly Owned Subsidiary; 100% shareholding acquired; cash consideration; authorized share capital SAR 100,000/- to be invested through multiple tranches; manufacturing and assembly of electronic products and sub-assemblies for Saudi Arabia and GCC markets.

### SWELECTES

SWELECT Radiant Power Private Limited (wholly owned subsidiary): additional investment up to Rs. 4,36,15,250 for up to 1,75,000 equity shares at Rs. 249.23 per share; cash consideration; shareholding to reduce from 100% to 74% after allotment to other group captive investors; for setting up a 4.5 MWp DC solar power plant under Group Captive Scheme in Kerala

### TVSHLTD

TVS Holdings Limited acquired an additional 12,55,26,842 equity shares of Rs. 10 each in Home Credit India Finance Private Limited (HCIFPL), a subsidiary, via rights issue for cash consideration of Rs. 38.55 per share aggregating Rs. 483.91 Cr; shareholding remains at 74.45%.

### VIYASH

Alivira Vietnam Company Limited incorporated as step-down wholly owned subsidiary of Viyash Scientific Limited via Alivira Animal Health Limited; cash consideration; investment up to USD 400,000 by AAHL; 100% shareholding; pharmaceuticals; importation, registration, marketing, sale and distribution of animal health products, APIs, and phytogenic solutions in Vietnam
