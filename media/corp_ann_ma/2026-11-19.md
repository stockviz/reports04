# Corporate Action Report

As of: 2026-11-19

## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### ADVENZYMES

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited acquired an additional 1.22% stake in its subsidiary JC Biotech Private Limited by purchasing 2,53,297 equity shares from selling shareholders at ₹90 per equity share, for a total consideration of ₹22.80 million; shareholding increased from 95.72% to 96.94% effective November 18, 2026. The company is also in the process of acquiring the remaining 3.06% stake, after which JC Biotech will become a wholly owned subsidiary.

### EMBDL

Embassy Columbia Pacific ASL Private Limited — Acquisition of 1,12,44,599 equity shares (face value INR 10 each) representing the entire 50% equity stake held by CP Senior Housing (India) LLC, USA, for consideration of INR 32,65,27,241; completed on November 19, 2026; Embassy Columbia has become a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.

### MAXESTATES

Acreage Builders Private Limited (ABPL) — Max Estates Limited subscribed to 14,55,166 10% Non-Convertible, Non-Cumulative, Non-Participating Redeemable Preference Shares (NCRPS) of face value INR 10 each at par, for an aggregate cash consideration of INR 1,45,51,660, representing 100% of the NCRPS offered under the rights issue; ABPL is a subsidiary of the Company and continues as such, with the Company's equity shareholding remaining approximately 51%. The subscription was made pursuant to an offer letter from ABPL and renunciation of rights entitlements by other existing shareholders in favour of the Company. ABPL is developing the commercial real estate project 'Max District' at Sector 65, Gurugram, Haryana, and had nil turnover in FY24–FY26. The subscription is intended to augment ABPL's paid-up share capital to meet Directorate of Town and Country Planning (DTCP) requirements and support business operations; expected completion on or before November 30, 2026.

### PAYTM

One 97 Communications Limited completed an additional investment of INR 500 Crores in its wholly owned subsidiary, Paytm Cloud Technologies Limited; subsequently, Paytm Cloud Technologies Limited completed an additional investment of INR 500 Crores in its wholly owned subsidiary, Paytm Singapore Pte. Ltd., a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.
