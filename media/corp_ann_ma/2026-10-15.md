# Corporate Action Report

## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### AERONEU

Aeroflex Neu Limited acquires 7,27,040 equity shares of Stilonn Valves and Controls Private Limited for ₹16,00,21,504, increasing shareholding from 19.58% to 51.01%, making Stilonn a subsidiary.

### IIFL

IIFL Finance Limited proposes to acquire India Infoline Foundation, a Section 8 company limited by guarantee without share capital and the CSR arm of the IIFL Group, from IIFL Capital Services Limited; no cash consideration or share swap involved, IIFL Finance will contribute the prescribed guarantee amount as a member; subject to requisite statutory and regulatory approvals; part of internal group restructuring/re-alignment.

### MANKIND

Mankind Pharma (Netherlands) B.V. to acquire 70% stake in Armed Cell Therapeutics B.V. (ACTx), Belgium-incorporated biotechnology company developing next-generation CAR-T therapies; EUR 48 million cash consideration in instalments; definitive agreement; Mankind Pharma additional investment up to EUR 45 million in wholly owned subsidiary Mankind Netherlands, aggregate limit EUR 50 million

### SOLARINDS

Solar Explochem Limited (wholly owned subsidiary of Solar Industries India Limited) to acquire 100% of Sivasakthi Industrial Explosives Private Limited for ₹115.02 crores cash consideration; completion within 12 months; business expansion into explosives manufacturing.
