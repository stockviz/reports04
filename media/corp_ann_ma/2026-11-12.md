### PHOENIXLTD

Island Star Mall Developers Private Limited (ISMDPL) — The Phoenix Mills Limited's shareholding in ISMDPL increased from 58.33% to 68.10% following completion of a selective reduction of ISMDPL's equity share capital, pursuant to which 3,25,69,360 fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each held by Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPP Investments) were cancelled and extinguished, with ISMDPL paying aggregate consideration of Rs. 1350,00,00,000 (Rs. 1,350 crore) to CPP Investments; CPP Investments' stake decreased from 41.67% to 31.90%.

### SKYWAYS

Skyways Air Services Ltd – Forin Container Line Private Limited: Allotted 16,96,791 equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each at an issue price of Rs. 250 per share (including premium of Rs. 240 per share), aggregating to Rs. 42,41,97,750, in its Material Subsidiary. Post-allotment shareholding is 85.84%. The investment is intended to provide financial support to the subsidiary to repay outstanding loans/borrowings and reduce debt obligations and interest burden.
