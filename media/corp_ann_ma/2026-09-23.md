# Corporate Action Report

As of: 2026-09-23

## Acquisition of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### POWERGRID

POWERGRID acquired 100% stake in Barmer HVDC Power Transmission Limited for an aggregate value of about Rs. 18.98 Crore (including 50,000 equity shares at par Rs. 10/- each along with assets and liabilities) under Tariff Based Competitive Bidding; target incorporated 30.09.2025 by RECPDCL; engaged in power transmission; not yet commercially operational.

## Acquisition of Another Company/Shares

### LENSKART

NESO Brands Pte. Ltd. (Lenskart's wholly owned subsidiary) acquired an additional 2.52% stake in Le Petit Lunetier Paris SAS (LPL), France, for EUR 0.248 million (~INR 2.71 Crore) via primary subscription on Sep 23, 2026, raising NESO's holding from 31.82% to 34.34% (aggregate 5.30% increase). Cash consideration; related-party transaction at arm's length.

## Merger of (Step-Down) Subsidiaries

### PITTIENG

Pitti Engineering Limited (PEL) amalgamated with Pitti Industries Private Limited (PIPL, formerly Bagadia Chaitra Industries Private Limited) and Dakshin Foundry Private Limited (DFPL); Scheme sanctioned by NCLT Hyderabad and effective 23-Sep-2026 (appointed date 1-Apr-2026); authorized share capital amended to ₹196.89 Cr / 39.38 Cr equity shares of ₹5 each.

### SAKSOFT

Augmento Labs Private Limited (wholly owned subsidiary) merged with Saksoft Limited (holding company); NCLT Chennai sanctioned the Scheme of Amalgamation per C.P.(CAA)/93(CHE)2025 dated 16.09.2026; Appointed Date 01.04.2026; scheme provides for cancellation of transferor shares, pooling-of-interest accounting under Ind AS 103 Appendix C, and dissolution of Augmento Labs without winding-up.

## Merger of Another Company

### WAAREEENER

Scheme of Amalgamation: Indosolar Limited (Transferor Company) merged with Waaree Energies Limited (Transferee Company) under Sections 230–232 of the Companies Act, 2013; consideration via share exchange ratio of 1:11 (Transferee:Transferor equity shares); shareholders of Transferor to receive shares of Transferee; related-party transaction on arm's length basis; Transferor to be dissolved without winding up.

### WAAREEINDO

Indosolar Limited (Transferor) merged with Waaree Energies Limited (Transferee); shareholders of Indosolar receive 1 equity share of Waaree for every 11 shares held; consideration via share exchange ratio determined by registered valuers (SSPA & CO. and GT Valuation Advisors), fairness opinion by ITI Capital Limited.
